BMW Sets 2 New Nürburgring Records With Help from Pirelli

📅 Nov 09, 2026  |  Category: Automotive  |  Read Source →

A revised semi-slick tire helped the carmaker knock down the compact car and touring car lap records at the Nordschleife.

Pirelli’s P Zero Trofeo RS is one of its most extreme performance tires, one capable of helping performance cars extract the most out of their potential. And that’s especially true when the company works with automakers directly—as the Italian rubbermaker did with BMW, developed a bespoke version specifically for the BMW M range. The new semi-slick tire remains street legal, but doesn’t sacrifice at-the-limit grip. And it helped BMW set two new ‘Ring lap records with its BMW M2 with the M Performance Track Kit and BMW M3 CS Touring.

The BMW M2 with the M Performance Track Kit managed a lap time of 7:25.068minutes. This lowered the Nürburgring Nordschleife compact car record, previously held by the M2 CS, by 0.5 seconds. The long-roof BMW M3, however, made more significant history: BMW’s M3 CS Touring lapped the 12.9-mile racetrack in 7:29.49 minutes, setting a new record for the fastest touring car ever by shaving off more than five seconds. The previous touring car lap record of 7:35.06 was held by the standard BMW M3 Touring. A combination of increased horsepower, stronger brakes, and custom rubber—as well as BMW M Development engineer Jörg Weidinger behind the wheel—pushed the wagon to success.

“The bespoke development program is the result of close collaboration between Pirelli and BMW M’s R&D teams. Thanks to advanced virtual development techniques, the two teams were able to adapt the tire’s characteristics to the different requirements from the earliest stages of the project, in line with Pirelli’s Perfect Fit approach, which aims to create products tailored to each specific model or application,” the tire maker said in a statement.

Pirelli initially tested the tires alongside BMW at the Nürburgring Nordschleife, and the tires were also later tested at the Nardò test circuit in Italy. BMW and Pirelli have collaborated on over 30 bespoke tire projects across the P Zero family over the years, and like many others, a keen eye can tell which ones are made for Bimmers; the manufacturer-specific tires feature a BMW star marking, as a nod to the integrated development process.

Golf In Comfort Year-Round In This HVAC-Equipped Golf Cart From Tractor Supply

📅 Nov 07, 2026  |  Category: Automotive  |  Read Source →

If you're an avid golfer but find the time spent between holes a bit uncomfortable, Tractor Supply has something that will make your life a lot nicer. Available right now for $15,849 is the Massimo MVR Cargo Max HVAC Lithium, an enclosed electric vehicle that, as its name suggests, includes integrated heating and air conditioning. Tractor Supply has it listed under its "Golf Carts" section of vehicles, but per its product description, its intended use is a lot wider.

The MVR is said to be "built for farm chores, property maintenance and recreation," and meant to let its owner "tackle property projects in comfort." And in that context, it really does remind me of a kei truck, especially with its power-dump cargo bed that uses a hydraulic mechanism for easier unloading.

With marine-grade vinyl seating for two, it support total loads of up to 1,100 pounds, and has an estimated driving range (that's how far you can drive with it, not a golf thing) of up to 45 miles, thanks to a 51.2-volt 105Ah battery. A 5.0 kW (6.7 horsepower) AC motor lets it hit a top speed of 15 mph. Golf carts can be powered by electric or gas, and each have their own pros and cons, but having this one at your local golf course would certainly add to the serenity both inside and outside of the cart.

To help it navigate less-than-ideal terrain, there's 12 inches of ground clearance and 23-inch all-terrain tires fitted all around. Tractor Supply currently lists the MVR Cargo Max HVAC in just two colors: Pearl White and Ultra Red, but Massimo's site also lists it in Midnight Silver, Deep Blue, and Glacier Blue.

Sixteen grand for a "golf cart" may sound like a lot, but, again, I feel like the Massimo MVR Cargo Max is only a golf cart on a technicality. You can definitely use it as fairway transport, sure, but it's really a utility cart meant to transport cargo and up to two people around all sorts of private property. And with HVAC, it'll keep you comfortable doing so all year round. You may even be able to take it around your gated community, if that's the sort of thing they all. Whether or not you can legally drive it on public roads, however, is highly dependent on where you're at.
