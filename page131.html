Prime Video Has The #1 And #2 Fantasy Shows On TV Right Now

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

It's a good time to be a fantasy fan with a streaming subscription. On Disney+, Percy Jackson and the Olympians is about to launch into its third season after banishing the memory of its big screen predecessor. Over at Netflix, the red brand is still working its way through The Witcher. Prime Video boasts the big-budget adaptation of Middle-earth's Second Age that is The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power. And for those who prefer a little more grit in their sandals, HBO's Westeros spinoffs are rapidly proving that Game of Thrones' poorly-received final season was just an unfortunate blip.

There is, then, a lot of fantasy TV around these days, with different flavors of the genre on offer to suit everyone's personal taste. And with all the major streamers getting in on the act, it would be almost ridiculous for the best two fantasy shows on TV right now to hail from the same platform. As it happens, the two best fantasy shows on TV right now not only hail from the same platform, they hail from the same franchise.

Even before forging its way onto streaming, the Critical Role franchise enjoyed significant success, popularizing the now-undisputed notion that viewers aplenty will watch a tabletop Dungeons and Dragons campaign broken up into online episodes. As well as spearheading a whole new format of fantasy storytelling, Critical Role became a brand unto itself by successfully introducing new campaigns. That ability to swap characters and storylines without losing momentum proved the group itself, and their endless creativity, was the real attraction.

From an early stage, anyone following Critical Role's journey could see the immense potential for a TV adaptation, and once Amazon joined the party as a partner, it became highly unlikely that The Legend of Vox Machina would roll streaming's equivalent of a natural one. But even the most passionate supporters of Critical Role's early endeavors couldn't have predicted just how deftly the franchise would transition to TV.

With its razor-sharp humor, handcrafted world-building, and tried-and-tested characters, The Legend of Vox Machina delivered that rarest of things: an adaptation to please existing fans while also enticing those who wouldn't recognize Matt Mercer if he was standing behind them in a convention toilet queue. Vox Machina's animated adventures are the pinnacle of fantasy television, beating every rival series by a comfortable distance.

Indeed, the only fantasy series coming close to The Legend of Vox Machina is its own Prime Video sibling series, The Mighty Nein, which offers an ingeniously magical twist on the same formula. Between them, the two shows are Dungeons and Dragons' equivalent of Breaking Bad and Better Call Saul in the sense that it's almost impossible to decide which is better. We only know they're both brilliant.

That dual success grants Prime Video the #1 and #2 spots on any list of the best fantasy shows around right now.

The prevalence of successful fantasy shows on TV can only mean one thing: we're getting more of them. From Harry Potter to Artemis Fowl, Brandon Sanderson to Stephen King, Fourth Wing to Ravenloft, streaming stands on the precipice of a massive fantasy explosion.

And yet none of them are obvious candidates to knock The Legend of Vox Machina and The Mighty Nein off the top spots.

The likes of Artemis Fowl and The Dark Tower are notoriously tricky to adapt, while expectations from book fans could hamper shows based on Fourth Wing, Stormlight Archive, and other highly popular works. Ravenloft's Dungeons and Dragons roots immediately set lofty expectations thanks to Amazon's Critical Role shows, and HBO's Harry Potter faces a near-impossible challenge to win over its many critics.

That's not to say that one of streaming's upcoming fantasy projects can't blossom into brilliance and overtake both The Legend of Vox Machina and The Mighty Nein. But, on paper at least, there isn't a project in the pipeline that'll have Scanlan waking up at night nervously grabbing his lute.

36 Years Later, Hulk's Greatest Design Still Has No Equal

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

Almost 40 years after its Marvel debut, the greatest Hulk design of all time has yet to be bested, despite major competition both before and after. Here's why the design in question deserves its crown, and which other contenders come close.

Inspired by horror icons Frankenstein and Mr. Hyde, the Hulk was created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby. Despite being one of the most iconic characters of all time, Hulk was originally very different - a grey-skinned jerk who spoke in sophisticated sentences and only came out at night.

Over the years, Hulk became the monosyllabic, emerald-skinned monster that Marvel fans know, though it took several of the most legendary creators in comic history to perfect Bruce Banner's other half.

The best Hulk design of all time is a hotly contested topic among fans of the Jade Giant, but we're handing the gold medal to Dale Keown, who drew The Incredible Hulk from 1990-1992 alongside acclaimed writer Peter David, with the two re-teaming for the non-canon Incredible Hulk: The End in 2002, which sees the character contend with a nuclear post-apocalypse.

Keown's Hulk is a barrel-chested behemoth with fists bigger than his head. His brow isn't just pronounced, it combines with a smaller, flatter nose and gaping mouth to create a hyper-expressive, highly distinct silhouette. The green skin is bright and luminous with a tonsure-like head of hair that looks like it didn't grow at the same rate as the rest of Bruce Banner's body.

So, what makes Keown our pick for the Hulk GOAT? It all comes down to one thing: Hulk's humanity. The best Hulk stories of all time understand that whatever Bruce Banner might think, the Hulk isn't a simple, singular expression of his rage. Hulk and Bruce are equally complex aspects of the same person - unique but deeply connected - and Keown communicates that better than any other artist.

There are a LOT of artists who have nailed Hulk's monstrous intensity and world-shattering rage. However, Keown's art is uniquely capable of showing Hulk laughing, glowering, grinning, wincing and roaring without losing the core of the character. Many of Hulk's earliest artists nail the sense of an impossible monster, but not one with a legitimate inner life.

The ability to depict different aspects of Hulk's personality was vital working alongside Peter David, who further splintered Bruce's psyche into the grey Joe Fixit, genius Professor Hulk and the brutal Maestro - the villain Bruce may one day become. These concepts have become the bedrock of Hulk's best modern stories - they were all good ideas, but it was Keown who ensured they were all 'Hulk.'

While we're holding Dale Keown up as creating the best Hulk design of all time, it's a close thing, given the wealth of talent who have worked on the character. And while you may not agree with our call, rest assured that there are many, many Hulk artists we still consider greats.

Hulk co-creator Jack Kirby drew Hulk with unbelievable menace, giving the character his heavy brow and tombstone teeth. Under Kirby, Hulk was a pure creature of the id - teeth bared and fists balled. Likewise, Herb Trimpe's Hulk is a howling monstrosity, reducing concrete to powder under his apish mitts and possessing a hunched, embattled physicality.

John Romita Sr.'s Hulk nails the concept of a monster that is still recognizably Bruce Banner, while Sal Buscema turned the character into a canvas for raw emotion. Despite a famously short run, it's worth noting that John Byrne accomplished the same sense of humanity as Keown, conveying a Hulk who is more than just rage.

In modern comics, Hulk has attracted some of the best artistic talent in comics. Adam Kubert's Hulk is an explosive mass of muscle that can't be contained by page or panel, Lee Weeks returns him to his Frankenstein-ian roots as a tragic homunculus, and John Romita Jr. captured a Hulk destroyed by grief and wrath, his humanity hanging on by a thread.

Bryan Hitch reinvented Hulk for the 2000s-era Ultimate Universe, turning him into a near-parodic wrecking ball of hate, lust and frustration. Daniel Acuña's work on Avengers: Twilight realized Bruce Banner's worst fears - the Hulk as an apocalyptic atrocity.

And while allegations that Joe Bennett hid antisemitic imagery in the background of Immortal Hulk can't be forgotten, he undeniably collaborated on the single greatest Hulk story of all time with writer Al Ewing, juxtaposing intense body horror and flying spittle with quiet, existential despair and righteous anger, bringing back Hulk's simian brow and bared teeth without abandoning the truth of a frightened child lashing out at an often deserving world.

While discussing Hulk's best comic designs, it would be silly to ignore his live-action appearances. Lou Ferrigno's snarling Hulk changed the franchise forever, acting as a near-unparalleled cultural touchstone. Ang Lee's Hulk embraced the imagery of a confused, superpowered toddler - dull-eyed and slathered in dirt - surpassing the pure rage of 2008's Incredible Hulk, at least in design.

However, the obvious winner is the MCU's Hulk, and for the same reasons as Dale Keown's art. Designed around the likeness of Bruce Banner actor Mark Ruffalo (who performs the Hulk's scenes using motion-capture technology), the most modern depiction of Hulk rages, but he also smirks, mourns, comprehends and fears.

This approach doesn't just make Hulk more interesting, it lets fans in on the vital lie that great Hulk stories deconstruct - that the Green Goliath is just a rage monster who lives inside Bruce Banner, rather than a vital aspect of his soul that's just as complicated and valid as the timid scientist.

Hulk's strength and size are essential to the character, but ultimately he also needs to be capable of emotional depth on par with his human side. The greatest piece of Hulk art of all time may or may not be by Dale Keown, but his design for the character encompassed everything the Hulk needs to be to tell the range of stories the character is capable of supporting.

Brock Taylor sets Vanderbilt football record with five field goals vs Mississippi State

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

STARKVILLE, MS − Brock Taylor set a Vanderbilt football single-game record by hitting five field goals in one game against Mississippi State.

The fifth field goal put the Commodores (3-6, 0-5 SEC) up, 22-14, in the fourth quarter against the Bulldogs (4-4, 2-4) in the game Nov. 7 at Davis-Wade Stadium.

Taylor's kicks were from 22, 34, 36, 38 and 54.

The previous record for most field goals in a single game was four, held by both Hawkins Golden in 1973 against Georgia and John Markham against Duke in 2000.

Taylor is up to 12 field goals this season and 43 in his career, which is third all-time in Commodores history. The school record for career made field goals is held by Markham, who made 47 between 1997-00. Taylor made 18 field goals in 2024 and 13 in 2025.

Nuxeo API authentication is only the first permission decision

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Nuxeo is a document platform with an API-oriented architecture. Its REST API can retrieve documents, inspect ACLs, run workflows, and expose custom endpoints. Authentication tells the server who is calling. The ACL attached to a document, folder, repository, or workflow determines what that identity can do.

On 2026-11-08, ZoomEye count-only observations returned 4 matches for app="Nuxeo", 186 for title="Nuxeo", and 28,973 for http.body="Nuxeo". The query used sub_type=all, page 1, and page size 1. The body count is far larger and can include documentation, examples, and unrelated pages. The application count is small, while the title count gives a broader product-level lead to validate.

Nuxeo documentation describes a default ACL managed through the document interface and permissions that can be granted or denied. It also describes an anonymous user that can be referenced when a repository administrator gives certain folders or documents public access. Anonymous access is therefore an explicit grant, not an accidental HTTP state.

The practical question is which anonymous grants still exist. A public document may be intentional, but a folder that was made public for a temporary campaign can become a durable information leak. The ACL enricher and @acl adapter can help an authorized operator inspect inherited and explicit access decisions.

Inventory each path. Confirm which headers are trusted, whether direct access to the application bypasses the proxy, and whether a test client can supply an identity header that the server accepts. A header that is safe only when stripped by the proxy is not an authentication control on a directly reachable listener.

List repositories and their public entry points. Identify top-level folders with broad read permissions, then inspect documents that are directly accessible through the REST path. Check inherited ACLs and any explicit deny or grant that changes the expected result.

Review principals, groups, and service accounts. An account used by a workflow may have access far beyond the worker's business purpose. Rotate credentials that are shared by multiple applications, and verify that removing a user from a group removes access to documents that the group granted.

Choose two accounts: one that should have access and one that should not. Request the same document by ID, path, and search endpoint. Then request the ACL and confirm that the effective decision matches the intended policy. Repeat after revoking a permission to ensure that cached authorization state does not preserve access longer than policy permits.

Use the REST API's error behavior as evidence, not as the whole test. A 404 can mean the document is hidden, while a 403 can reveal that it exists. Record the result without assuming that an authentication success means the object was authorized.

Search: ZoomEye application fingerprint for Nuxeo

Nuxeo's API is designed to be programmable. The same flexibility makes an ACL inventory necessary. The product finding is useful only when paired with repository-level authorization evidence.

Where To Spot The Airbus A380 In 2026: The 10 Airports With The Most Superjumbo Flights

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

The Airbus A380 remains one of the most recognizable aircraft in commercial aviation, and despite production having ended, the world's largest passenger airliner continues to operate an impressive international network. For enthusiasts hoping to catch the Superjumbo in action during 2026, some airports offer considerably better opportunities than others.

The Simple Flying Flight Tracker reveals where the aircraft makes the most appearances, which airlines operate the greatest number of services, and where spotters can find the widest variety of individual A380s. This article covers every Airbus A380 movement recorded between October 5 and November 3, 2026, providing a snapshot of the aircraft's continued global presence. Movements count each flight at both its departure and arrival airports, meaning a total of 14,140 movements represent 7,070 individual flights.

Manchester Airport (MAN) recorded 167 flights operated by the Airbus A380 between October 5 and November 3, with Emirates the sole operator of the type. Although its overall activity is considerably lower than that of the major international hubs, Manchester is notable because the Dubai-based carrier provides a regular Superjumbo presence, making it a convenient destination for those in northern England. Its position also gives British spotters an alternative to traveling to London to see the aircraft.

Meanwhile, New York John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) saw 284 Airbus A380 flights over the same period. These services were operated by the likes of Korean Air, Emirates, and Asiana Airlines, reflecting continuing demand for high-capacity international services connecting the US with major overseas destinations. This airport's presence in the list highlights how the A380 remains an important part of the transatlantic and transpacific aviation landscape.

Other airports also benefit from Emirates' continued commitment to the Airbus A380. Birmingham (BHX), Prague (PRG), Denpasar (DPS), and Christchurch (CHC) are among the destinations where enthusiasts can encounter the aircraft on daily services, while Glasgow (GLA) receives less frequent visits. These locations demonstrate that the Superjumbo's presence extends well beyond the world's largest connecting hubs, offering spotting opportunities at destinations that might otherwise appear unlikely to feature such a large aircraft.

According to the Simple Flying Flight Tracker, Paris Charles De Gaulle Airport (CDG) recorded 333 flights operated by the Airbus A380 between October 5 and November 3, 2026. Operators of the A380 to the French capital include Emirates, Qatar Airways, and Etihad Airways, demonstrating the continuing importance of Gulf-based airlines in maintaining the aircraft's European presence. Air France formerly operated a fleet of 10 A380s before retiring the aircraft type during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Meanwhile, Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) saw 342 Airbus A380 flights in the same period, making it one of the leading destinations in the United States. Its airline list includes Asiana Airlines, British Airways, Qantas, Korean Air, Emirates, and Lufthansa, giving it six different operators.

The airport's extensive long-haul connections explain its position in the ranking. Airlines deploy the A380 on selected routes linking Los Angeles with major international hubs across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. Its role as a major gateway to the US ensures that several international carriers continue to bring the aircraft to California from destinations including London Heathrow Airport (LHR), Seoul Incheon International Airport (ICN), and Dubai International Airport (DXB).

Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) recorded 356 Airbus A380 flights, operated by Asiana Airlines, Etihad Airways, Emirates, Qatar Airways, and Lufthansa, providing a healthy selection of operators to and from the Thai capital. The airport's position as a major international gateway to one of the world's most popular tourist destinations helps sustain its appeal among airlines operating busy long-haul routes.

Emirates, Qatar Airways and Etihad Airways provide important connections through their respective Gulf hubs, while Lufthansa and Asiana Airlines further diversify the airport's offering. Although Bangkok cannot match Dubai's flight frequency, its combination of aircraft numbers and airline variety makes it an attractive alternative. The presence of five operators also gives aviation enthusiasts a broader selection of aircraft to look out for on the Simple Flying Flight Tracker.

The US is a key long-haul market for superjumbo operators.

Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport (SYD) recorded 628 flights operated by the Airbus A380, placing it among the busiest airports in the world for the aircraft type. Airlines that operate the A380 to and from Sydney include Asiana Airlines, Singapore Airlines, Qantas, and Emirates, reflecting Australia's strong international travel market and demand for high-capacity long-haul services. The airport's extensive international connections make it an important destination for several of the world's remaining Superjumbo operators. Qantas is naturally an important part of the Sydney spotting experience, given its Australian headquarters and long-standing investment in the Airbus A380. Singapore Airlines and Emirates also contribute to the airport's activity, providing opportunities to see several different aircraft and liveries. This combination makes Sydney particularly interesting for those looking to compare the different configurations and appearances of international A380 operators.

Singapore Changi Airport (SIN) follows with a total of 895 flights operated by the Airbus A380 recorded between October 5 and November 3, 2026. British Airways, Qantas, Emirates, and Singapore Airlines all deploy their Superjumbos to the airport, reflecting its importance as a major destination for tourism, business, and transit flights. Singapore Airlines is particularly significant as the launch operator of the Airbus A380 back in 2007, and the aircraft remains part of its long-haul operations today, almost 20 years later.

London Heathrow Airport (LHR) saw 1,162 Airbus A380 flights, placing it second overall. However, its most impressive statistic concerns the number of different aircraft and airlines represented. Six different airlines operate the aircraft type to and from the UK's largest airport, which is also one of the busiest in the world for international traffic.

Airbus A380s from British Airways, Qantas, Emirates, Qatar Airways, Singapore Airlines, and Etihad Airways all appeared at the airport between October 5 and November 3, 2026. This makes London Heathrow particularly appealing for those looking to track a wide variety of different liveries.

With a total of 12 Airbus A380s in its fleet, British Airways is the joint-second largest operator of the aircraft, together with Singapore Airlines. The UK flag carrier deploys its A380s on selected long-haul services, including to Miami International Airport (MIA), San Francisco International Airport (SFO), and Singapore Changi Airport (SIN).

Emirates also maintains a significant presence with multiple daily frequencies, while Qatar Airways and Singapore Airlines connect London Heathrow with their respective international networks. Qantas and Etihad Airways provide further variety, ensuring that the airport regularly welcomes aircraft from several major global operators.

A disruption at one of cryptocurrency’s best-known investigative and compliance providers is also a test of operational resilience for the companies that depend on it. In a security incident notice updated November 6, Chainalysis said it had begun restoring access to its Know Your Transaction, or KYT, and address-screening products through a phased process.

The restoration followed several days of service restrictions and increasingly detailed disclosures about a cyberattack. Because many crypto businesses use screening tools to investigate suspicious transactions or meet compliance obligations, this is a story about both information security and the ability of regulated operations to continue.

Chainalysis initially reported discovering unauthorised activity affecting its internal systems and tools. Its October 30 statement said it had found no evidence at that point of access to certain sensitive materials. But the November 4 update disclosed that user information, including names, usernames, password hashes and live tokens, had been exposed to an attacker.

That sequence is important. Early incident statements often reflect a limited investigation, and later forensic work can establish a wider impact. The responsible reading is the latest dated disclosure, not a permanently frozen version of the first announcement.

The company also reported abnormal activity around KYT and other screening tools, prompting additional products to be taken offline. Chainalysis said it had no evidence of access to investigation content or manipulation of its attribution data at the time of its updates. That is a narrower claim than declaring every possible effect ruled out.

Chainalysis told customers to submit trusted IP addresses for allowlisting and prepare to reset passwords and rotate API keys as access is restored. A password hash is not the same as a plaintext password, but exposed hashes create security concerns depending on how they were generated and the strength of the underlying credentials. Live tokens can present a separate risk because they may enable access while valid.

For organisations, the operational response should follow the vendor’s direct instructions and their own security teams’ assessment. Users should not click unexpected reset links in unsolicited emails; signing in through a verified service address is safer than relying on a message that could be spoofed.

The wider issue of controls and dependency is also present in NewsBTC’s coverage of Aave’s protective restrictions on new tBTC positions. Different systems and risks are involved, but both illustrate how a provider’s status can affect the options available to dependent services.

Compliance teams need accurate screening, audit trails and clear decisions about when they can accept funds or process an onboarding request. Temporarily losing a primary tool can complicate those processes even if the underlying blockchain continues operating. Backups, manual escalation and incident playbooks are therefore part of real crypto infrastructure.

Chainalysis said restoration would expand as it validated enhanced security controls. It had not, in the cited November 6 update, confirmed full restoration of every affected product. That distinction should guide reporting and customer decisions.

The key future questions are the complete scope of exposure, remedial actions, the timetable for Reactor and other affected products and independent assurance that compromised credentials have been invalidated. For now, the firm has acknowledged a serious security incident and begun a controlled recovery.

Prima di guardare altrove, Icardi avrebbe provato a riaprire una porta che per lui ha sempre un significato particolare: quella dell'Inter. L'argentino si sarebbe persino dichiarato disponibile a un taglio importante dello stipendio e ad accettare un ruolo marginale nelle gerarchie offensive di Cristian Chivu. Da Milano, però, è arrivato un rifiuto senza possibilità di interpretazioni. Giuseppe Marotta, prima della sfida contro il Feyenoord, ha infatti escluso categoricamente un possibile ritorno, chiarendo che Icardi non rientra nei programmi della Beneamata. Ecco allora che prende corpo l'ipotesi Vecchia Signora. A rendere particolarmente suggestiva la candidatura bianconera è la presenza di Luciano Spalletti, allenatore che conosce bene Icardi e con il quale l'argentino ha vissuto alcune delle migliori stagioni della propria carriera. Insoddisfatta di Woltemade e Kolo Muani, la Juventus cerca maggiore peso offensivo e proprio un centravanti con esperienza internazionale come Maurito potrebbe rappresentare la soluzione giusta.

Il futuro di Hakan Calhanoglu resta uno dei principali nodi da risolvere in casa Inter. Il centrocampista turco, sotto contratto fino al 30 giugno 2027, vorrebbe continuare la propria esperienza in nerazzurro, ma la trattativa per il prolungamento è ancora in una fase interlocutoria. La distanza riguarda soprattutto l'aspetto economico. Calhanoglu percepisce circa 6,5 milioni di euro a stagione e vorrebbe un rinnovo biennale a cifre simili. L'Inter, invece, valuta una proposta più contenuta, considerando l'età del giocatore, che compirà 33 anni a febbraio, e i problemi fisici delle ultime stagioni: undici infortuni muscolari e oltre 200 giorni di indisponibilità. La società preferirebbe un accordo annuale con opzione, caratterizzato da una parte fissa più bassa e bonus legati al rendimento. Nei giorni scorsi Giuseppe Marotta aveva comunque rassicurato sull'andamento dei dialoghi: "Non siamo in una situazione di ansia per quanto concerne i rinnovi, c'è solo un giocatore che è in scadenza di contratto, ed è Calhanoglu, con cui abbiamo un ottimo rapporto". Intanto, attorno al centrocampista si muovono diversi club. Oltre agli interessamenti dalla Turchia, senza offerte finora ritenute adeguate, il suo nome è stato accostato alla Juventus. Secondo Tuttosport, anche il Como starebbe valutando il profilo del regista, considerato un possibile innesto d'esperienza per la squadra di Cesc Fabregas.

Secondo quanto raccolto da TMW, il primo obiettivo di Italiano sarebbe André Zambo Frank Anguissa del Napoli. Il trentenne di Yaoundé è in scadenza nell'estate che verrà e le trattative per il rinnovo sono più che in stallo. Non solo: il momento del Napoli certo non aiuta e non dà forza alla dirigenza nelle trattative sui nuovi contratti e c'è da immaginare che Anguissa adesso voglia valutare anche un progetto importante a livello sia sportivo che economico per il prosieguo di carriera. Non gli mancano certo le pretendenti, tra paesi arabi, Serie A e grandi leghe europee. Il centrocampista che abbini qualità, quantità, esperienza e struttura manca però in questo momento, quanto meno un altro giocatore di personalità che riesca a dare tanto a un Besiktas che nei momenti di difficoltà fatica a reagire. La differenza tra la qualità del Besiktas e la rosa per esempio del Galatasaray è lampante ed evidente. L'ambizione di Italiano è forte e concreta, i rinforzi per gennaio sono necessari. E quello di Anguissa può essere un nome caldo.

Democrats plan to push clean energy to lower bills and challenge Trump’s fossil fuel agenda

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Democrats can begin investigations and hold hearings into climate policy rollbacks and elimination of green energy grants by the Republican administration, potentially trying to undo some changes and restore renewable energy incentives.

Some Democrats are promising to push for more clean energy to help tackle rising energy costs when they take control of Congress in January, though President Donald Trump says he will use his veto power aggressively.

With new majorities in the House and Senate after last week’s midterms, Democrats can set an agenda boosting clean energy and addressing climate change. While those are traditionally core priorities of the party, climate was not a central topic in this election cycle.

Democrats can begin investigations and hold hearings into climate policy rollbacks and elimination of green energy grants by the Republican administration, potentially trying to undo some changes and restore renewable energy incentives.

Sen. Sheldon Whitehouse of Rhode Island, the top Democrat on the Senate Committee on Environment and Public Works, said Trump’s clean energy attacks drove up prices for taxpayers. He said Democrats will “work every angle to lower consumers’ energy costs, and we will demand accountability for the massive corruption wrought by Trump’s fossil-fuel donors.”

American Clean Power Association CEO Jason Grumet said everybody who is trying to build clean energy “feels bolstered.”

Enacting major changes would require Republican support because Democrats will lack the numbers to override presidential vetoes. Even then, lower energy bills are not guaranteed because prices also depend on fuel costs, demand and the expense of maintaining the electric grid.

“The wins give Democrats more leverage over spending and more political power to advance a coherent agenda; the question is whether they’ll use that power effectively,” said Lisa Sachs, director of the Columbia Center on Sustainable Investment. “The vote was for affordability and opportunity. Democrats now need to be clear about which policies deliver them.”

Trump said after Tuesday’s election that Democrats will send over “things that are insane” and he will veto them, speaking generally about legislation. But in the comments to Fox News, he predicted that “after a couple of months of vetoes,” he and Democrats will find ways to work together.

The White House said in a statement Wednesday that Trump is “committed to unleashing reliable, affordable, and secure energy sources to lower prices, strengthen our grid, and protect our national security, and is willing to work with anyone to accomplish this.”

Trump promised to lower energy prices. Instead, his war with Iran and other policies increased the price of gasoline, diesel and electricity. At the same time, the administration canceled many clean energy projects and grants and slowed clean energy permitting.

Trump particularly dislikes offshore wind. Developer Orsted said Thursday that Revolution Wind, a large wind farm in federal waters off Rhode Island, has started operating commercially.

Will the president change his tune on green energy? That’s doubtful, said former Sen. Byron Dorgan, D-N.D., co-chair of the government relations practice at ArentFox Schiff.

“He is likely to keep pushing his energy issues, and I think he will be forced to give some room for a new Congress to recreate a different strategy that will add clean energy,” Dorgan said in an email.

Trump promotes expanding production of oil, natural gas and coal, which cause climate change, because he argues that would give the U.S. the lowest-cost electricity. Mike Sommers, CEO of the American Petroleum Institute, the oil and gas industry’s top lobbying group, said that as voters have changed the balance of political power in Washington, “the affordability message is unmistakable.”

Sommers said in a statement there is a real opportunity for bipartisan cooperation to reduce costs and strengthen the economy, starting with an energy permitting reform proposal announced in September. The legislation is aimed at shortening the timeline to get big energy projects started.

Ife Kilimanjaro, executive director of the U.S. Climate Action Network, said Democrats should reject that proposal and use their stronger position to pass legislation that actually builds out much-needed clean energy.

The administration has reshaped the Environmental Protection Agency, reversing pollution limits as it promotes fossil fuels.

Clear Street energy transition analyst Tim Moore said he expects Democrats to investigate the climate and environmental rollbacks, in an effort to block some from taking effect. Democrats could also try amending the Clean Air Act to cut carbon pollution and push back against EPA budget cuts, he added.

Rep. Sean Casten, D-Ill., said he is cautiously optimistic some Republicans will now seek to differentiate themselves from Trump and back pro-environmental policies. But without Republican support, Democrats’ more ambitious bills will largely lay out their agenda for 2028, he added.

Climate is not necessarily at the top of Democrats’ list of priorities. They are targeting healthcare access, trade tariffs and the Iran war.

Rep. Frank Pallone, the New Jersey Democrat who is expected to become chair of the House Energy and Commerce Committee, said the party should focus on healthcare, affordability, consumer and environmental protections, clean energy and regulating Big Tech.

David Victor, a professor of innovation and public policy at the University of California, San Diego, largely expects a stalemate at the federal level on climate and energy policy because of the divided government. He said agreement could come on some bipartisan measures such as a permitting overhaul.

Victor thinks Democratic-led investigations and hearings will center on corruption and other topics that may only touch on energy and climate. Like Casten, Victor said many of the Democratic bills likely will not become law, but will outline what Democrats could accomplish if they win the White House in 2028.

“The Democrats are going to be drafting things that the administration’s not going to like. They don’t have the votes to override a veto, and on top of it, the administration is going to be in turmoil because of its own electoral failures and because of all the investigations,” Victor said. “This seems to me, at the federal level, largely a recipe for kind of intense chaos over the next two years.”

One of the biggest wild cards is the behavior of the administration, which could use executive power to double down on rollbacks of climate and environmental rules, he said.

Democrats scored victories in races for governor in states long controlled by Republicans, including Texas and Georgia.

Leah Stokes, a political scientist at the UC Santa Barbara, said there could be a lot of state action on climate next year because of those wins. That is where climate leadership has traditionally come from, she noted. At the federal level, Stokes is optimistic about extending clean energy tax credits since some Republicans support that.

Jay Inslee, a former Democratic governor and congressman from Washington, also expects growing momentum for clean energy in states that elected Democratic governors, particularly in states where they also won legislative majorities.

“This is a really powerful instrument for the United States to join the rest of the world and get back in a leadership position, even if we still have a climate denier that might exercise a veto on some things that we want to do federally,” Inslee said. “So I hope people will pay attention to the big progress we’re going to make on a state-level basis.“By JENNIFER McDERMOTT Associated Press
