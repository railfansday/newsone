The episode closes with Em returning to the real world and threatening to kill his Slim Shady alter ego, who’s hiding out in the spaghetti, a callback to his 2024 album, The Death of Slim Shady (Coup de Grâce), which arrived in July 2024 and topped the Billboard 200.

Eminem has reached the summit of the Billboard Hot 100 on five separate occasions in his decorated career. New Homes Offer More Affordability for Buyers: Report

📅 Nov 19, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

With affordability remaining an issue at large in housing, new homes continue to represent a tempting alternative in a challenging market.

The latest data from the National Association of Home Builders (NAHB) found that affordability for new homes actually improved more than that of existing homes in Q3, focused on both home prices and rates.

The Q3 NAHB/Wells Fargo Cost of Housing Index (CHI) found that families earning the national median income of $106,800 needed 33% of their income to buy a median-priced new home, compared to 36% of their income for a median-priced existing home.

Low-income families (those who earn only 50% of the national median income) need 66% of their income to buy a new home, compared to 73% for an existing home.

NAHB Chairman Bill Owens noted that this was the “widest affordability advantage (for new homes) since the CHI began,” as builders “are working hard to offset rising costs.”

The report also noted that this is due in part to the difference in price between new homes ($400,400 median price) and existing homes ($416,700 median price), called out as the widest national median price gap since the CHI began.

The other attribution for the new home “affordability advantage” is the incentives and deals offered by builders.

Owens called out NAHB’s recent data on builder confidence, which saw 43% of builders offer price cuts in October—the highest share since 2020. Additionally, 65% of builders offered sales incentives.

A recent Realtor.com® report also found that many buyers purchasing new homes are seeing lower mortgage rates due to builders offering buy downs or other deals with mortgage lenders. The average starting mortgage rate of a new-home buyer was 5.01% in Q3, compared with 6.15% for existing-home buyers.

While new homes are offering better affordability than existing homes, overall housing affordability is still very much challenged.

For both new and existing homes, families earning the national median income remain fairly cost-burdened (paying more than 30% of their income for housing) or severely cost-burdened (paying more than 50% of their income for housing).

Of the 175 metro areas tracked in the report, 87 are cost-burdened by housing, and eight are severely cost-burdened. Leading the way among the eight severely cost-burdened markets is San Jose, California, where families use 79% of their income on housing.

Owens outlined that in order to move housing affordability in the right direction, “policymakers must do more to reduce red tape, address labor shortages and fix supply chains so builders can expand the nation’s housing supply.”

“As builders contend with rising construction costs, tighter lending standards and persistent labor shortages, policymakers should remove regulatory obstacles, enact permitting reform and create a stronger business environment that enables builders to produce more attainable, affordable homes,” added NAHB Chief Economist Robert Dietz.
