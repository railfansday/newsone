Leeds United Scout 'Phenomenal' Bagsman Beljo to Replace Nmecha

📅 Nov 11, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Leeds United are said to have joined Manchester United and Newcastle United in scouting a reliable goalscorer as they contemplate strengthening their front line. Dinamo Zagreb's Dion Beljo is said to be the man piquing the interest of the Whites, who are in need of goals.

The Whites have had a solid start to the season, and while there were concerns over their form after going five games without a win, a big home win over Tottenham has put things back in perspective. Daniel Farke's men didn't come particularly close to relegation last season, but they were involved in the relegation battle, but they currently sit ninth in the table, which represents a significant improvement.

But there are still some issues to be ironed out, and one of them is the front line. Dominic Calvert-Lewin has provided four goals this season, but he needs more help and competition. In fact, the Whites have only scored 10 goals in the league this season, making them the joint-third-lowest scorers in the league.

Farke also has Mateo Joseph and Lukas Nmecha as striking options, but the former has been dealing with an injury issue, and Nmecha is not a goalscorer. To take the next step in their evolution, the Whites may well need a clinical finisher, and Beljo is one of the names to have been suggested.

The 24-year-old has been in impressive form for Zagreb, scoring 13 goals in 11 league games and four in just three Europa League appearances.

According to TeamTalk, Leeds and Newcastle United watched Beljo in action in England recently, when Zagreb paid Sunderland in the Europa League and Beljo scored the second of two goals to secure a 2-0 away win. Manchester United have also been linked, with Beljo clearly a reliable goalscorer, already amassing 115 goals at senior level in a relatively young career. He also has four senior caps for Croatia, scoring once.

Leeds could well bid Nmecha goodbye in January, especially given he is out of contract next summer, meaning the winter window could be their last chance to get some money back for the German, if he doesn't agree a pre-contract agreement in the meantime.

Replacing Nmecha with a reliable hitman would be a wise move, and while we don't know if Beljo will be a hit in the Premier League, his numbers suggest he is more dangerous in front of goal than Nmecha.

Beljo scored 31 goals in 36 league games last season, leading his admittedly biased agent, Srđan Lakić, to say: "I said already two years ago in an interview, and I always say this, that I think Beljo is the best Croatian striker.

"Beljo is having a phenomenal season. He's immediately behind Kane, Haaland and Mbappé in the Golden Boot race. Not many players in the Croatian league have scored in that manner. That deserves respect."

Beljo is under contract at Dinamo Zagreb until 2030, and Transfermarkt value him at £12.5million, but he is likely to cost more, especially in the usually expensive winter window.

And the second international break of the campaign may have come at the right time for some, whose positive starts have been somewhat faltering in recent weeks.

Going into the previous weekend, the two teams who had accumulated the most points and had arguably been the most impressive were both Middlesbrough and Southampton.

"Maybe the international break is coming at a really good time for those clubs."

The three clubs that all came down from the Premier League earlier this year have also had their trials and tribulations, with Burnley the one really struggling the most at the wrong end of the table.

Wolves and West Ham are still in the mix at the top end of the standings, but it is the Hammers that Goodman believes will make it back to the top flight at the first time of asking between the two.

"You've got to say that along with Middlesbrough and Southampton, the best squads in the Championship belong to Wolves and West Ham," Goodman added.

"But there's just something unbelievably underwhelming about both of those teams at the moment.

"I know that Wolves are picking up points at a very good rate but feel like they're scraping points.

"Whereas when you've watched Middlesbrough and when you've watched Southampton, a lot of the time, you see them dominate games of football for literally the entirety of the game.

"You just don't really see that with West Ham or Wolves, so if I'm going to pick one, I think West Ham can only get better.

"I think we've seen the worst of them at the moment, so I would expect West Ham to climb up the table."

With the new Parker-led West Ham one of the three teams being tipped for promotion from the Championship, which two others does Goodman believe are heading to the upper echelons of English football with them?

"I would expect Middlesbrough and Southampton to recover after the international break, so if I'm picking three, then they would be it," Goodman concluded.

"But honourable mentions for sure to Birmingham City and to West Bromwich Albion.

Agents Look Ahead as AI Keeps Advancing: ‘We Have the Privilege of Being in a Human Industry’

📅 Nov 11, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Jbeau Lewis, Cristina Baxter, Robby Fraser, Jarred Arfa, Josh Mulder and Shirin Nury joined the conversation at the 2026 Billboard Live Music Summit.

Agents Jbeau Lewis of UTA, Cristina Baxter of THE•TEAM, Robby Fraser of WME, Jarred Arfa of Independent Artists Group, Josh Mulder of TBA and Shirin Nury of CAA joined the Billboard Live Music Summit at the 1 Hotel in West Hollywood, California, on Tuesday (Nov. 10). Explore Billboard See latest videos, charts and news

Moderated by Billboard senior editor Taylor Mims, the panel spanned how the middle class of touring has been getting squeezed in recent years, rising production costs, smart tour packaging, and how far in advance tours are being booked. Related Michael Sanft, Attorney Who Represented Keffe D in 2Pac Murder Trial, Dies at 54 Kenny MacPherson, Veteran Music Publisher and Big Deal Music Co-Founder, Dies at 71 Diddy Moved From New Jersey Prison to Similar Facility in Pennsylvania

Baxter spoke of how rising tour costs and larger production has created a fine line on becoming profitable, with artists wanting to present their all but needing to be mindful of budget. For artists performing clubs and theaters, those challenges are even more apparent.

“The margins are just so razor thin now,” Baxter says. “Whether a tour is profitable or not and becomes a successful, sold-out tour, can come down to just a couple of decisions, such as the number of days and the cost of a certain element in production.”

Arfa added, “There are situations when making money may not even be the goal. Maybe an artist is trying to ascend to a new level, and the conversations are how to break even.”

As ticket prices continue to rise, those on the panel also noted that concerts may be considered a more luxury experience. Following the COVID pandemic, touring experienced a boom with more artists on the road and more tickets sold than ever before. Now, those in the live industry are seeing that growth has begun to slow, with 2024 and 2025 experiencing a plateau in ticket gross, according to Billboard’s 2025 year-end touring report.

“There’s been a sea-change in our business post pandemic,” Lewis said. “We saw a dramatic rise and change in the model and behavior of consumers. Expecting it to rise consistently is unrealistic. I think we’re probably there now.”

Despite this, tours are being booked further in advance, with some agents already looking to 2028 and 2029. As they look ahead, agents note the need for flexibility.

“You need to be ready to route and reroute many times,” Baxter says.

Nury added, “I think we’re living in such an interesting time, with so much volatility in the label system. I think the rest of the industry is really looking to agents to act as the architects for a rollout.”

Lewis noted, “We’re going to keep booking ahead as far as we can, but it’s going to come with challenges.”

While looking to the future, the agents suggested that technological advancements and artificial intelligence don’t seem to pose a massive threat to their careers.

“AI pulls from a certain data set that’s available to it to make decisions, but there’s so much more that goes into the way a tour is routed and the reason you choose a timeframe,” Baxter said. “A lot of that is not publicly available information… I don’t see it impacting [touring and booking] the same way as some other industries.”

Nury concluded, “We have the great privilege of being in a human industry, a social industry.”

With Forest standing to benefit immensely from it.

Bitcoin is struggling to find its footing after another brutal sell-off pushed its price below $73,000. The decline wiped out hundreds of millions of dollars in leveraged positions, leaving us questioning if the market has entered a deeper correction phase.

Bitcoin is now trading around $73,600 after briefly losing the $73,000 level. Ethereum slipped below $2,100, while XRP approached $1.05 and Solana struggled to hold $82. More than $723 million in crypto positions were liquidated over 24 hours, with nearly 79% coming from long bets.

The timing is particularly uncomfortable for Bitcoin bulls. October’s hotter-than-expected US inflation report has revived concerns about interest rates, while recent US election results have introduced another layer of regulatory uncertainty. Now, a banking dispute involving the Federal Reserve is adding fuel to the fire.

The sell-off has several forces working against it. First, October’s US Consumer Price Index (CPI) increased 0.4% month over month, exceeding economists’ expectations of 0.3%. Core inflation also climbed 0.3%, above the 0.2% forecast.

The interest-rate outlook has become even more concerning. According to CME’s latest rate probability data, the chance of the Federal Reserve keeping its target rate unchanged at 3.75%–4% has fallen to zero. This represents a sharp reversal from just one day earlier, when the probability of unchanged rates stood at 16%. It was 17.3% a week ago and 15.9% one month earlier.

Markets now assign an 86.3% probability to a 25-basis-point rate increase, taking the target range to 4%–4.25%. Another 13.7% probability is assigned to a larger 50-basis-point increase.

The liquidation data paints an equally uncomfortable picture. CoinGlass recorded $732 million in forced closures across crypto markets over 24 hours on November 11. Long positions accounted for $578 million, meaning traders betting on higher prices absorbed most of the damage.

Bitcoin also lost its 50-week moving average near $76,100, ending a seven-week streak above the technical level. The breakdown followed a week of heavy institutional selling, with US spot Bitcoin ETFs recording approximately $1.28 billion in net outflows.

The combination of ETF withdrawals, leveraged liquidations and inflation uncertainty has created a difficult environment for buyers. Social chatter has pushed back against describing the move as a full-blown crash, pointing to comparisons with the Nasdaq’s monthly decline. Nevertheless, losing important technical levels has given bears another reason to test market confidence.

The crypto community is increasingly discussing the possibility of a Gensler 2.0 regulatory environment. The concern centers on whether a tougher approach could return for digital asset companies, particularly decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs), digital asset treasuries (DATs), memecoins, and privacy-focused crypto.

Lighthouse issues urgent Ethereum node patch due to hidden security flaws

📅 Nov 11, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Ethereum's Lighthouse urged all beacon node operators to install a high-priority security patch ahead of a planned vulnerability disclosure.

Ethereum consensus client Lighthouse released a security patch on Nov. 9 and urged operators of all its beacon nodes to upgrade as soon as possible.

The v8.3.3 release makes that work a high priority for users who stake and those who do not, but the release notice rates validator-client upgrades as low priority for staking users.

As a consensus client, Lighthouse connects with other clients in Ethereum's proof-of-stake network. Its beacon node works with an execution engine to validate transactions in blocks, while the validator client connects to the beacon node and manages keys, according to the Lighthouse Book.

Operators can also run a beacon node without a validator client. Lighthouse's documentation includes data analysis and applications among these non-staking uses, and those operators receive the same high-priority beacon-node instruction as staking users in the v8.3.3 update table.

The project's priority definitions put high-priority work on a timescale of hours or days, to be completed as soon as possible. Low-priority work belongs in the next routine update cycle, with two weeks given as an example.

The beacon-node upgrade is high priority with or without staking. Staking users have a separate, lower-priority validator-client update.

Upgrade the beacon node promptly. The validator-client update has low priority and belongs in the routine cycle.

Beacon nodes used for applications or data analysis have the same high-priority instruction. No validator-client priority applies to this group.

Staking and non-staking beacon nodes are high priority; staking validator clients can use the routine update cycle.

Bukayo Saka told he will struggle to get back his place in Arsenal team - expert

📅 Nov 11, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Noni Madueke's superb form for Arsenal will spur on Bukayo Saka to get back from injury and return to action.

That is the opinion of experienced former Tottenham, Aston Villa and Everton scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The winger has been missing since October, when he injured his quad during a game against Bayern Munich in the Champions League.

Madueke has taken his role on the right for Arsenal in his absence, and has looked an entirely transformed player in recent weeks.

The former Chelsea man arrived in a £52million deal in the summer of 2025 but was unconvincing in his first season at the club.

However, there is hope that the 24-year-old is now starting to realise the potential Mikel Arteta and Arsenal expected of him, with Saka facing a battle to get back into the team.

Noni Madueke has been in fine form for Arsenal and leads the way in the Premier League for chances created per match 👏📊 pic.twitter.com/p3hxg9FWHR

For years, there were concerns over the lack of competition and cover for Saka, with his frequent injuries now appearing to be a direct consequence.

But finally, in Madueke, it appears that Arsenal may have found a winger capable of pushing the best out of the 25-year-old.

"There's nothing better than a player with confidence, and players around him with confidence," King told Football Insider.

"It shows when people are saying, 'well, they're not missing Saka as much as you thought you might do when Madueke came in'.

"I bet Saka can't wait to get back in, because no player likes being injured and he's certainly a player that we all know has got great ability.

"He's going to have to up his game to get back in the team, which is only good for the team."

With the England international now seemingly having undergone a transformation to his mentality, there is plenty of excitement surrounding his future.

"The way he reacted to that I think is a great example for the rest of the squad, which is, 'I want to do more,'" Arteta said last week, referring to Madueke starting just two of the opening seven games of the season.
