Scrambled Eggs Get A Major Protein And Flavor Boost When You Add This Omega-Rich Ingredient

📅 Nov 16, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

We may receive a commission on purchases made from links.

When many of us think "hot breakfast," we tend to naturally gravitate toward thoughts of scrambled eggs. Aside from the fact that, when prepared correctly, they are tender, delicate, and fluffy (or custardy, depending on how you like them), eggs are considered the gold standard for protein, so they are quite wholesome as well. On their own, though, they can get a little monotonous, especially if you're making them every day, so it does help to add an ingredient here or there. And if you happen to enjoy omega-3-rich cold-smoked salmon, well, it pairs seamlessly with a classic scramble, boosting both the flavor and the protein content.

Once considered an expensive delicacy, smoked salmon is quite popular now in the U.S., and as a result, it's much easier to find at your local grocery store (even Aldi carries its own version, and it can be found at Walmart, too). Cold-smoked salmon, despite the fact that it tends to be packed in super-thin slices, is buttery and velvety. While it does taste fishy, there are also light, smoky notes that give it a deeply savory flavor, which works beautifully when combined with the mild egg scramble. Additionally, a serving (which will vary in size) might contain anywhere from 12 to 18 grams of protein, which is great if you have daily goals you want to meet or you're just looking to get more of the macronutrient in general.

Truly, the most difficult part about incorporating smoked salmon into your scramble is cooking the eggs to the right consistency without burning or browning them. All you have to do with the salmon is slice the ultra-thin pieces into slivers or small squares. Once your eggs have reached the texture you desire, you'll take the pan off the heat and fold in the salmon. That's literally it; you don't have to actually cook the salmon over direct heat because it's so delicate that the residual warmth of the scrambled eggs will gently bring it up to temperature.

Now, because smoked salmon is so light and thin, you might want your eggs to match that energy, so create a fluffy, soft texture with a bit of cream whisked in. Alternatively, you could take a page out of Bobby Flay's book and fold in a dollop of crème fraîche, or really up the decadence and melt in some cubes of cream cheese (and take care not to overcook anything, because smoked salmon with burnt, rubbery eggs? No thanks). Also, because all of the elements involved are either rich or thick, a sprinkling of something green, like fresh chives or dill, or even a little parsley or tarragon, would brighten things up, both aesthetically and in terms of flavor.

The moment that galvanised England to deliver major statement against Spain

Spain vs England was a game that had almost everything, and may yet mean more; perhaps the most significant moment might have come before it had even started.

The hosts took the opportunity of a first match back in Madrid since the World Cup to showcase the trophy, in what almost felt another homecoming.

The mood was very much of revelling in their embossed status, something that would have had a particular edge for this specific nation.

No country that has won the World Cup has waited as long for another trophy as England.

And, as the 2010 winners Iker Casillas, Fernando Torres and Fernando Llorente presented Unai Simon that with that precious piece of brilliant gold, it was almost literally in England’s faces.

Thomas Tuchel’s side had to watch the goalkeeper wave the World Cup about right in front of them.

It had been noted on the Spanish side that Jude Bellingham was the only player who clapped, this now being the city that he calls home with Real Madrid. He then played like he was demanding the same appreciation, and he was not the only one.

As one figure with knowledge of the England camp said: “They will have been thinking ‘that’s what we want’.”

Seeing the World Cup so close and yet so far, with more than three years until the next edition, only deepened a mood that had been felt in the build-up to this game.

England, in the words of both Declan Rice and Thomas Tuchel, knew they were going to win.

“I could feel the true belief in the dressing room before the match that this is exactly the moment where we want to prove ourselves and push our limits,” the manager said.

They had that conviction to show what they were really about, and to show they would be the only team in the world not cowed by Spain. It was that mentality that comes when you feel ready to beat a rival that has long had it over you; that has been a benchmark.

A pace was set just moments after seeing that trophy, as a ferocious England subjected Simon to a flurry.

Just as importantly, they displayed a new level of their own, even beyond Tuchel describing it as the best performance of his time.

This wasn’t, after all, the usual counter-attacking approach to playing Spain - at least not for when England actually got so far ahead.

It was England imposing their game, an evolving ideology, on Spain.

“We are well aware which steps we took after the World Cup,” Tuchel said, conspicuously repeating this more than once.

The manager was deeply stung after all the criticism following the Argentina defeat.

And yet, much as he might bristle about it in public, it does feel as if some of that criticism has been acknowledged.

England look much more technical, but still with pace.

Throughout the World Cup, after all, there was a definite sense of his team just seeking to force their way through.

This was something much more considered and coherent.

“We wanted to get better in the deep build-up, we wanted to get better at defending in a low block and on top of it, we wanted always to impose our identity and the way we want to play on every opponent,” Tuchel remarked.

This similarly feeds into another theme of the night, which was how James Trafford’s performance may start a path that sees him become England’s No 1.

It was conspicuous that, when asked about the goalkeeper’s brilliant late double-save, Tuchel immediately pointed to his pure football effect.

“But there was a lot to do in the deep build-up as well, to overcome the first line of press, which he did very well with everyone around him,” he said. “So there was a lot to do, never lose the concentration, never lose the focus, never lose the vision, to find the free man to overcome the press and put the game into the opponent’s half.”

Tuchel clearly wants to change the very focus of this team.

And, in doing so, he wants to change its entire outlook.

There was naturally a lot of talk about “confidence” after a victory like this, which Tuchel was only too happy to immerse himself in.

“You want confidence, not only through results but confidence through performance and the result follows,” Tuchel said. “That’s the ideal process and that’s what we got.”

When Spain beat France in the World Cup semi-final with what was the performance of the tournament - “the statement performance” as Tuchel would have it - some close to Didier Deschamps’s squad felt the reason for the win had actually come much earlier.

Spain had beaten France in a series of high-profile games, above all the Euro 2024 and World Cup 2026 semi-finals. Lamine Yamal had been right to emphasise that, because he knew it played on French minds.

It gave Spain an edge. They knew they could beat France, but France were unsure if they could beat Spain.

That’s also why the frantic ending to this game in Madrid, so at odds with the first hour, may have been so important.

Had Spain equalised, or potentially won, England would have just been another team to fail to beat them. It could similarly have been the deepening of England’s own complex about Spain, since this would have been three high-profile set-backs in succession.

Even if this had just been a draw, after all, it would have felt like a defeat given it was a 3-0 lead.

It was a win, and a first defeat that Spain have endured in 44 games over two and a half years.

As such, England are not just another team the Spanish are reigning over.

They’re a team that know how to beat them, that can carry that through to a home Euros.

It might even create a bit of consideration on the Spanish side, even allowing for how assured they are in their own approach.

It might be the beginning of a proper modern rivalry.

Might, of course. England have to go and use this to do what Spain have made customary, and actually win one of those trophies.

How Are Hotels Paid When You Redeem Points, And For Elite Perks?

📅 Nov 16, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

There’s a real thrill to redeeming points for stays at luxury properties, and getting outsized value. I’m sure many people have wondered about the economics of that. How much is the hotel getting reimbursed for your stay? Who is paying for any elite perks that you receive? Does the hotel care if you redeemed points or not? Let’s talk about those various points in this post…

The major global hotel groups (Hilton, Hyatt, IHG, Marriott, etc.) don’t own a vast majority of their properties. Instead, the companies have an asset light strategy, and have management or franchise contracts for the individual properties.

In some cases, large investment firms own hotels, and they may have properties belonging to all kinds of brands. In other cases, a hotel may even be family owned.

The way the major hotel groups make money is primarily through fees (they’re also increasingly directly monetizing their loyalty programs, but that’s a whole different topic). There’s typically an initial fee to open the hotel, and then on top of that, there are fees on ongoing revenue. Then any upside (or downside) typically falls with the hotel owner. So the major hotel groups are making money, regardless of whether a hotel is raking in the cash or hemorrhaging money.

Now, one of the reasons that consumers flock to certain hotel groups is because of their loyalty programs, which are massive marketing machines. A big part of the value proposition of these loyalty programs is the ability to redeem points.

As you’d expect, those points are redeemed centrally through the program, and then the loyalty program has to “buy” that room from the hotel. After all, the hotel owner wants to be compensated for each head in a bed, especially since they’re already paying a system fund fee, to fund these perks. That brings us to the question of how hotels are reimbursed…

As you’d expect, hotel groups don’t publicly disclose how they reimburse their properties when members redeem points. However, over the years we’ve been able to get some insights into this.

Sometimes guests are given folios that are actually intended for the loyalty program, while in other cases the reimbursement rates showed up online when they weren’t supposed to.

So I’m totally making this up here, but if a hotel is charging $400 on a particular night, maybe the loyalty program would pay $80 for that room if occupancy is under 90%, while it might be paying $350 if occupancy is over 90%.

Note that the number of points you redeem for a stay doesn’t directly determine reimbursement for a hotel. In other words, a hotel doesn’t get paid X amount per redeemed point. Similarly, if a program offers a fifth night free on award redemptions, the hotel is getting reimbursed for all five nights, as it’s the loyalty program incurring the cost there.

Instead, points redemption rates are set by the loyalty program, and are intended to reflect the cost incurred by the program. That’s why in many cases you’ll find that redemption rates don’t correlate directly to how much a property would cost in cash, but rather also factor in average occupancy at a property.

A hotel loyalty program would probably rather you redeem your points at a $1,000 per night hotel that’s 50% full, rather than a $500 per night hotel that’s 95% full, because the latter redemption will cost the program more. Hotel loyalty programs use historical data to determine points pricing, and the costs they incur.

Let me actually give a real example from a recent stay. I recently redeemed points for a two night stay at a Wyndham Rewards property, at the cost of 30,000 points per night. The folio I was sent accidentally had the amount Wyndham had to pay, rather than the amount I had to pay.

Even though I was paying the same number of points both nights, the hotel was being reimbursed around $87 for one night, and $184 for the next night. Funny enough, while sitting in the lobby working the first morning, I overheard one front desk agent tell the other that they were fully sold out the following night, so that tracks. The first night was being reimbursed at a reduced rate, while the second night was being reimbursed based on something closer to the average daily rate.

If you have elite status with a hotel loyalty program, how does taking advantage of elite perks factor into this when redeeming points? Who pays for breakfast? Who pays for suite upgrades? Who pays for waived destination fees and parking?

As you can see, funding elite benefits is a combined effort between the loyalty program and hotels.

For those curious, generally individual hotels don’t know how you earned your elite status. So it doesn’t matter to them if you earned it through actual hotel stays, or just by holding onto a credit card. Presumably a front desk agent could look at your stay history, but they typically wouldn’t have any reason to do so, in this context.

This is a loaded question, and there’s obviously no single right or wrong answers. A few thoughts, though.

Hotel staff are trained by corporate to treat guests redeeming points the same way as those paying cash. Many hotel groups even train front desk staff to thank people for redeeming their points, since it reflects engagement in the program. Officially, you should expect exactly the same treatment as if you paid cash, and you absolutely shouldn’t feel sheepish about the fact that you didn’t pay cash for your stay.

Now, in practice that might not always be how things play out. There’s sometimes a back and forth between hotel owners and hotel groups. Hotel owners often want it both ways — they want the power of the major hotel group distribution channels and loyalty programs, without actually incurring the costs associated with it, whether it’s complimentary upgrades, points redemptions, or a decent breakfast.

Will you sometimes find a hotel that’s perhaps a bit condescending if you redeem points and have an issue? Absolutely, but it’s not how it should be, and it’s the exception rather than the norm. Some may remember my stay at the Aegon Mykonos, at the time a Marriott Autograph Collection property (the hotel has since… mysteriously disappeared?).

Long story short, I was downgraded despite using a confirmed upgrade, and the manager was completely unapologetic about it, and was unwilling to do anything meaningful. One of his defenses was that I was staying “complimentary,” and therefore shouldn’t be complaining.

The hotel industry is complex, given the various parties involved, from the hotel group to the individual hotel owners. Hopefully the above is a useful rundown of how reimbursement works when redeeming points. The important thing to understand is that hotels get paid a lot more when you’re redeeming points if the property is full.

When redeeming points, you definitely shouldn’t feel sheepish about it. You earned your stay, and the reason hotels belonging to major loyalty programs are able to command a revenue premium is because of the points and loyalty perks they offer.

Capcom Reveals Fanmade Robot Master for Mega Man: Dual Override

📅 Nov 16, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

The most iconic characters in the Mega Man franchise, second only to ol’ Rock himself, are the Robot Masters, the powerful, highly-specialized robots built by Dr. Light, Dr. Wily, or whoever else who are programmed to wreak havoc upon the world, usually in batches of eight. Naturally, the newest upcoming entry in the mainline series, Mega Man: Dual Override, will have a new swath of Robot Masters to do battle with, several of which like Jet Man, Cook Man, and Twinkle Girl have already been revealed.

However, in addition to these new Capcom-made Robot Masters, Dual Override is slated to bring back a long-dormant tradition of the Mega Man series: fan-designed Robot Masters. Not long after the game’s initial reveal, Capcom launched a character design contest in which fans were encouraged to design their own original Robot Master and submit it for a chance to be included in the game.

Capcom showed off a new Mega Man: Dual Override demo at Tokyo Game Show this year, proving the veteran franchise has still got it.

Many months later, Capcom has officially announced the winner of this contest and revealed the fanmade Robot Master who will be joining the rest of the new Wily Numbers. Strap on your scouring pads and turn your spray nozzles to “ON,” because our latest menace is Cleanser Man.

As director Shinya Ikuta and producer Hiroyuki Minamitani announced in a video revealed during its TwitchCon 2026 panel and posted to the official Mega Man YouTube channel, Cleanser Man was selected out of around 10,000 entrants into the contest from around the world, which then had to be meticulously narrowed down to 20 proper participants, followed by just 6 finalists. These 6 finalists included Sweeper Woman, Recycle Man, Juggle Man, Valve Man, Cactus Man, and of course, Cleanser Man.

“In the end, we chose Cleanser Man because of the unique way the concept was brought together and the originality of the color design,” Ikuta explained.

These 6 finalists included Sweeper Woman, Recycle Man, Juggle Man, Valve Man, Cactus Man, and of course, Cleanser Man.

According to an official character summary sent by Capcom to Game Informer, Cleanser Man is a cleaning robot with an obsessive love of all things sparkling and pristine who will go to absurd lengths to scrub out any perceived imperfections, even if he ends up breaking things in the process. His stage will be set in a trash processing plant that’s flooding the world with garbage so people better appreciate his dedication to cleanliness.

The Tokyo Game Show 2026 Presentation revealed new details regarding both the second protagonist and the game's core mechanics.

Cleanser Man comes to us from a Brazilian artist who goes by “miya” on their X, formerly Twitter account. Upon the reveal that their design was chosen, they posted a screencap of Cleanser Man’s official render side-by-side with their concept art, adding the caption, “The attention to detail almost made me cry. It truly means a lot.”

Not only was the character-design contest a fun way to get people hyped up for Mega Man: Dual Override, not to mention showcase some really cool character-design work, it’s also an important revival of a long-forgotten Mega Man tradition.

Following the release of the original Mega Man game and prior to the release of Mega Man 2, Capcom held a similar contest for its Japanese players to decide the designs of that game’s eight Robot Master. This contest format would be repeated for every subsequent mainline Mega Man game up to and including Mega Man 8, resulting in many of the most notoriously-difficult Robot Masters, before being discontinued from Mega Man & Bass through Mega Man 11. While there was still some fan design encouragement, it was mostly relegated to spin-off series like Battle Network and Star Force.

While it’s only for a single Robot Master rather than a full set of eight, Cleanser Man is still the first fanmade Robot Master to make it into a mainline Mega Man title since 1996, with the added accolade that he’s the first Robot Master to come from a designer in South America. It’ll truly be an honor to shove my Buster Gun in his face.

Thomas Frank has been appointed as Crystal Palace’s new manager on a deal running until the summer of 2029, with the Danish coach now tasked with helping the south London club climb up the Premier League table.

Frank makes his return to management in England following a difficult eight-month spell leading Tottenham, having previously spent seven successful years in charge at Brentford.

Palace parted company with Pierre Sage last week after managing just two victories from 10 league fixtures, leaving the south London club bottom of the Premier League during the November international break. Frank will take charge for the first time away to Coventry on Saturday.

Frank said: "It’s an honour to sit here and be in charge of this fantastic football club. I’m really, really looking forward to it.

"Crystal Palace is an iconic club in England and in the Premier League. I will do everything I possibly can to help and support the players and everyone around the place, and I’m really looking forward to meeting them."

Paddy McCarthy has been retained as assistant manager alongside goalkeeping coach Andy Quy, who was previously part of Frank's staff at Brentford, with further backroom personnel to be confirmed.

Sage originally arrived in the summer to replace Oliver Glasner following his trophy-laden tenure at Selhurst Park, but the French coach immediately faced problems.

Joining after a successful period with Lens, Sage saw key Palace defenders Maxence Lacroix and Daniel Munoz leave before the side lost three of their opening four league games.

Although Palace advanced to the Carabao Cup quarter-finals, a solitary win in the Europa League and seven defeats across 10 Premier League matches prompted chairman Steve Parish to dismiss Sage on November 12.

Frank was picked as the ideal candidate to steer Palace away from a potential relegation scrap after overseeing multiple successful survival campaigns at Brentford.

The 53-year-old took the Bees into the top flight in 2021 before helping them establish themselves in the Premier League despite having a small budget compared to rival clubs.

However, Frank’s opportunity to succeed in a different environment at Tottenham turned out to be tough despite an encouraging opening period in north London.

Amid increasing supporter frustration and a stretch of two wins in 17 league matches, Frank was sacked on February 11. He holds the worst win record of any permanent Spurs manager in Premier League history, but now seeks to succeed at Palace.

Parish said: "I’m delighted to be able to welcome Thomas to Crystal Palace. He has a proven track record of both creating winning teams and improving players, making him the perfect fit for our club.

"I’m confident Thomas’ extensive experience in the Premier League will help us achieve our goals for the rest of the season."

Grammy Awards: All the Artists Who’ve Been Nominated in the Big 4 Categories in the Same Year

📅 Nov 16, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Remarkably, 13 of these 17 artists are women.

When the nominations for the 69th annual Grammy Awards were announced on Monday (Nov. 16), Ella Langley became the 17th artist to receive nominations in each of the so-called “Big Four” Grammy categories – album, record and song of the year plus best new artist – in the same year. Remarkably, 13 of those 17 artists are women. How to account for that? Grammy voters seem to like to give extra encouragement to talented young women artists.

Langley is the first country artist to be nominated in each of the Big Four categories since Bobbie Gentry in 1968, who was the first artist from any genre to achieve the feat.

Three times in recent years, two artists achieved the feat in the same year. Billie Eilish and Lizzo both achieved the feat in 2020. Olivia Rodrigo and FINNEAS both achieved it in 2022. Chappell Roan and Sabrina Carpenter both achieved it in 2025.

This has happened more often in recent years because the number of nominees in each of the Big Four categories has expanded. Until the Grammy ceremony held in 2019, there were generally five nominees in each of those categories. The upcoming 69th annual Grammy Awards, set for Sunday (Feb. 7), will mark the ninth year in a row there have been eight or more nominees in each of those categories. There were 10 at the ceremonies in 2022 and 2023, but the Recording Academy (mercifully) brought it back down to eight in 2024.

All but one of the 16 previous artists to receive nods in each of the Big Four categories had a spotlight performance slot on the Grammy telecast. The exception was FINNEAS, and even he was featured in sister Eilish’s performance of “Happier Than Ever” on the 2022 telecast.

Here’s a complete list of artists who have been nominated in each of the top four Grammy categories in the same year. The years shown are the years of the Grammy ceremonies.

Gentry, who was 25 at the time, was nominated for her debut album Ode to Billie Joe and the intriguing story song that was its title track. She won best new artist and in two other categories – best vocal performance, female and best contemporary female solo vocal performance. Gentry hasn’t won another Grammy since that night, though she was nominated for her 1970 album Fancy.

Cross, then 29, was nominated for his eponymous debut album and his hypnotic ballad “Sailing.” He won in all four categories and for best arrangement accompanying vocalist(s). Cross received three more nominations the following year for “Arthur’s Theme (Best That You Can Do),” though he hasn’t added to his trophy collection since his big night.

Lauper, then 31, was nominated for her first solo album, She’s So Unusual — and, in a unique occurrence, two different works for record and song of the year, respectively. The zesty “Girls Just Want to Have Fun” was nominated for record; the poignant “Time After Time,” which Lauper co-wrote with Rob Hyman, was nominated for song. She won best new artist. She won a second Grammy 29 years later – best musical theater album for Kinky Boots.

Chapman, then 24, was nominated for her eponymous debut album and her surprise hit, “Fast Car.” Chapman, the first Black artist to be nominated in each of the Big Four categories in one year, won best new artist and in two other categories – best pop vocal performance, female and best contemporary folk recording. She won a fourth Grammy eight years later for “Give Me One Reason.”

Carey, then 20, was nominated for her eponymous debut album and her sleek ballad “Vision of Love,” which she co-wrote with Ben Margulies. She won best new artist and best pop vocal performance, female. She has since won three additional Grammys.

Cole, then 29, was nominated for her debut album, This Fire, and her hit “Where Have All the Cowboys Gone?” She won best new artist. She had seven nominations that night, including producer of the year, non-classical, but hasn’t even been nominated since that night.

The singer, then 26, was nominated for her debut album, Acoustic Soul, and her single, “Video.” She co-wrote the song with Carlos “Six July” Broady, Reginald Harris and Shannon Sanders. India.Arie was the first artist to receive nods in each of the Big Four categories but not win in any of them. She went 0-7 that year (though she came back to win two Grammys the following year and two more in the years since then).

Winehouse, then 24, was nominated for her second (and final) studio album Back to Black and her retro-sounding hit “Rehab.” Winehouse, the first English artist to be nominated in each of the Big Four categories in the same year, won for record and song of the year, best new artist and in two other categories – best pop vocal album and best female pop vocal performance. Winehouse won a sixth Grammy four years later for “Body and Soul,” a duet with Tony Bennett. Winehouse died in 2011 at age 27.

The trio — the only group to be nominated in each of the Big Four categories in the same year — was nominated for its second (and still most recent) album, Some Nights, and its power ballad “We Are Young,” featuring Janelle Monáe. The trio, consisting of Jack Antonoff, Andrew Dost and Nate Ruess, co-wrote the smash with its producer, Jeff Bhasker. fun. won song of the year and best new artist. Antonoff has since won 12 additional Grammys.

Smith, then 22, was nominated for their debut album In the Lonely Hour and for the “Darkchild Version” of the smash hit “Stay with Me” (which Smith co-wrote with James Napier and William Phillips). Smith won record and song of the year, best new artist and best pop vocal album. Eight years later, they won a fifth Grammy for “Unholy,” a record-setting collab with Kim Petras.

Eilish, then 17, was nominated for her first full-length album, When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? and her quirky hit “bad guy” (which she co-wrote with her producer/brother, FINNEAS). Eilish was the youngest artist to receive nods in each of the Big Four categories in the same year. She won in all four categories, the first person to do so since Christopher Cross 39 years earlier. Eilish won a fifth Grammy that night, best pop vocal album, and has since won five additional Grammys.

Lizzo, then 31, was nominated for the deluxe edition of her third album, Cuz I Love You, and her genre-blending smash “Truth Hurts” (which she co-wrote with her producer Ricky Reed, Steven Cheung and Jesse Saint John). She lost in each of the Big Four categories to Eilish but still managed to win three awards that night – best pop solo performance (in which she managed to beat Eilish), best urban contemporary album and best traditional R&B performance. Three years later, she won record of the year for “About Damn Time.”

FINNEAS, then 24, was nominated as a writer and producer on Eilish’s sophomore album, Happier Than Ever, and its title track and also for his work on the deluxe “Triple Chucks” edition of Justin Bieber’s Justice. He was nominated for best new artist for work on his debut studio album Optimist and its lead single “What They’ll Say About Us.” He didn’t win in any of the Big Four categories that year, but not to worry: He has amassed 11 Grammys, one more even than his superstar sister.

Rodrigo, then 18, was nominated for her debut album Sour and its smash hit “Driver’s License.” She won for best new artist, as well as best pop vocal album and best pop solo performance.

Carpenter, then 25, received an album of the year nod for Short n’ Sweet, a record of the year nod for “Espresso” and a song of the year nod for “Please Please Please.” Short n’ Sweet was also nominated for best pop vocal album. “Espresso” was also nominated for best pop solo performance. Carpenter won in the latter two categories.

Roan, then 26, received an album of the year nod for her first full-length album, The Rise and Fall of a Midwest Princess, and record and song of the year nods for its breakout hit “Good Luck, Babe!.” The Rise and Fall… was also nominated for best pop vocal album. “Good Luck, Babe!” was also nominated for best pop solo performance.

Langley, 27, received an album of the year nod for her sophomore set, Dandelion, and record and song of the year nods for its megahit “Choosin’ Texas,” which set a new record for most weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard 200. Langley is also nominated for best country solo performance, best country song and best contemporary country album.

The simple and convenient Apple Maps feature every CarPlay user should know about

📅 Nov 16, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Let your friends know what time you'll be arriving.

For many iPhone users, Apple CarPlay has become synonymous with a quality driving experience, from playing their favorite tunes to navigating to their next destination. And although Apple continues to upgrade its popular infotainment system, one of its most useful features has been hidden in Apple Maps for years and is critical to letting your family, friends, and, if you are like me, enemies know when you will arrive at your destination.

I am, of course, referring to sharing your estimated time of arrival (ETA) through Apple Maps. Most users are probably familiar with Apple's Find My and location-sharing functions, but CarPlay's Share ETA feature is woefully underappreciated. Both simple to use and highly customizable, the feature is a godsend for worried parents and surprise parties everywhere.

To enable Share ETA, go to Settings > Apps > Maps. From here, scroll down to Share ETA and enable the feature. You can also enable location sharing in Messages, Contacts and Find My. You may also want to turn on Siri to make sharing an ETA more convenient. To do so, go to your iPhone's Settings, tap Siri and enable Turn on Siri.

Sharing your ETA is a simple way to improve your CarPlay experience. If you're using Siri, you can initiate a voice command when you're following Maps directions, either by holding the voice command button on your steering wheel or saying "Hey Siri" if your car supports voice activation. Next, say "share my ETA with (contact name)."

If you would rather not use Apple's voice assistant, you can share your ETA directly in Maps. After you've set off, tap the route card at the bottom of the screen, then Share ETA and choose a recipient. If the contact isn't on the list, you can find them under Open Contacts. To share with more than one person, tap Sharing with at the bottom of the screen, Open Contacts and select your intended recipients. You can also stop sharing your ETA by opening the route card again, tapping Sharing with, selecting Stop Sharing ETA and choosing the contact you want to stop sharing with.

You can also automatically share your ETA with someone by adding their contact to a pinned location. First, go to Maps > Places > Pinned. Next, tap the blue information button > Add Person. Next, tap the desired contact and confirm the selection. Now, whenever you select the pinned location in Apple Maps and tap Go, it will automatically share your ETA.

Backlash Over ‘Robots’ Taking Photos at Christmas Lights Event

A video of Christmas lights being switched on in Central London has provoked a debate about people’s picture-taking habits and the importance of living in the moment.

The video was shared by BBC London on November 4. It shows a sea of people, seemingly every single one of them with their phones aloft, filming in anticipation of the elaborate lights on Carnaby Street turning on and the fireworks that followed.

The comments were flooded with people disgusted that no one appeared to be taking in the moment, instead watching a real event that was unfolding right in front of them through the small screen of their phone.

“I’ve stopped filming most things like fireworks and concerts when I realized I haven’t watched a single one of them back,” says Sufian Ahmed. “It’s so much about being in the moment. Experiencing it with the person you’re next to. You lose all that when watching it through a narrow screen.”

Many compared it to an episode of the Black Mirror TV show, while others called out the people for being “robots.” Others pointed out the hypocrisy of social media users complaining about the video while watching it on their phone.

Previous studies have found that taking a photo of an event affects human memory. A 2018 psychological study suggested that shooting photos or videos gives people a false sense that they preserved a moment, when in actual fact it’s the camera that did it for them.

In 2013, researchers asked one group of people to tour an art museum without taking photos, while another group was assigned to take photos. Those who took photos were significantly worse at identifying items in the museum and recalled fewer details about the items they did remember than those who didn’t take photos.

Dr. Kaitlyn Regehr of University College London and the University of Toronto says public attitudes toward smartphones and technology are shifting.

“This is not the first year that people have attended events and watched those events through screens,” the smartphone academic tells the BBC.

“But what’s significant is that this year there’s a lot of critical public discourse happening. And that, I think, is really, really positive.

Dr. Regehr says the backlash could be down to “our discomfort with the amount of influence that big tech is playing on our lives.”

On Monday, the Supreme Court published its order list, revealing that it had denied certiorari in Custodia's case against the Federal Reserve Board.

The Supreme Court has said it would not take up a Custodia Bank's challenge to secure a Federal Reserve master account, denied its petitions for a writ of certiorari.

On Monday, the Supreme Court published its order list, revealing that it had denied certiorari in Custodia's case against the Federal Reserve Board. Custodia's legal journey has spanned over the past six years to try to obtain a master account, which allows institutions direct access to the Fed's payment systems and provides the most direct access to the U.S. money supply available to financial institutions. Those without master accounts are often forced to rely on partner banks with master accounts to provide services.

Custodia Bank, a Wyoming-chartered crypto-focused firm founded by Wall Street veteran Caitlin Long, began its master account quest in 2020 when it applied for that account in Kansas City. The Kansas City Fed then denied its application in January 2023, citing concerns about the bank's crypto-related business model. Custodia sued the Kansas City Fed in June 2022, initially over a 19-month processing delay and later arguing that federal law requires access for eligible institutions.

In March, an appeals court denied Custodia's petition for rehearing en banc, leaving intact a previous ruling that the Fed has discretion to approve or deny a master account for eligible institutions.

Custodia then petitioned for the high court to take its case up in July. The Supreme Court's decision to not take up the bank's challenged now leaves intact the previous decision. Custodia did not immediately respond to a request for comment from The Block.

Over the past year, attention has since shifted to an idea first floated by Fed Governor Christopher Waller for a "skinny master account." As innovations have evolved, the Fed said it may be time to offer a tailored approach while trying to mitigate risks, but some lawmakers have pushed back, questioning the safety and soundness of the Fed's payment rails.

The Fed asked for comments on the proposed slimmed-down master account last year and said it would not allow for interest on balances and could not access the discount window to borrow funds, among other limits. Comment letters from crypto advocates called the proposal "overdue" and also asked for some revisions. Community banks, however, warned that novel financial institutions were not subject to the same "robust regulatory compliance regime."

Custodia had previously told the Supreme Court that a limited purpose account was not sufficient, in part because it would mean that interest couldn't be obtained on the account's closing balance, Bloomberg reported.

Crypto exchange Kraken's banking division became the first crypto company to obtain a master account in March.
