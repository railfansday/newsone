Overcompensating follows the chaotic college journey of Benny, who’s a closeted former football player and homecoming king from Idaho. Along with his best friend and older sister, he must navigate college at Yates University while trying to come to terms with his identity. Overcompensating season 1 ends with Benny getting accidentally outed by his best friend, shattering the trust within the core group. The next season will feature Benny in his second semester of freshman year, as he and his friends juggle new lovers, cringey encounters, sexual awakenings, and the riskiest of all: collegiate sports.

Overcompensating was created, written, and executive-produced by Benito Skinner, who based the show on his own college experiences. CNC Aluminum Parts: What Actually Changes Between 200 Pieces and 10,000

📅 Nov 10, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Buyers rarely ask us the most useful question about a CNC aluminum order. They ask for a unit price at 10,000 pieces. What they should ask is: what changes between 200 pieces and 10,000 pieces? The answer is almost everything - fixtures, inspection, even which machines run the job. Below is how it actually works on our floor in Shenzhen, with photos from the shop.

At 200-500 pieces, your order is still small enough that per-part setup dominates cost, and large enough that re-indicating every blank is madness. This is the band where zero-point pallet systems earn their keep: parts move between machines without re-datum, and the price drops toward machine-hour rates. If a shop quotes your 300-piece aluminum order at nearly ten times its 30-piece price, it has no pallet system - that is the tell.

Aluminum makes this band easier in one specific way: 6061 and 7075 cut fast and predictably, so the machine-hour component is low. What you are really paying for at a few hundred pieces is engineering judgment - which faces to datum, where the part will move after stress release, how the anodizing mask affects the tolerance stack.

Somewhere past a few thousand pieces, the honest answer is that the job stops being a machining problem and becomes a process-design problem. Dedicated fixtures. Tool-life tracking. In-process gauging instead of final inspection on every piece. And - this is the part most quotes hide - some geometry should migrate off the milling center entirely: turned features go to lathes, and the unit price follows the cheapest process that still holds the print.

Our floor runs 70 Tsugami Swiss-type lathes alongside the milling centers, around 5 million pieces per month across the group. When a buyer's aluminum housing grows from hundreds to tens of thousands, the machining moves to the right machine class instead of the price doubling.

The enclosure above is the kind of part that lives in the few-hundred-pieces band: aluminum, multiple milled faces, tight fit against an off-the-shelf component. At this size, cosmetic consistency (anodizing color match across a batch) matters as much as dimensional tolerance - ask your shop how they control dye lots before you order 300 of them.

One question worth asking any shop at the 10,000-piece level: who owns the dedicated fixtures after the first run? If the answer is vague, your second-order price is negotiable in the wrong direction.

We keep fuller breakdowns of each band on our site - the small-batch aluminum work is here: Small Batch Aluminum Housing CNC, and the volume side is here: CNC Batch Production and Mass CNC Production.

퀘스트 2·프로 '기능 개발 종료'가 던지는 질문: 공간 컴퓨팅 시대, 소프트웨어는 어떻게 살아남을까?

📅 Nov 10, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Engadget 보도로 메타가 퀘스트 2와 퀘스트 프로의 기능 개발을 중단한다는 소식이 전해졌다. 내년 이후 새로운 OS 기능이나 하드웨어 최적화 업데이트는 기대하기 어렵지만, 앱 스토어는 계속 운영된다. 기존 기기 보유자에게는 아쉬운 소식이자, 동시에 개발자와 연구자에게 던져진 묵직한 질문이다: 하드웨어 수명주기가 끝나도 소프트웨어 생태계는 어떻게 지속 가능한가?

이 질문의 답을 찾아가는 여정에서, 나는 Seven Sportz가 만든 NexoPeek이라는 VR 분자생물학 실험실을 다시 마주쳤다.

NexoPeek의 핵심 가치는 하드웨어 스펙에 의존하지 않는다. 메타 퀘스트 2, 프로, 3 모두에서 단일 빌드로 동작하며, 계정 귀속 라이브러리로 차세대 기기로 넘어가도 구매 내역은 그대로 따라온다. 중요한 건 ‘단백질 구조를 평면 모니터가 아닌 공간에서 이해한다’는 패러다임 자체다.

6대 분야 12편의 내레이션 투어는 RCSB PDB 실구조 데이터를 바탕으로 한다. 헤모글로빈의 산소 결합, ATP 합성, 해당과정, 트립신의 단백질 분해, 아스피린의 COX 저해, 카페인과 페니실린, CRISPR‑Cas9, 야마나카 인자, 플라스틱 분해 효소까지 — 교과서 속 2D 도식이 내 손끝에서 3D 공간으로 펼쳐진다.

교육용 투어를 넘어, NexoPeek은 전문가급 PDB 뷰어이자 분석 도구다.

이 모든 기능이 Unity 2022 LTS + URP 기반 단일 코드베이스에서 빌드되며, OpenXR 런타임을 통해 퀘스트 2/프로/3 모두에서 네이티브 성능을 낸다. 내부 벤치마크(Quest 2, 90 Hz)에서는 평균 72 fps, 메모리 1.2 GB 이하를 유지한다.

Room View 토글 한 번으로 3D 분자 구조가 실제 물리 공간에 떠 오른다. 벽 메뉴는 그대로 조작 가능하니, 책상 위 페이퍼와 나란히 단백질 포켓을 들여다보며 회의를 할 수 있다. 패스스루 카메라 피드를 실시간으로 합성하는 셰이더는 단일 패스로 구현돼 프레임 드랍 없이 60 fps 이상을 보장한다.

NexoPeek은 교육·연구 시각화 소프트웨어다. 의료 조언·진단·치료가 아니며, 포켓 예측과 구조 정렬은 검증된 과학 파이프라인과 전문가 판단을 보조할 뿐 대체하지 않는다. 연구 결과는 1차 문헌과 검증된 방법론으로 재확인해야 한다.

지난 겨울, 연구실 책상 앞에서 퀘스트 2를 꺼내 들었다. 펌웨어 업데이트 알림이 더 이상 뜨지 않는다는 사실을 알았을 때, 마음이 조금 무거웠다. 하지만 NexoPeek을 실행하자마자, 헤모글로빈의 산소 결합이 내 손바닥 위에서 숨을 쉬는 걸 보았다. 2D 논문 그림으로는 결코 느낄 수 없던 공간적 거리감이, 내 손가락 끝에서 앵스트롬 단위로 측정됐다.

그날 밤, 나는 Foldseek 파이프라인으로 CRISPR‑Cas9 변이체와 야생형을 슈퍼임포즈해 도메인 보존성을 확인하고, PrankWeb으로 드러거블 포켓을 예측했다. 결과는 논문 리뷰어에게 보낼 보충 자료로 바로 쓰였다. 퀘스트 2의 하드웨어가 더 이상 업데이트되지 않아도, 소프트웨어 스택은 여전히 살아 있고, 내 연구도 계속된다는 확신이 들었다.

하드웨어 세대가 바뀌어도 ‘분자 안으로 들어간다’는 몰입적 경험의 본질은 변하지 않는다. 지금 메타 퀘스트 스토어에서 NexoPeek을 다운로드해 직접 확인해보자: https://www.meta.com/experiences/nexopeek/1453797551140347/

Reds' Sal Stewart 'just getting started' with NL Rookie of Year award

📅 Nov 10, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

LAS VEGAS — Sal Stewart had no sooner accepted the National League Rookie of the Year award than he embarked on the task of getting the Cincinnati Reds back to the playoffs next year.

Well, he planned to embark on that once the party in Miami with dozens of friends and family concludes at some point this week.

"Just a kid from Miami living his dream!" he said at the end of a video conference with Cincinnati media after becoming the ninth player in franchise history to win the award.

"You might wake up at 8. a.m., and I'm still gonna be out partying. I'll be enjoying it."

No matter how long that party might last, no matter how long a labor lockout might last once MLB's labor agreement expires Dec. 1, Stewart has already fixed his sights firmly on 2027 and helping lead the Reds from last place back to the postseason he experienced a month into his big-league career in 2025.

"My career is just getting started. I promise you that," he said. "I promise you this is just a steppingstone, and there's more to come.

"This season didn't go the way we wanted," he added, "but I feel like if I want to be the player that I want to be for this organization, (this award) is a good steppingstone to show everybody (coming up), 'Hey, I'm willing to do it. Everyone that comes up here better be willing to do it, to put your life on the line for your team.'

"I feel that will go a long way, and I feel our season will turn out better next year."

Stewart, 22, hit 32 home runs and drove in 112 runs in 2026, earning an All-Star selection and the Reds' first Rookie of the Year award since Jonathan India in 2021 – winning 20 of 30 first-place votes and beating second-place Carson Benge (seven first-place votes) of the Mets 170 points to 107. JJ Wetherholt (three) of the Cardinals was third with 99 points.

The award was a goal Stewart set for himself long before getting to spring training, even with a loaded field of impact rookies who figured to be in play across the league.

"Because so many people doubted me. So many people didn't think I could," he said. "I love that. My whole life I've been doubted. Even when I got drafted, I had people on MLB Network saying I was a bad decision. 'He didn't have a hit ability.' 'His power needed to come.' 'His defense was bad.'

"I don't forget those things," he said. "I keep all the receipts for all the people that talk. I just wanted to prove those people wrong."

The ultra competitive Stewart played all season with a level of confidence and apparent chip on his shoulder that belied his age and experience level. It especially showed up in high-leverage moments, with men in scoring position and in close-and-late game situations.

It's what led to RBI totals that led the league much of the season before Stewart eventually finished four behind the MLB leader.

Beyond the significance of being one of just seven players to play all 162 games this season, he preaches the value of an RBI stat that has fallen out of favor in this era of advanced stats and analytics – an appreciation instilled by his baseball mentors growing up and that boils down to situational hitting to try to win games.

"Whether it's a ground ball to shortstop with the infield back or whether it's a sac fly," he said. "You can't win a game if you don't drive in runs at the end of the day."

The Reds didn't have enough runs or enough wins regardless in this case. Which already seems to be Stewart's focus with the prestigious award in his back pocket.

He says he wants to get with team officials to find out what their plan is for him positionally next year – back to his original third base or staying primarily at first – then working this winter on getting better there.

And performing again, and helping lead others, to get back to the playoffs.

"I really want to win for this city," he said. "This award's great, but at the end of the day I don't like sitting down and watching the playoffs. I want to be playing in them. And that's something I've got to figure out a way to help my team do that.

"I know what we're capable of," he added. "We have the right guy at the helm (in manager Terry Francona). Tito knows what the expectation is. And he's the first one to make sure I'm living up to it, and if I'm not he's the first one calling me into his office making sure I'm living up to it. We know what it takes. We know what the expectation is. Losing's not fun, man. We do this to win."

PTT has rebounded before. After a rough debut loss in the promotion, he turned things around in style and strung together impressive wins to establish himself as a name to watch in the division. Now he looks to show the same resilience against a dangerous finisher in Rachid and start a new run.

WEST LAFAYETTE − At last, Purdue basketball fans can breathe a sigh of relief.

It took huge late run to blow the game open, but the Boilermakers left Mackey Arena with their first victory.

A lineup change that included Jack Benter and Antione West Jr. among the starting helped pilot Purdue's 76-59 win over Illinois State.

Purdue (1-2) led 50-48 midway through the second half before landing the knockout blow it needed after losses to Gonzaga and Valparaiso to open the season while also snapping out of its 3-point shooting funk.

After combining to shoot just 20.4% from 3 in the first two games, the Boilers made 8 of 19 against the Redbirds (1-2).

Despite a hot shooting first half, Purdue couldn't separate itself from the Redbirds.

Illinois State had a chance to cut the lead to two Louie Semona II's 3-pointer was off and Enzo Shahrvin pulled down the rebound. Purdue called timeout to set up a final play. Antione West Jr. ended up making a stepback 3-pointer from the right wing to give the Boilermakers a 36-28 halftime lead.
