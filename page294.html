Quartz watches will only require the replacement of the battery every few years, which is a simple task and costs fairly little.

Backed by the motion of your wrist—no battery required. The gears that make up the movement wind themselves when the watch moves. They'll also require consistent maintenance and servicing to keep them optimally running. Automatic watches typically cost more than most quartz watches, but there are many great options that are affordable these days.

Water resistance tells you how well your watch can handle moisture. There are two equivalent measurements you will typically find when it comes to this rating. Meters (as in depth) is the first and atmosphere (designated as ATM and measures the pressure of every 10 meters. So the first rating is 30m, which is rated at 3 ATM. This is splash-proof, which means your watch can handle occasional hand-washing or light rain, but don't submerge it. 50m (5 ATM) is good for shallow water activities like light swimming, but no diving. 100m (10 ATM) can handle swimming, snorkeling, and most water sports. 200m+ (20 ATM) is built for diving and serious water activities. Watches like the Omega Seamaster are built specifically for this sort of pressure.

A chronograph is a type of complication added to the watch movement that essentially gives the watch a stopwatch functionality. It's great for timing events, workouts, or even laps around a track. You'll typically see sub dials for seconds, minutes, and sometimes hours. While it adds functionality, it also gives the watch a sporty, complex look. While some people appreciate the natural look of a shadowy under-eye, many of us hope to find a solution—which is where the best eye cream for dark circles comes in. “Not all eye creams are created equal, because not all eye problems are equal,” says board-certified dermatologist Dr. Shereene Idriss. “When a person is looking to treat dark circles, the question is: Why do you have dark circles?”

The answer is different for everyone. For some people, it’s pigment, which can be genetic or the result of tiredness. For others, the skin under the eyes is so thin that the blood vessels underneath show through as a bluish or purple cast. If you’re experiencing puffiness, that can cast its own shadow, especially in the morning. Finally, as we get older, a little volume loss where the lower lid meets the cheek can make the whole area look darker than it really is.

That’s why when it comes to your eye cream, the ingredient list matters. Vitamin C, niacinamide and licorice root are good picks for pigment. Formulas that include retinol and peptides help thin skin look firmer and less see-through over time, and caffeine is every skin expert’s go-to for puffiness. Hollowing is the one thing a cream can’t fix, but the right formula can still make the skin around it look smoother and brighter.

Do eye creams actually get rid of dark circles?

Though eye creams can soften the appearance of dark circles, they’re (alas) not a one-swipe magic wand. “Eye creams can improve the quality of the skin around your eyes, but they can’t fix every undereye concern,” says Brauer. “Eye creams mainly help to moisturize and protect the skin around your eyes—one of the most delicate areas of skin on your face. Also called mocha chocolate, the rich, glossy shade was first popularized by celebrity colorist Jenna Perry, whose multidimensional brunette wizardry has fans like Bella Hadid and Kaia Gerber. Since then, the smooth and chocolaty hue—which combines darker espresso tones with lighter, milkier brunette shades—has become popular with a much wider section of the A-list, with Hailey Bieber and Daisy Edgar-Jones among the latest devotees.

“It reminds me of a chocolate mousse or a rich chocolate cake,” Perry tells Vogue. “That’s where the name came from. According to Stephen Buller and Anita Rice, founders of Buller and Rice salon, these cacao tones feel luxurious for winter, particularly when the color isn’t completely flat. The key? Having a varied tone. “This is important throughout the lengths and ends so it reflects light and feels expensive rather than looking like one solid block of dark color,” they say. “This also gives clients who have had years of balayage an opportunity to go darker without feeling as though they’re simply covering everything up.”

Celebrity hairstylist Lorraine Dublin says that these indulgent, chocolaty shades look especially beautiful on textured and curly hair: “Think deep espresso brown with subtle ribbons of mocha, creating a rich, multidimensional finish,” she previously told Vogue. “It works beautifully on textured hair because the tonal variations catch the light, highlighting the shape and movement of curls, coils, and kinks.” She recommends asking your colorist for a “rich espresso base” with “delicate mocha or cinnamon brown accents,” which will impart a soft, seamless color melt.

Another important part of mastering the mocha chocolate look is keeping hair strong and shiny. “What makes this hair color so incredible from the start is having healthy hair,” explains Perry, who adds that preserving pigments with a color-safe shampoo and conditioner is key. Try Matrix Total Results Brass Off Shampoo to nix brassy or red tones. Engineered to contain flames and burn fuel more completely, smokeless fire pits deliver ambient warmth with a lot less smoke in your eyes than a traditional campfire. Whether you want a smokeless fire pit to heat your outdoor space or be more of an ambient seasonal moment is an important consideration. Burning pellets instead of wood was the closest we got to a truly smokeless fire during our testing. Material affects how long your new pit will look the way it does on arrival. Corten-steel usually costs more but forms a protective rust-colored patina that looks attractively weathered over time. Painted or powder-coated steel usually costs less, but can scratch and chip with repeat use.

Whatever finish you choose, keeping your smokeless fire pit empty of ash and covered when it’s not in use will help extend its life. Pure Titans resemble humans but with varying levels of enlarged forms. The majority of the observed Titans, both in the manga and the anime, belong to this category, their overarching feature being their inability to form and process thoughts.

Despite being made similarly to other Titan types, Pure Titans are mindless and tend to attack without discrimination. Eren Yaeger would later improve the Attack Titan’s metrics by drinking the Armor serum that allowed him to harden the Titan’s skin during pivotal moments.

To find the best dual-fuel generator for your home, campground, or worksite, consider the factors listed below, including fuel efficiency, fuel tank capacity, power output, and control panel configuration.

Dual-fuel generators have an isolated gasoline tank with a fuel capacity that ranges from as small as 2 gallons to more than 10 gallons. The larger the fuel tank on the generator, the longer the generator can provide gasoline-based power. But, as tank size increases, so does the size of the generator.

Fuel capacity does not affect how much propane you can use with your dual-fuel generator. Propane gas is stored under pressure in canisters, which attach to the generator with a hose and pressure regulator for direct fuel consumption.

The power output on dual-fuel generators is measured in watts. Manufacturers’ specs include peak power output and running power output. Peak power output refers to the maximum number of watts the generator can provide at any one time. Running power output equals the average number of watts produced during use.

Dual-fuel generators generally produce a power output from 1,000 watts to over 12,000 watts, depending on their size. Gasoline produces a higher wattage than propane, so many manufacturers of dual-fuel technology break wattage into propane peak and running outputs and gasoline peak and running outputs.

For greater power output needs, users may need to consider a standby generator rather than a portable dual-fuel option.

You can start your dual-fuel generator with an electric start switch or a recoil cord.

Many dual-fuel generators produce 50 to 90 dB of noise, depending on fuel type (gasoline runs louder than propane) and power output. The most common range is between 65 dB and 75 dB, comparable to the sound of normal conversation.

The quietest dual-fuel generators combine dual-fuel technology and inverter technology to create a dual-fuel inverter generator.

Dual-fuel generators are typically large and heavy, with hoses or tanks that make them difficult to carry and move. Thus, some manufacturers include wheel kits for tilting the generator back and rolling it to the desired location.

You may also be able to purchase a wheel kit separately to add to your existing generator or replace an older wheel kit that doesn’t work well anymore. Either way, a wheel kit makes transporting a portable dual-fuel generator much easier.

Lift hook bars are handy on jobsites, allowing workers to attach the generator to a hook-and-pulley system and raise or lower it instead of carrying it up or down stairs or ladders. In industrial settings, this feature helps raise the generator onto a walkway or platform.

A lift hook bar will increase the generator’s weight (and likely the price), so don’t look for a generator with this feature unless you truly plan to use it.

Most dual-fuel generators are between 22 inches long by 22 inches wide by 22 inches high and 35 inches long by 30 inches wide by 30 inches high. They typically weigh between 100 and 250 pounds.

If you plan to move the generator frequently, figure size and weight prominently into your decision. Make sure the model you choose fits into your vehicle so you can transport it as needed. Remember, however, that larger, heavier motors are also the most powerful. You’ll want to find the right balance of size, weight, and power to meet your needs.

Gasoline and propane each have their pros and cons as fuel options. Gasoline is easier to find, but propane costs less. Propane is a better fuel source in hot summer weather, while gas burns better in the cold. A dual-fuel generator lets you choose one or the other based on immediate circumstances.

Using both fuel sources, one after the other, greatly increases a generator’s runtime. Users can run to the gas station to refuel while the generator keeps providing power on propane.

Before buying a dual-fuel generator, consider the answers to the following frequently asked questions.

A dual-fuel generator operates like a single-fuel generator, except it can burn propane and gasoline to produce electrical energy. It converts mechanical energy produced by the burning fuels to force the movement of electrical charges and generate electricity.

While some single-fuel generators can be adapted for use with natural gas, this isn’t the case with dual-fuel generators. Unless specially designed, dual-fuel generators do not run on natural gas and cannot be adapted for this purpose.

Propane gas is less expensive than gasoline, nearly impossible to spill, and burns cleaner, resulting in less wear on the engine over time. Running a dual-fuel generator on propane will save money but slightly decrease the machine’s peak and running watts.

Runtime is determined by fuel efficiency and fuel tank capacity. On average, a standard dual-fuel generator runs about 8 to 10 hours on propane.
