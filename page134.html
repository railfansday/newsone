Newcastle United are reportedly preparing to compete with Liverpool and several other Premier League clubs for the signature of highly-rated Lens defender Ismaelo Ganiou ahead of next summer’s transfer window.

TL;DR: Starbucks Red Cup Day 2026 takes place on Nov. 17. To qualify for your free cup, buy any handcrafted holiday or seasonal beverage (hot, iced, or blended) in any size.

Starbucks has been very busy lately. They launched their holiday menu on Nov. 5, quickly followed by a fresh lineup of viral Bearista cups.

That's obviously very exciting, but it's not the main draw for seasoned coffee lovers and cup collectors. We're all thrilled about the Peppermint Mocha, but Red Cup Day reigns supreme. This is arguably the biggest day on the coffee lovers' calendar. Starbucks hands out a free, limited-edition red reusable cup with every qualifying handcrafted holiday beverage order on this special day. And you guessed it: fans and collectors go wild.

Starbucks has officially confirmed the date for Red Cup Day 2026: You can score a free reusable holiday cup on Nov. 17.

Google adds 66 regional dialects to Gemini

📅 Nov 16, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The feature was teased during Google I/O earlier this year.

One of the less visible risks of conversational AI is its flattening effect on a culture's unique dialect and accent. Humanity speaks with a thousand voices, but assistants often just respond in a nicely generic transatlantic monotone. Now, Google is doing something about it by adding 66 regional dialects to Gemini to help it better relate to the people using it. The feature was first teased at Google I/O earlier this year, and starting today, the Android and iOS apps will be able to speak to users in a variety of voices from different countries.

For instance, UK users can set Gemini to speak in a Geordie, Scouse and Brummie accent to better engage with people from Tyneside, Merseyside and Birmingham. The list also includes Scots, Welsh, Northern Irish and Multicultural London English, among many others. And it's not just the UK that gets this treatment, with tailored dialects for India, Brazil, Indonesia and Korea also in the mix.

Google says it worked with native speakers to ensure the voices were authentic and clear, rather than exaggerated and stereotypical. It added that Gemini isn't going to suddenly fill its responses with "street slang," but instead focus on sounding more "like a local." Given how much pride people put into their accent as a signifier of their heritage, it'll be very interesting to see how well these voices are received by the people intended to use them.

To activate the voices, you'll need to access the voice settings in the Gemini app or tap the three-dot menu. Google suggests a good way to test their authenticity is to ask for a recommendation for local food to better hear the "warmth and rhythm of each voice."

Instagram now lets creators add Amazon affiliate links in reels and feed posts

📅 Nov 16, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Instagram announced on Monday that it’s launching an integration that will give creators a new way to earn by adding Amazon affiliate links directly in reels and feed posts. Creators can add up to 30 products per reel or 20 per feed post, which makes it possible for them to earn from multiple products through one piece of content.

While creators have long shared Amazon affiliate links through stories, DMs, and their bios, the new integration brings them to new parts of the app. The Meta-owned company told TechCrunch in an email that it has seen product links directly on creators’ reels and posts draw about twice as many clicks as the links in their bio.

By allowing creators to embed multiple product links directly in Reels and feed posts, the platform is expanding their opportunities to monetize content, while also giving them more flexibility in how they share recommendations. The change also strengthens the platform’s appeal to creators, as it continues to compete with rivals like TikTok and YouTube for their attention and content.

Instagram says adding products and affiliate links is free, and that it doesn’t take a commission on purchases or charge transaction fees.

Eligible creators can now connect their Instagram and Amazon Influencer accounts through the Monetization section of their Professional Dashboard. Once connected, Instagram automatically adds affiliate links when creators tag Amazon products in their content, with links directing viewers to the Amazon Shopping app. Connected affiliates also unlock image previews for their links, making it easier for viewers to recognize the products.

When creators connect their Amazon Associates account to Instagram, affiliate attribution is automatically applied to any Amazon product they add, even when they paste a regular product link, which wasn’t the case before, as they were required to add an affiliate URL.

Instagram says creators can also see each product’s estimated commission rate before adding it to a post or reel, with commissions continuing to be paid out by Amazon.

The new integration marks another step in Instagram’s efforts to expand its monetization tools while also making affiliate marketing more accessible for creators on its platform.

Instagram will let creators add Amazon affiliate links directly to their posts and Reels

📅 Nov 16, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

The app will become even more of a shopping mall, just in time for Black Friday.

Meta has a new gift for Instagram creators just in time for Black Friday and the holiday shopping season. The company is making it easier for influencers to promote Amazon affiliate products directly in their posts without having to rely on a link in bio service.

US creators who are also in Amazon's influencer program will now be able to add dozens of affiliate links directly to their Reels and Instagram posts, Meta announced. The links will take viewers directly to the Amazon app and creators will earn their usual commission from any purchase made via their content.

Instagram has enabled product tagging and shoppable posts for some time. But the company previously required that tagged products in Reels already exist in Meta's commerce catalog. Now, creators can instead link their Amazon account directly to Instagram and add up to 30 products in a single video (there's a 20-product limit for feed posts). The Instagram app will also show creators what commission to expect for each item.

For creators, the timing of the new feature is particularly good. Commission from affiliate links is a central part of how many Instagram influencers earn money from their accounts. And posts with products linked directly can generate twice as many clicks as bio links, according to Meta. And while the company won't take a cut of creators' commissions, these links are no doubt a valuable source of data to Meta about what their users are interested in.

The update is the latest way Meta has made it easier for Instagram influencers to direct users off-platform. The company has also started allowing creators to add clickable links to captions and Reels if they pay for certain Meta One subscription plans. Those paid tiers also come with expanded link pages. Up to now, the company's restrictions on link-sharing have led many creators to depend on separate "link in bio" services. Now, it seems Meta is increasingly interested in offering those tools to creators directly.

For now, Amazon affiliate linking is only available to creators in the United States who have a professional account on Instagram and are 18 or older. The company hasn't said if it plans to expand the feature to creators in more countries.

OpenAI releases a new image classifier and ChatGPT warnings to combat CSAM

📅 Nov 16, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

OpenAI today announced new tools to combat child sexual abuse material (CSAM) amid growing safety concerns around the use of AI tools. These features include a new image classifier to detect CSAM, warnings and temporary blocks to prevent users from creating or accessing content that sexualizes children, a new guideline for developers, and a set of tools for law enforcement.

The company unveiled a new image classifier that can detect CSAM images, including the ones generated by AI. OpenAI is giving select companies access to its classifier through its API at no cost. The tool looks for visual signs of abuse in images not seen before and assigns a score that can give an indicative direction to reviewers about dealing with CSAM.

OpenAI said that it developed this classifier with the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC), and it is working with Tech Coalition and ROOST to share access to the tool. In its early testing with partners, the AI company found that the classifier flags 48% fewer images while finding the same number of abusive images, reducing the work of reviewers. Plus, images flagged by the classifier are twice as likely to be abusive images.

What’s more, the company is adding new warning screens in ChatGPT to prevent users from accessing material that sexualizes children. ChatGPT will show an alert to users attempting such prompts, direct users towards specialized support to stop harmful behaviour, and also suspend the account for 24 hours as a warning. The app already blocks CSAM content and requests related to it. It said that repeated offences could lead to the account being removed permanently.

OpenAI noted that it formed the message and guidelines around this behavior with organizations and researchers, including Protect Children, the Lucy Faithfull Foundation, and Joel Scanlan at the University of Tasmania, who independently evaluated a trial of deterrent messages.

The company is also publishing guidelines for developers using OpenAI tools to prevent, detect, and help users report CSAM content. It said that in early testing, the guidelines have helped a number of developers adopt child safety tooling and register to report CSAM to NCMEC. OpenAI is also equipping law enforcement dealing with child safety with AI-powered features like dedicated plugins, investigative tools, and complex data analysis, with access managed by program administrators. It is also providing training to investigators along with a field guide on how to use AI.

OpenAI has had several lawsuits against the company blaming its technology for harming users, especially children. Saftey-focused nonprofit Common Sense Media labeled OpenAI’s tech as “unacceptable risk” for teens. Other AI chatbots — specifically Grok — have been used to create nonconsensual sexualized imagery, including of children.

The AI startup had made changes in the last 12 months to its technology including releasing a safety blueprint, updating content guidelines to bar inappropritate content, retuning its model to prevent them from generating harmful content, and releasing a special version of ChatGPT for teens.

La nostra idea era quella di prenderlo per quella squadra un anno prima, ma non siamo riusciti a trovare un accordo con l'Hellas Verona. Volevano tenerlo e hanno deciso di mandarlo in prestito in Serie B, al Catanzaro Ovviamente lo abbiamo seguito molto attentamente anche lì, in Serie B, e lì è letteralmente esploso nei primi sei mesi. Ci siamo detti che aveva superato talmente bene il salto dalla Primavera e dalla panchina del Verona alla Serie B che a quel punto bisognava muoversi".

Come hai convinto il Club a puntare su di lui? "Convincere il club non è stato così difficile, perché lavoravamo su Cisse già da diversi anni. Il club lo conosceva, ne avevamo parlato, io stesso ne avevo parlato con l'amministratore delegato e con i diversi allenatori che si erano succeduti. Avevamo uno storico abbastanza importante delle sue partite e delle sue prestazioni. Insomma, conoscevamo già abbastanza bene il dossier. La difficoltà, a volte, è il tempismo. Eravamo nella finestra di mercato invernale e bisognava fare in fretta. Perché altrimenti il PSV Eindhoven lo avrebbe preso direttamente. E invece di pagare tra i 7 e gli 8 milioni di euro al Verona, se fosse andato al PSV e tu avessi voluto acquistarlo successivamente, magari dopo aver giocato in Champions League, il prezzo sarebbe salito a 30 o 40 milioni di euro. A volte bisogna accelerare un po' le cose. Ma all'interno del club lo conoscevano già tutti, e questo è stato positivo".

Sugli aspetti più sorprendenti di Cisse: "Tutto quello che riguarda la conduzione della palla, i cambi di direzione... Soprattutto quando era più giovane, riusciva a fare tantissima differenza in questo modo. Ma anche la capacità di utilizzare entrambi i piedi. È sempre più difficile trovare giocatori di questo tipo. È capace di giocare da esterno, ma anche da numero 10, da trequartista, come si dice in Italia, oppure da seconda punta. Quindi deve avere tante soluzioni a disposizione. E in Serie B gli avversari gli mettevano addosso una pressione enorme. Non è passato da un grande club europeo. All'inizio ha giocato in società più piccole. Ed è proprio questo, secondo me, che gli ha permesso di trovare soluzioni da solo e di crescere attraverso le difficoltà. Nessuno gli ha regalato niente, non ha avuto nulla di facile".

Amorim e la crescita di Cisse: "Se l'allenatore è in sintonia con il club e accetta di far giocare questi giovani, è perfetto per la società. A volte, come club, bisogna assolutamente dirsi: questo giocatore dobbiamo prenderlo. Poi vedremo cosa succederà, magari all'allenatore non piacerà, ma intanto avere quel giocatore sotto contratto, all'interno del club, è fondamentale. E bisogna vederlo il prima possibile. Amorim, quando era al Manchester United, aveva fatto una cosa interessante con Dorgu. Inizialmente lo impiegava come terzino sinistro, naturalmente, ma poi lo aveva schierato anche da ala destra in Premier League. Che abbia funzionato o meno, questo allenatore non ha avuto paura di fare scelte del genere e di cambiare posizione ai giocatori".

Come avete convinto il giocatore a scegliere il Milan invece del PSV? Gli avete promesso qualcosa di specifico? "Innanzitutto, noi non promettiamo niente. Penso che un club come il PSV Eindhoven possa dire a un giocatore: "Vieni qui e sarai titolare". Noi non potevamo dirglielo. La fortuna che abbiamo avuto è che, prima di tutto, ci chiamiamo AC Milan. E ti posso assicurare che questo rende le cose abbastanza facili. E soprattutto lui è italiano. Quindi, per un giocatore italiano, arrivare al Milan rappresenta una consacrazione. Per quanto il PSV Eindhoven possa promettergli di giocare titolare tutte le partite, resta comunque il PSV, nei Paesi Bassi. Non è l'AC Milan. Non ha lo stesso peso per la famiglia, per il giocatore e anche per il suo agente. Bisogna considerare tutte le persone che circondano il giocatore, è importante. E nel suo caso ho visto immediatamente che, appena ha sentito parlare del Milan, aveva già fatto la sua scelta. È stato positivo. Ho visto un ragazzo molto determinato. Sapeva già quello che voleva".

Oltre il talento, ci vuole anche un carattere forte: "A volte ci sono dei fenomeni, sai, giocatori talmente forti… Penso a Mbappé e Bernardo Silva, che erano con noi al Monaco. Erano talmente forti che quasi potevano permettersi di 'essere arroganti'. Anzi, a volte è persino meglio. Giocatori come Neymar o Rafa Leão hanno una grande 'arroganza calcistica'. Penso che Alphadjo non sia così. Ha questa umiltà perché viene da quella zona d'Italia, da Verona, perché è passato attraverso piccoli club. Penso che non si renda ancora conto del suo potenziale".

Frank appointed Crystal Palace manager

📅 Nov 16, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Thomas Frank returns to the Premier League with Crystal Palace after being sacked by Tottenham in February; he will replace Pierre Sage at Selhurst Park; Palace are bottom of the table with just seven points from their first 10 games

Crystal Palace have appointed Thomas Frank as their new manager.

The Dane replaces Pierre Sage, who was dismissed on November 12 following a seventh league defeat of the season to Liverpool.

Frank has signed a deal until the summer of 2029.

Frank was among a list of names that Palace considered in the summer before Sage was appointed to the role.

Other candidates that Palace were interested in included Frank Lampard, Sean Dyche, Vitor Pereira, Kim Hellberg, Vitor Matos, Tony Popovic, Jens Berthel Askou and Ole Werner.

Frank had great success at Brentford during his seven-year spell between 2018 and 2025, getting the Bees into the Premier League and then establishing them as a top-flight club, reaching a ninth-place finish in 2023.

He then moved to Tottenham last season to replace Ange Postecoglou, but was sacked after less than eight months in charge with the club in 16th place, five points above the relegation zone.

Frank failed to win over the Tottenham faithful, with his short tenure littered with setbacks, including drinking from a coffee cup which carried the badge of rivals Arsenal.

Palace are bottom of the Premier League with seven points, two from safety. They face the team in 17th place - Coventry - when the league resumes on Saturday.

Frank is believed to be rejuvenated and refreshed after taking some time away from the game, having recently returned from Australia.

His first task is to get Palace out of the relegation zone, with the club two points from safety.

The Dane's willingness to use a variety of formations could be a key factor in bringing the best out of Palace's current squad.

Frank was also interviewed for the Manchester United and Chelsea jobs in the summer of 2024.
