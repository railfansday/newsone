Philadelphia Eagles Finally Get Good News Before Steelers Game

📅 Nov 20, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

It has been an absolutely miserable NFL season for the Philadelphia Eagles, and at no point have things been looking up.

The Eagles are just 2-7 heading into their Week 11 matchup with the Pittsburgh Steelers. Jalen Hurts is out for the season. They traded multiple key pieces before the trade deadline. Their receiving corps have also been riddled with injuries.

But in terms of the latter, Philadelphia has finally received some good news.

Both DeVonta Smith and Dontayvion Wicks were in practice this week, and Smith particular has been given the green light to play this Sunday against the Steelers, per the team's injury report.

Smith last played on Oct. 18 due to a hamstring injury. In the four games he has played this season, he has logged 26 catches for 310 yards and a couple of touchdowns.

Oct 18, 2026; Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA; Philadelphia Eagles wide receiver DeVonta Smith (6) walks off the field after a loss against the Carolina Panthers at Lincoln Financial Field. Mandatory Credit: Bill Streicher-Imagn Images

The Eagles appear to be entering a rebuilding phase, and their future is definitely up in the air.

On the bright side, Philadelphia has stockpiled draft capital thanks to the recent trades it has made. Philly moved center Cam Jurgens and defensive tackle Jordan Davis prior to the deadline. And last June, it sent wide receiver A.J. Brown to the New England Patriots.

That obviously puts the Eagles in a strong position moving forward, especially with general manager Howie Roseman at the helm.

But with Hurts' future very much uncertain due to blood clots, Philadelphia may have to consider making a change under center in the spring, so it will be interesting to see how the Eagles approach it.

For now, though, there is now question that Philadelphia is no longer a legitimate contender and will definitely have to retool in order to re-establish itself as a serious threat in the NFC East.
