Eagles QB Joe Milton III just stands and watches his fumble in shades of infamous Cam Newton Super Bowl play

📅 Nov 22, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

This is not the kind of effort to get Eagles fans on your side.

Philadelphia quarterback Joe Milton III made his second start for the Eagles Sunday in place of the benched Tanner McKee and sidelined starter Jalen Hurts. He did not do his best to make a good early impression on the home fans.

On Philadelphia's second play from scrimmage, Milton was stripped of the ball in the backfield by Steelers pass rusher Alex Highsmith. But it wasn't the fumble that's likely to draw the ire of Eagles fans. It was his response to the loose ball.

Milton wasn't tackled on the play. And as the ball hit the ground near his feet, he just stood there and looked at it.

Milton made no effort to secure the loose ball that he had a chance at recovering.

Fortunately for the Eagles, left tackle Jordan Mailata did make a play on the ball and was able to secure it before Highsmith could. It was a good break for the Eagles that could go a long way in saving Milton from heat in Philadelphia.

The Eagles were able to convert the recovery into an early 3-0 lead.

The play drew immediate visions of Cam Newton. The former Carolina Panthers great made — or didn't make — a famously familiar play with considerably higher stakes attached.

With his Panthers trailing the Denver Broncos by six points in the fourth quarter of Super Bowl 50, Newton was stripped of the ball by Broncos pass rusher Von Miller.

Like Milton on Sunday, Newton had a shot at recovering the ball and made no effort to do so. But Newton's Panthers weren't as fortunate as the Eagles on Sunday.

The Broncos recovered Newton's fumble deep in Carolina territory to help seal 24-10 Denver win.

Milton's lack of effort clearly won't go down in infamy like Newton's. But it's never a good sign to draw those comparisons.

Game Of Thrones Officially Returning With $4,399.00 New Release

📅 Nov 22, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Some pieces of television history are easy to revisit, while others need a fairly large display cabinet, and a budget that could cover a holiday. For fans who still remember the Targaryen claim to the Iron Throne, a new Game of Thrones collectible brings one of the show’s most recognizable figures back into view. There is a catch, though: this return isn’t happening on television.

You can now pre-order JND Studios’ Daenerys Targaryen Ultimate Edition 1:3 scale statue, listed for $4,399 at Spec Fiction Shop. The retailer estimates that orders will ship in the first quarter of 2027, although that date could change. The piece is considerably more than just a standalone figure of the Mother of Dragons. The Ultimate Edition includes Daenerys seated beside her throne-like sofa, with Drogon, Rhaegal and Viserion displayed alongside her. Each dragon comes with its own base, making the set a much larger display than a conventional character statue.

In case you’re wondering about the dimensions, the retailer lists the Daenerys-and-Drogon section of the statue at 65 x 49 x 74 centimeters, including its base, and gives its weight as 24 kilograms. The additional dragons are sizable too: Viserion is listed at 31 x 29 x 69 centimeters and 3.95 kilograms, while Rhaegal measures 29 x 30 x 41 centimeters and weighs 3.3 kilograms. This isn’t just a plastic figure, mind you. Daenerys’ head, shoulders and arms are made of silicone, while the rest of her body is made of resin. Her dress is fabric, with resin used for jewelry and other accessories. The dragons are also resin pieces.

There’s also a less expensive version for collectors who can let go of the full dragon lineup. The Regular Edition, priced at $3,919, includes Daenerys with just Drogon: the Ultimate Edition, at $4,399, adds Rhaegal and Viserion to complete the Game of Thrones family. Both versions are part of a combined edition run of 299 pieces, according to the retailer’s listing.

This isn’t a brand-new reveal, though—the hype has existed since JND Studios published promotional material for its Daenerys collectible back in August 2025 and announced pre-orders. Originally, the Ultimate Edition had a price of $3,499, while the current shop listing is $4,399, so the price has risen significantly since the initial announcement was made.

Under IFAB Law 12, a team official who delays the opposition’s restart by holding onto the ball commits a sending-off offence. Players, by contrast, are generally cautioned for delaying a restart. Baum acknowledged that Petersen’s decision was technically correct but suggested that the rules impose an unnecessarily harsh punishment on coaches.

“According to the rules, the coach must be punished draconically in such a case,” Baum continued. “A player would be given a yellow. A coach must automatically be given a red for such a thing. What? I find that a little strange. I think a yellow card would have been sufficient. But, okay. That’s the letter of the law. Forget Plain Bacon: Add This Crispy Ingredient To Grilled Cheese For Sweet & Smoky Flavor

📅 Nov 22, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Plain old bacon can elevate even the plainest grilled cheese, but it tends to be a bit one-note, contributing mainly salty, crispy protein. However, if you add candied bacon instead, that extra sweetness can transform a wide variety of cheeses and breads, especially if you take the time to prepare it with extra seasonings.

Candied bacon is a delicate recipe, but if you manage to make it without burning the sugars, you get an incomparably delicious ingredient for grilled cheese. The syrupy sweetness helps cut through the sodium, whether from aged parmesan or bread crusted with garlic salt, and the texture provides a nice contrast to gooey cheese. The smokiness, in particular, is a great flavoring agent that you can easily control by increasing or reducing the amount of bacon you layer on. The end result is a nice middle ground between turning grilled cheese into a dessert and keeping it exclusively savory.

Candied bacon can take a hot minute to prepare and cook, and while it only lasts about a week in the fridge, it stays good in the freezer for up to six months. Since all you have to do is let it thaw, then zap it in the air fryer for a few minutes at 380 degrees Fahrenheit, it's a superbly easy accoutrement to keep on hand for when you want something a little special.

The best part of using candied bacon is that while it may close a few flavor doors, it opens up so many more. You may not want to pair it with an herbed goat cheese or wine-veined cheddar, but it can substantially upgrade many of the tastiest underrated cheeses into something spectacular.

When considering what to add to your new sandwich, try to think about the types of cheeses you'd find in desserts. Something creamy and high in fat provides fantastic texture and an aromatic backdrop of flavor but still lets the candied bacon take center stage. Brie, Camembert, and mascarpone are all sublime with a bit of sugar, but when balanced with the savoriness of cured bacon, they become even better. Any pale, rich variety should do the job well.

You should always use a variety of cheeses in your grilled cheese, and a dash of something sharp and aged can balance out the powerful flavors of candied bacon. Microplaning some aged Manchego or provolone on top diversifies the flavors without overpowering them, and the extra-fine texture helps even harder cheeses melt more evenly. While you don't want these to be the main characters of your recipe, a bit of added nuttiness and earthiness can bring out the best in your bacon, even if you only add a tablespoon or two.

Forget Butter And Oil: Cook Steak In This Rich Ingredient For A Better Crust & Beefier Flavor

📅 Nov 21, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

When preparing steak, cooks often focus on selecting the best cut, then preparing it with a specific method to an ideal doneness. Less emphasis is placed on the fat employed for the task — although what you pour into the pan also impacts both the flavor and texture of your meal. So skip standard pantry staples like butter and oil, and try a version with beef tallow instead.

So — what is beef tallow? It's essentially rendered beef fat, an ingredient that lends numerous advantages to steak night. Foremost is the ultra-savory profile: Tallow is made by slowly cooking down beef trimmings, resulting in a rich, meaty taste. This staple works as a flavor enhancer in addition to a cooking medium, complementing the deep, savory character of steak. And if you buy a higher-quality grass-fed variety, you may impart even more complexity, introducing earthy and grassy notes to your dining experience.

In parallel, beef tallow helps create a mouthwatering crust. The ingredient boasts a higher smoke point than butter and some other fats. So whether you're searing on high heat or finishing the steak in the oven, the fat can withstand high temperatures, enabling browning and Maillard reactions that create a terrific crust. That adds up to an elevated steak experience, all from swapping out the fat that goes into the pan.

Preparing a steak with beef tallow doesn't require any special accommodations. The fat is solid at room temperature and can be shelf-stable when properly stored, making it an easy pantry addition. You'll only need about a tablespoon per portion: Simply spoon the tallow into the skillet, where it'll melt and cover the cooking surface. It's a cooking medium that's especially effective on cast iron — whether a griddle or a pan — as it can also be used to season the cookware. You can also use tallow when grilling by coating both the cut itself and the grill grates with a thin layer of beef fat.

Any steak cut can be prepared in tallow; just note that it lends a rich flavor. So if you're making a mild-tasting filet mignon, for example, the addition could be overpowering. It's better to pair tallow with robust cuts like a ribeye, porterhouse, or New York strip, thereby producing an especially beef-forward meal. Otherwise, there's no need to worry about the details. Simply add a spoonful to your pan and sear away; this staple makes for a dependably delicious steak experience.

Emergenza rientrata. Per settimana l'Atalanta ha avuto l'infermeria piena, tanto che Maurizio Sarri ha dovuto fare di necessità virtù a volte adattando giocatori in alcuni ruoli dove c'era piena emergenza. Ma in vista della gara contro l'Inter, il tecnico recupera quasi tutti. Come riporta Tuttosport, buone sensazioni su Gaetano che ha lavorato in gruppo al pari di Kessie, Bellanova e Raspadori. "Un ventaglio di alternative che contro i nerazzurri di Chivu mancherà solo di Pompei (terzo portiere), Kristensen (lavoro individuale in campo per lui), Kossounou e Hien".

"Rispetto alle ultime uscite, prima della sosta i nerazzurri avevano toccato anche punte di 10 assenti per infortunio. Bellanova è destinato a giocare dal primo minuto sulla corsia di destra, mentre Kessie, in campo 95 minuti in due partite con la nazionale, sarà protagonista nel 4-2-3-1 insieme a Ederson davanti alla difesa. Il rientro di Gaetano e Raspadori tra i convocati offre la possibilità di avere maggiori soluzioni a gara in corso con l'eventuale passaggio al 4-3-3 da mettere tra le opzioni da sfruttare".

"In attacco, ad esempio, sono diverse le varianti che si possono mandare in campo. La più accreditata vede Elmas e Zalewski esterni con De Ketelaere alle spalle di Krstovic, non va esclusa la presenza di Rowe sulla corsia mancina così come una staffetta nel ruolo di centravanti tra il belga e il montenegrino. Scamacca e Samardzic, al momento, sono da considerare come alternative pronte a subentrare per una squadra che recuperando i giocatori che si sono finalmente messi alle spalle tutti i problemi fisici che avevano possono senza dubbio dare un contributo di grande rilievo alla causa".
