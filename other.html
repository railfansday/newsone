📅 Nov 20, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Sheffield Wednesday are expecting the midfielder to be out of action until the next campaign. SWFC signed him from on a three-year deal running until June 2029 in the summer.

Onyango, 23, cut ties with Everton at the end of last term when his contract expired and he subsequently became a free agent before the Owls swooped in. He suffered an ACL injury against Leyton Orient back in August.

In this latest update regarding his condition, Challinor has said, as per the official club website: “Tyler is a really positive character because he’s had real disappointment over the short career he’s had so far. He’s had to deal with significant injuries, and that’s probably made him better. I think, looking at him, you always get to the point where you think he might be due a couple of games before the end of the season. But I think you probably have to step back and accept that there might be some setbacks along the way that sort of halt that.

“So, if he can walk out of this building in the summer having not played any minutes, but being fit and able to go into the summer and come back in a really, really good state for pre-season, that would be great. If we get anything better than that, I think it’ll be a bonus.”

Sheffield Wednesday snapped up Onyango on a free transfer back in August. He was brought in by SWFC to provide more competition and depth in the middle of the park.

At the age of 23, he is still young and has potential to develop down the line. When fit again, he will battle it out with the likes of Callum Slattery, Billy Mitchell and Barry Bannan, among others, for a place in the Owls’ starting XI.

For now though, he continues on his long road to recovery. The Luton-born man had two loan spells under Challinor at Stockport County so knows him already.

Onyango rose up through Everton’s academy and was a regular for the Toffees at various different youth levels before going on to play five times for their first-team as a youngster. As well the Hatters, he was also loaned out to Burton Albion and Forest Green Rovers in the EFL to get some experience under his belt.

Sheffield Wednesday need to be patient with Onyango and won’t want to rush him back in. As Challinor has alluded to, it seems likely that he will feature for SWFC again next season.

The Owls will be hoping that they are in the Championship again by then. They were relegated to League One earlier this year after they finished in the bottom three along with Leicester City and Oxford United and they are eyeing an immediate return.

It was a summer of transition for the South Yorkshire outfit and there were both plenty of comings and goings. Henrik Pedersen eventually lost his job late last month before Challinor’s arrival.

Sheffield Wednesday won 3-0 at home against Plymouth Argyle last time out. They are back in action on Friday night away at Barnsley.

Vision has undergone numerous transformations since then, from taking on his emotionless white form to being semi-recreated as the Young Avengers hero Jonas. Now, Vision undergoes his darkest transformation yet and a new name change.

Marvel recently revealed new details for the upcoming Hulk War: Infernal Rage #1, officially revealing the names of each of the 'Infernal Avengers' commanded by the titular villain. These monsters are all heroes corrupted by the Infernal Hulk's magic and possessed by the souls of ancient monsters.

The press release confirmed what fans had guessed - that the robotic being by Hulk's side is a transformed Vision. Patterned after a biblically-accurate angel, this new Vision is a floating metallic skull with a single eye and four wings, a gigantic, uncovered brain emerging from the top.

Marvel's new images name the being "The Diaboli Ex Machina," meaning "the devil from the machine." The reference is to an ancient Greek theatrical convention where a supernatural being would emerge to conclude the story, often appearing by a literal mechanism. "Diaboli Ex Machina" therefore speaks to a devil literally emerging from a mechanism, and also a supernatural being appearing suddenly to defeat Marvel's heroes.

Vision hasn't yet taken this form in Marvel Comics, though fans did glimpse it in an Amazing Spider-Man 1000/Queen in Black CGD 2026 #1 flash forward to the upcoming events of Hulk War.

The transformation is likely to happen in December 2's Infernal Hulk vs. Avengers #1, which will see Captain America, Iron Man, Captain Marvel, Vision and Scarlet Witch take on the Infernal Hulk, aka the demonic Eldest.

Because everyone but Wanda has a confirmed monster form in Hulk War - and due to their past romantic and familial bond - fans have speculated that The Diaboli Ex Machina is a combination of Scarlet Witch and Vision.
