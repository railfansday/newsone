New York building collapse kills at least four people

📅 Nov 12, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Watch: Aerial video shows survivor being pulled from collapsed building in New York

At least four people have died after a building in New York City collapsed into an open construction site on Wednesday afternoon.

Nineteen others have been injured, of whom four are in a critical condition, according to the BBC's US partner CBS News, which cited local officials.

The collapse sparked a massive search-and-rescue operation, with more than 250 first responders going to the scene of the fallen three-storey building in Brooklyn.

Aerial footage has shown firefighters pulling a survivor, covered in dust, out of the debris on a stretcher. She was later said to have been a pregnant woman.

Officials previously said they did not know how many others are missing. The BBC has contacted the New York City Fire Department (FDNY) for further comment.

An investigation is under way into what caused the sudden collapse.

Witnesses described a terrifying sound as the building came down at about 16:00 local time (21:00 GMT).

"I live literally right behind the building," one woman, Emmanuella Carilus, told the New York Times. "It sounded like a big boom and you just heard a bunch of screaming."

The newspaper published images showing a crowd of fire trucks and police vehicles at the same, and wrote that the noise of sirens had not stopped in the area.

"I've never seen anything like this," said another witness, named only as Dashik, who spoke to local television station PIX 11.

Officials are yet to identify those who died or were injured.

Officials say the rescue efforts could last for days

Locals have gathered at the scene to await news of those feared to have been caught in the collapse.

The rescue mission has been described as "extremely dangerous" by New York City Fire Department (FDNY) Commissioner Lillian Bonsignore.

She added that people from the collapsed building and also the construction site had been trapped.

"Members have been working methodically and haven't stopped since they arrived this afternoon," Bonsignore said.

The woman who was filmed being rescued from the rubble was identified by her husband.

Speaking to a reporter from CBS News New York, he described his relief after an anxious wait: "She is alive. She is still alive. I'm very happy. I'm very happy. I'm very happy. I thank all of you guys. She's pregnant, six months."

Rescuers have used search dogs, drones, axes and chainsaws to carry out their work. They say they anticipate the effort will last for several days.

The collapse exposed parts of the building's interior - including bedrooms and wardrobes

Some pictures of the collapsed building, on Flatbush Avenue in Brooklyn, show an entire side of it gone, with bedrooms and closets exposed. Among the debris, smashed concrete and twisted metal, are household items and clothes.

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani joined emergency responders at the scene, praising them for their rapid response.

They "arrived in under a minute and have spent hours working in dangerous and highly technical conditions to rescue New Yorkers," he later wrote on X.

The Brooklyn building was built in 1930, city records show, according to CBS, the BBC's US partner.

At the adjacent construction site, where plans have been approved for a new building, active work was going on when the incident occurred, according to FDNY Chief of Department John Esposito.

The activity was described by another official as "excavation work". It was not immediately clear whether the work had anything to do with the collapse of the neighbouring building.

Zcash's active developer count has roughly doubled in two years, despite broader industry contraction.

ZEC price is facing another uncomfortable test after falling below $1,000 in November. The latest decline has raised concerns about whether Zcash can defend its broader bullish structure, particularly as the token retests its 20-week EMA band. Interestingly, this isn’t the first time ZEC has experienced a brutal correction after reaching new highs. Its history suggests investors shouldn’t dismiss another 75% drawdown scenario.

The previous market structure offers some uncomfortable comparisons. Since 2021, ZEC had traded inside a descending triangle, repeatedly testing the $95-$100 support zone. Expectations of a breakout increased during the first half of 2022. Instead, the structure broke down.

What followed was an unusually long consolidation stretching from the second half of 2022 through the first half of 2025.

Then came the turnaround. ZEC reached an all-time high of $740 in the second half of 2025 before profit-taking triggered a correction exceeding 75%, eventually bringing prices toward $200.

During Q1 2026, another descending triangle emerged. This time, the breakout worked. By the end of Q3 2026, ZEC had established a new all-time high of $1,695. Now, November’s decline has pushed the token below $1,000 again.

The current correction stands approximately 41% below the latest all-time high. Despite the decline, the broader uptrend remains intact, with price hovering around the 20-week EMA band.

However, historical comparisons aren’t particularly comforting. ZEC previously dropped from $380 to $95 in 2021 and from $740 to $200 in 2025. Both corrections represented declines approaching 75%.

If selling pressure continues, $740 and $550 emerge as potential support areas. A deeper breakdown could expose the $400-$440 region, representing another substantial correction from the latest peak. These are potential downside scenarios, not confirmed price targets.

Institutional flows aren’t helping the immediate picture. US spot Zcash ETFs recorded $14.61 million in outflows on November 11, their largest daily withdrawal since October 15, according to SoSoValue data.

Funds experienced withdrawals on six of the last nine trading days. November’s net outflows consequently reached $29.78 million, while total net assets declined to $562.16 million, their lowest level since September 4.

The reversal is notable because late October delivered the strongest weekly inflows since mid-September.

Here’s where the bearish price action gets interesting. Grayscale research indicates Zcash’s monthly active developer count has roughly doubled over two years, even as the broader cryptocurrency developer population declined.

Approximately 70% of active Zcash developers have more than one year of crypto experience, compared with 58% across the wider industry.

Grayscale believes this development momentum could eventually support ZEC’s valuation. However, the market hasn’t rewarded that progress yet.

For now, ZEC price faces competing signals: weakening ETF flows, a substantial correction, and improving developer participation. Whether the 20-week EMA holds could become particularly important. If selling accelerates, the historical 75% correction pattern makes $740, $550, and potentially $400-$440 levels worth watching.

Cheaper crude may not bring the fuel-price relief Bitcoin needs: damaged refineries are keeping the 2027 diesel forecast above $5 a gallon.

The US Energy Information Administration raised its forecast for next year’s retail diesel price by 12%, reducing the fuel-cost relief it previously expected. That leaves less room for falling fuel costs to cool inflation and bring the lower interest rates that could help Bitcoin, even as the agency projects cheaper crude in 2027.

The EIA’s November 10 outlook puts the 2027 US diesel average at $5.03 a gallon, up from $4.49 in its October 6 forecast. That is an upward revision to the same annual forecast, rather than a 12% increase in observed pump prices.

Diesel is still projected to fall from a forecast $5.25 average in 2026. EIA also expects Brent crude to average $86 a barrel in 2027, down from a projected $97 this year. Inputs were finalized November 5, so the outlook does not specifically account for subsequent market events.

Cheaper crude does not guarantee a matching decline in finished-fuel costs. EIA assumes petroleum-product supply constraints will continue through the end of 2027, supporting wider crack spreads: the gap between refined-product prices and crude costs.

The agency attributes elevated refining margins to damaged refineries in the Middle East and Russia, which have reduced product exports. It expects disruptions to be concentrated early next year and constraints to ease thereafter, while warning that unplanned outages and deferred maintenance could disrupt refinery operations.

US refinery crude inputs are forecast at 16.5 million barrels a day in 2027, against 16.7 million this year, despite similar utilization. Running the available refineries hard therefore does not necessarily mean processing more crude.

Arsenal face January dilemma as Liverpool and Chelsea join Alex Scott transfer race

📅 Nov 12, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Lucie Turner writes about Arsenal for Read Arsenal and Newcastle United for Read Newcastle. A long-time Arsenal supporter, she also follows rugby, snooker, darts and Formula 1. Her work covers match previews and reports, breaking news, player profiles and opinion pieces.

Arsenal have made contact with Bournemouth over a potential move for Alex Scott. Mikel Arteta may have to rethink his transfer plans if competition for the midfielder intensifies.

According to The i Paper, Arsenal, Liverpool and Chelsea have all made exploratory enquiries about the 23-year-old. The clubs want to establish what it would take to persuade Bournemouth to sell. However, none appears close to agreeing to a deal.

Arsenal are believed to be keen on a summer move. Liverpool and Chelsea are more interested in signing Scott in January. However, if they were to make a serious offer, it could be hard to hold out.

Scott has become one of the most exciting midfielders in the Premier League. He is an attractive option for clubs looking to improve their squad going forward due to his age, experience and being a homegrown player.

However, there is some uncertainty around Scott’s future. The England international has turned down two contract offers and is unwilling to sign a new deal. There are 18 months left on Scott’s contract, meaning it is an increasingly important decision for Bournemouth.

Arsenal are interested in Scott with doubts surrounding their own midfield options. There have been reports linking Martin Zubimendi and Mikel Merino to exits.

Scott would give Arteta another option, but Arsenal will have to consider if they want to make a move and give Liverpool and Chelsea an advantage.

Bournemouth are reported to want more than £100m to let him go during the January transfer window.

It would be a big asking price, especially given that Scott has 18 months left on his contract. There are a few reasons why Bournemouth could be holding out for a big fee.

The Cherries had to pay £25m to bring Scott in from Bristol City, who will receive 20 per cent of any future transfer fee. They will also have to find a suitable replacement at the halfway stage.

Reports said Chelsea received a number of offers worth around £70m in the summer, but Bournemouth have rejected claims that a bid of £80m will get them talking.

Scott is believed to be happy to stay at the club until the end of the campaign. Bournemouth are also having a great season under Marco Rose, currently sitting in seventh place in the Premier League with eight straight wins in all competitions.

Arsenal have little to hurry into any deal and could use the summer to negotiate a better deal. This is especially true with Scott’s contract situation becoming more of an issue.

The problem is that Liverpool and Chelsea are keen competitors and could lead to a bidding war in January, leaving Arsenal with little room to negotiate.

Whilst Arsenal have made their interest known, this doesn’t necessarily mean he will be moving anytime soon. If Scott is a genuine target, then they must be prepared to pounce if the situation arises.
