Rihanna Is Committed to This It Bag From the 2000s

📅 Nov 11, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Fashion’s nostalgia craze isn’t going anywhere. These days, It girls from Bella Hadid to Anya Taylor-Joy, Hailey Bieber, Blake Lively, and Kim Kardashian are pulling from the archives to deliver striking ensembles that look to fashion eras of the past. It’s a little trick our favorite funky dresser, Rihanna, knows plenty about.

The Fenty Beauty founder has a special appreciation for the ’90s and early aughts. She’s shown us this through her wardrobe for years—what with her tracksuit jackets, sporty jumpsuits, and most importantly, her handbags.

Rihanna has stepped out carrying everything from the timeless Lady Dior of the 1990s to the Anaconda bag from Tom Ford’s Spring 1996 collection for Gucci. But she is especially drawn to a good ol’ Louis Vuitton monogram.

In the past, we’ve seen the “Umbrella” singer carry the colorful LV x Takashi Murakami Monogram Speedy 30, the Speedy Nano in blue denim with a white monogram, and the Speedy Soft 30 Boho in the classic brown-and-tan pattern. Yet the most fabulous piece in her LV collection may be her Speedy P9 25, which she carried out this week, and which debuted in the Menswear Spring 2024 show—Pharrell Williams’s first collection as men’s creative director. In this style, the Speedy’s iconic monogram and colors remain traditional, but the leather is crocodile. (The design is one of the most luxurious from Williams’s Speedy P9 series.)

Now, some backstory. Louis Vuitton introduced its monogram canvas in 1896 and expanded it into travel and everyday bags—including the Keepall and its smaller sister, the Speedy (originally called the “Express”)—in 1930. While the Speedy debuted in linen with leather handles, it was reintroduced in 1959 in the monogram coated canvas we know today. Some years later, in 1965, the one and only Audrey Hepburn requested a smaller, sleeker version, which is how the Speedy 25 was born.

The house’s creative directors have since given new life to the style, while fashion muses, like Rihanna, continue to prove its icon status.

Rihanna styled her bag with a striped black-and-beige long-sleeve, tan pants, white sneakers, skinny shades, and the glossiest caramel lip for a night out in Los Angeles.

"Some of the best games of the last decade came from teams of fifty": Destroy All Humans! remake devs Black Forest Games split from THQ Nordic to launch new indie studio group

📅 Nov 11, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

"We're excited to shape our own future as owners again"

Black Forest Games, developers of the Destroy All Humans! remakes, have spun out of Embracer Group-owned publishers THQ Nordic via a management buyout and announced plans to form a new "developer-driven group" of independent studios. The new group, dubbed Third Signal, will aim to "give teams the independence, resources and creative freedom" to "bring their own ideas to life".

Black Forest, based in Germany, announced the move in a press release. "We are grateful to THQ Nordic and Embracer for nine good years and the games we made together," co-boss Adrian Goersch said. "Now, we're excited to shape our own future as owners again, to work on an exciting new project with a great team and strong partners behind us."

"Some of the best games of the last decade came from teams of fifty, not five hundred," added Third Signal founder Andreas Tobler. "Black Forest Games is one of them, and the right studio to launch Third Signal. Our vision is to build a developer driven group and give teams the independence, resources and creative freedom to continue their creative legacy and bring their own ideas to life." As of writing, Third Signal's website is just a logo splash which promises something coming soon. To be fair, it does also look to have an email address, plus legal and privacy notices. Black Forest's management team will become a co-owner of the studio group as part of this move, but "continue to collaborate on game development tasks" with THQ Nordic.

Black Forest had been in the embrace since 2017, releasing both 2020's Destroy All Humans! remake and Destroy All Humans! 2: Reprobed in that time, plus DLC Clone Carnage. Over the years they've also made multiple entries in the Giana Sisters platformer series, survival game Fade to Silence, and 2017's Bubsy: The Woolies Strike Back.

That's a right old mix of stuff, but the biggest names in it are all resurrections of series or characters created by other folks back in the day. Pandemic Studios in the case of Destroy All Humans!, Accolade in the case of Bubsy, and Germany company Time Warp in the case of Giana Sisters. That makes the fact it sounds like this move'll give Black Forest a bit more creative freedom intriguing. Here's hoping that if they do plan to try and get some more of their own original ideas off the ground, rather than trucking on with a steady diet of remakes and revivals, they manage to stick the landing. It's all fine declaring that a 50 person team can make a top game these days, but that certainly doesn't mean it's in any way easy to pull off.

For those not in the know, Encore is a recut version of Endgame that adds scenes tying into Doomsday. The film was released in theaters on Sept. 25, grossing $142.9 million and making Avengers: Endgame the highest-grossing film of all time at $2.942 billion. Avatar and Endgame have been trading the box office crown for some time now: Endgame’s original 2019 release made it the highest-grossing film over Avatar, before a 2021 Avatar re-release let Cameron's sci-fi epic temporarily retake the No. 1 spot. Marvel fans who missed Encore in theaters can see the film on Disney Plus on Nov. 13.

As for what’s changed in Encore, the film has four extra scenes that add four minutes to theruntime. For those who don’t mind spoilers, here’s an outline of what’s different. Encore adds an extended ending, two scenes that play mid-credits, and another sequence at the end of the credits.

In the extended ending, Steve Rogers and Peggy Carter are visited by a version of Loki who promises to help them — this is likely why Captain America will appear in Doomsday, despite seemingly livinga happy life with Peggy in the past. As for the mid-credits scenes, one shows Bruce Banner self-isolating in a military complex due to an “incident” (presumably referring to the events of Spider-Man: Brand New Day). As Banner experiments with a way to suppress the gamma radiation in his body, Doctor Doom teleports him out of his cell.

In the final post-credits scene, the sound of metal being hammered during the credits is revealed to be Doctor Doom forging his mask.

These scenes were added to segue more smoothly into Avengers: Doomsday, which didn’t have the same degree of buildup as previous Avengers films. While several films leading up to Avengers: Infinity War and Endgame hyped Thanos as an antagonist and teased the Infinity Stones, Doomsday came together far more hastily.

Never Paid Into Social Security? Here's How You Might Qualify for Benefits Regardless

📅 Nov 11, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Social Security benefits typically have to be earned, but there's another option if you don't have a work history.

Social Security is a lifeline for millions of older Americans today. And for many people, the path to being eligible for benefits involves working and paying into the system.

Social Security's primary source of revenue is payroll taxes. As such, to qualify for benefits in retirement, you need to pay taxes on a certain amount of wages to accumulate work credits.

In 2026, for example, the value of a single Social Security work credit is $1,890. In 2027, that value is rising to $1,970.

You can collect a maximum of four Social Security work credits per calendar year, and you need 40 total work credits in your lifetime to receive benefits in retirement. But if you never worked or have a minimal earnings history, you may not have those 40 credits by the time your senior years roll around.

The good news is that there may be another path toward collecting Social Security. It's important to know what options you have if your earnings history does not render you eligible for benefits.

If you're married to someone who's eligible for Social Security benefits or are an eligible divorcee, you may be entitled to collect spousal Social Security benefits. But it's important to know how they work.

First, if you're married, you can't file for spousal Social Security benefits until your spouse signs up. If you're divorced, you may be able to get your spousal benefits before your former spouse files. Your ex-spouse also can't prevent you from claiming spousal benefits, and your claim doesn't impact the amount of money they get.

Secondly, spousal Social Security benefits max out at 50% of your current or former spouse's full retirement age benefit. And you can claim your spousal benefits in full at your full retirement age.

In other words, if your spouse gets $2,000 at their full retirement age, your spousal benefit is worth $1,000 if you claim it at your full retirement age. If you file early, which you can do starting at age 62, your spousal benefit will be reduced for life.

You also can't boost your spousal benefit by delaying your claim past full retirement age. Delayed retirement credits are available to Social Security recipients claiming benefits based on their own earnings records, but they don't apply to spousal benefits.

If you never worked or barely worked, you might assume that Social Security is off the table. But you may be entitled to spousal Social Security benefits that can serve as a key source of income later in life.

The key is to make sure you understand how those benefits work, what they're worth, and when to file. The more you know about spousal benefits, the more helpful they might be to your finances.

Forget Salmon: This Fast-Cooking Fish Has Fewer Calories

📅 Nov 11, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

Salmon's rich intensity also means it generally has more calories, which can mean different things based on someone's dietary needs. If you're conscious of this and still want something that cooks up quickly (and deliciously), try flounder, a leaner, lower-calorie option that never lacks flavor.

Exact calorie amounts can vary quite a bit, especially when it comes to wild versus farmed salmon, but even the leanest ones usually sit between 130 and 200 calories per three-ounce serving. Flounder populations, however, have a more uniform lifestyle and consistently clock in at around 100 calories per three-ounce serving. That brown or gray layer of fat may help a salmon migrate and survive cold waters, but flounder are sedentary ambush predators that don't have nearly the same energy requirements. On your plate, this means a fillet of flounder is lean, clean-tasting, and quite mild compared to a richly marbled piece of salmon.

Because flounder is often thinner and far less fatty than salmon, that also means it cooks exponentially quicker. While a skin-on fillet can widen your margin of error, it's still much easier to render your tender fish into a dense, rubbery mess. Your best bet is to treat it like a lean steak and use a hot and fast cooking method, letting a bit of carryover cooking finish the center rather than trying to reach the perfect temperature in a pan or on the grill.

Flounder is delicate, light, and can easily lose its natural flavor if seasoned too heavily. While it's not exactly a beginner-friendly fish, the best ways to cook it are often straightforward and focus on timing and temperature, not technique. Hands-off methods that require little manipulation or rely on a sauce for flavor are usually ideal and keep your meat moist and tender.

If you haven't tried making a meunière yet, it's a classic seafood dish you should try at least once. Made by combining browned butter, lemon, and parsley, it's just flavorful enough to elevate your flounder without overwhelming it. Just about any French butter sauce, from beurre blanc to béchamel, is great with flounder because it adds a bit of richness and is a great vehicle for other tasty seasonings. Plus, they're flavorful enough that you'll only have to pan-sear each side of your fish, reducing the chance that it'll fall apart or overcook.

Flounder grows more delicate the more it's cooked, so preparation methods that rely on you only handling it when raw are also quite ideal. Baking is a precise method, and you only have to move the fish once to transfer it to a plate. On the other hand, flounder is fantastic when deep-fried, and the breading helps it hold its shape. You'll still want to use a large, slotted tool and a delicate hand when fishing it out, but all you need to do is batter and drop it into oil for a great result.
