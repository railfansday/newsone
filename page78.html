This morning I actually slept in pretty late at our room in Strawberry. It was nice knowing that there was no pressure to go anywhere or do anything. My only goals for the day are to get some editing done, eat a bunch of food, and resupply for this next stretch of trail. By the time that I did finally get up, Slide was very ready to eat. So, we decided to get our rain gear on and walk down the road to the breakfast farm stand right by the Strawberry Inn. I checked the hiker group chat and saw that a group of hikers from the Airbnb had walked over there and was hiding out from the rain in some kind of a shed. The place pretty much only has outdoor seating, but apparently the staff showed the hikers where they could hide out and eat at the same time.

Grabbing breakfast from the farm stand place in Strawberry.

When we got over there a handful of hikers was hanging out and ordering some more food. We got in line and ordered some stuff to take back and eat at our room, since it was right down the road. Everyone else finished up and wound up going back to their Airbnb to hang out. I figure we’ll head over there at some point today. But I also have a mountain of editing to get done, and it will definitely be easier to do it somewhere quiet. My eyes were way bigger than my stomach this morning, so I wound up ordering two things. The first was a lemon ricotta pancake, which sounded absolutely divine. And I also got a breakfast burrito to eat later on, just in case. On trail I always feel like I order way too much food or not nearly enough. Which is likely why I usually wind up ordering way too much.

The farm stand breakfast spot in Strawberry, AZ served one of the most incredible pancakes that I’d ever had.

We took the food to go and brought it back to the Strawberry Inn to eat and relax. And that lemon ricotta pancake was one of the best things that I’d ever had. I love ricotta but have never had it in a pancake before! Prior to getting on trail I was cooking a lot at home and making a ton of homemade pizza. Oftentimes I would top it with ricotta because it is so delicious. But this just opened my eyes to a whole new use for the stuff. I didn’t wind up eating everything right away but figured I’d just graze on it for the next couple of hours.

After I ate, I decided to get right into business and start doing some writing and video editing. I also had an exciting call with a brand that I’ve worked with in the past and am going to be working with next year. My plans for 2027 are finally starting to come together and I’m really excited to be able to share it with you all eventually. The tiny home that we were staying in had a separate room which was pretty much the size of a walk-in closet. That was the perfect place to do some audio recordings and get certain work done for a while. Once all of that was done, I could get the rest of the work done more comfortably, while also watching a little bit of TV.

In the afternoon I also had to place a Walmart delivery order to the inn. I had a lot of food, but definitely not enough to be comfortable for the next 71 miles. Our plan is to hike out of town and continue 71 miles to the next highway crossing, where we can go into the larger town of Payson. That will be about 3 days of hiking including the day out of town, and I’ve had a big appetite lately. It also sounded nice to get some drinks and snacks to enjoy this evening while we were at it. Before I placed the order, I messaged the hiker group chat to see if anyone needed anything random. Hikers always do! And sure, enough one hiker needed some sunblock and things like that. Which was easy enough to add on to the order. Plus, we’re going to be getting a ride back to trail tomorrow with a few of these hikers, so we’ll be seeing them.

Figuring out that you can do things in small towns such as place an online food order to be delivered is certainly a thru hiking hack. I had never even thought about doing anything like that prior to hiking the CYTC last year. Time was my most valuable resource and I was willing to do a lot to save it when I could. Oftentimes you’ll spend an hour walking to a store in town, resupplying, and then perhaps another hour walking back to the room. Some trail towns are spread out with miles between all of the essential locations. Oftentimes stores like Walmart won’t even charge a delivery fee for orders if you spend over a certain amount. Today I’m grateful to be able to do this and not have to hitch into the town of Pine to go back to the grocery store. Then hitch back once we’re finished.

While the Walmart order got delivered, I got a bunch more editing work done. I’ve also been sending a lot of emails and setting plans into motion for my upcoming hike next year. It feels a bit like Deja vu, honestly. When I was hiking the CDT in 2024, I did the bulk of all of my planning for the following years border to border CYTC hike. There are so many logistics involved in a hike like that. Plus, you need a lot of gear! I remember dedicating serious time in towns to do video editing and also all of that coordinating back then on the CDT. Then when I finished up the hike I went home and had about 6 weeks to finish all of the planning and work as much as I could to save up money for the endeavor. I guess this is just the season for it. But this all feels very familiar.

This wound up being the perfect space to spend a couple of days off in town. I got most of my work done in that little room by the couch. And there is another bed on the other side of that wall.

Eventually our Walmart order got delivered, but not without minor issue. The driver had some trouble finding the place, which I was expecting. The address for our location was pretty confusing. But then after the groceries were delivered, I noticed an item that we hadn’t ordered. Then I realized we were missing a few things that we had ordered. Luckily, they were all drinks, so it wasn’t anything essential to us getting back out on trail. But they were sugary drinks that I was excited to enjoy. There isn’t any kind of a store or gas station in Strawberry, which is really the only thing that this town is missing. My favorite thing to do in town is to buy a bunch of different beverages and drink them all over the course of the day. Typically, on a double zero such as this, I would probably have bought a small case of soda or something like that. Liquid calories are my secret to not losing too much weight on trail.

By now the day was already starting to wrap up. Zero days on trail have such a miraculous way of slipping right through your fingers. It happens every single time without a doubt. At least I did get a lot of “work” done today and I also felt like I relaxed a lot. There really isn’t anywhere to go in this town, so there was no reason to be anywhere except for the room all day. A little after 5PM we started figuring out what to do for dinner. Everyone at the Airbnb had gotten so many groceries that they were just going to be staying in and trying to eat through everything. I wasn’t starving at this point, but I knew that later on I would be. Over the course of the day, I ate the rest of my lemon ricotta pancake and the whole breakfast burrito. That little farm stand in Strawberry was fantastic.

Surprisingly our options for dinner this evening were not limited. There were actually a few places open in town tonight because it was later in the week. One place sold empanadas, but they actually did wind up closing right before we were ordering food. Then there was also a steakhouse and an Italian restaurant. We wound up getting a big white sauce pizza to split for dinner and were able to just walk down the road and pick it up. It was hardly raining at this point, though it had rained heavily for most of the day. It was pretty nice out when we walked to grab the pizza. The moon is incredibly full right now which is exciting moving into this next section of trail. I love night hiking when the moon is full.

Once we picked up the pizza we retreated back to the room for the rest of the night. The gang was just over at the Airbnb watching a movie. And while it would have been nice to hang out with everyone, I wasn’t in the mood to walk another mile just to watch a movie somewhere when I could just stay in bed and watch a movie there. It was too easy. The pizza was delicious and just what I needed. After we ate, I was contemplating going in the hot tub again. I went in this morning after breakfast and it was wonderful. But I think that going in first thing tomorrow will be even better. After eating I just relaxed for the rest of the night. I got a little bit more writing done and we watched TV for a while. Then I did my stretching routine and laid up on my phone for longer than I should have before finally calling it a night.
