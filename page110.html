Tulum Is Losing Airlines Fast: Why The Hyped New Airport Is Failing

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

In late 2023, we saw the opening of the Felipe Carrillo Puerto International Airport (TQO), in Tulum, Mexico. For those not familiar, Tulum is located on the eastern coast of the Yucatan Peninsula, and for years, it was a growing tourist destination.

While it could previously be accessed via Cancun International Airport (CUN), the hope was that by opening a new international airport, visitor numbers would expand massively. Unfortunately what has actually happened is exactly the opposite, as airline after airline has pulled out. What exactly is going on here? I think it makes for an interesting story, and thanks to TravelinWilly for flagging this.

In 2023, we saw airline after airline announce flights to Tulum. Among US carriers, we saw American, Delta, JetBlue, and United, all launch flights to Tulum, while Spirit announced flights, but never launched them.

That was only a small part of the overall international service to the airport, as we also saw flights to Tulum on Air Canada, Air Transat, Avianca, Copa, Discover, and WestJet, along with flights on domestic carriers like Aeromexico, Mexicana de Aviacion, Viva, and Volaris.

Less than three years after the airport opened, where do things stand? When it comes to international year-round service, we’ve now gone from service to 12 airports, to service to four airports, which is an absolutely massive reduction:

Just to put this into numbers, in the first half of 2026, the airport saw a 33.2% reduction in passengers. June 2026 was the worst month to date, with a 44.8% drop, as there were just 47,600 passengers, compared to 86,200 passengers the year prior.

It goes without saying that for a newly built airport, this is rather catastrophic, and not what you hope to see.

In some ways, it feels like the whole concept behind Tulum Airport was sort of unnecessary. That’s also easily explained by the fact that the airport was well over a decade in the making, so while I can see how it might’ve seemed like a good idea at some point, tourism trends do evolve over time.

The reality is that there was a point where Tulum was cool, edgy, and a bit off the beaten path, compared to… whatever it is now. But when it comes to the struggles that airlines have had with flying to Tulum, it seems like the problems have been endless:

It’ll be interesting to see if Tulum can reverse any of these trends, and regain visitors…

A few years ago Tulum Airport opened to some fanfare, as a good number of airlines rushed to add service there. Unfortunately the excitement didn’t last long, as the airport has gone from a dozen year-round international routes, to just four.

This reduction in demand particularly evident from the United States, as soon there will just be nonstop service on American and Delta, and even that will be with reduced frequencies and routes.

Tulum Airport is facing endless challenges, not the least of which is a decline in visitors to Mexico from the United States, combined with increasing competition for those fewer visitors. Mexico just isn’t a value destination in the way that it used to be in places like Cancun, Tulum, Los Cabos, etc., and that’s understandably impacting demand.

I’m curious if Tulum can find a way to turn things around, but it’s not a good situation.

Stronger Together: HomeServices of America Celebrates Top Agents, Fuels Future Growth

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Above: HomeServices of America President and CEO Chris Kelly addresses the crowd at the company’s “Stronger Together” Conference

While real estate success is often measured individually—with an emphasis on closed transactions, milestones achieved and accolades earned—some of the greatest opportunities for growth present themselves when real estate professionals come together.

That philosophy was at the heart of HomeServices of America’s 4th Annual “Stronger Together” Conference, held Oct. 26 – 29 at the Fontainebleau Miami Beach. The oceanside event brought together more than 400 agents from the HomeServices’ owned brokerage companies to celebrate the professionals whose achievements earned them a place among the company’s best—each invited on the strength of their success.

Collectively, these agents rank in the top 1.5% of approximately 1.4 million Realtors® nationwide—averaging seven times the sales volume of the national average and three times more transactions. Generating $10.2 billion in annual sales volume, Stronger Together agent attendees are responsible for helping 16,483 families move each year.

“Our agents earned their place at Stronger Together, and we wanted every moment to reflect how much we value them,” HomeServices of America President and CEO Chris Kelly said in a release.

The mastermind behind the reinvigoration of the organization, Kelly has led HomeServices of America’s transformation from a quiet holding company into a bold parent company focused on providing strategic direction, cohesion and resources across its family of brokerage, mortgage, insurance and title services.

Central to that vision is a commitment to investing in people, performance and long-term growth that extends throughout the organization—a commitment reflected in reflections and anecdotes shared with RISMedia after the event from both HomeServices of America executives and attendees alike.

Playing a key role in educating and elevating the performance of brokerage owners, operators, team leaders, office managers and the sales associates in the field each and every day, HomeServices of America Chief Coaching Officer Jimmy Burgess takes pride in seeing that work come together.

“I’ve been part of this event for a long time, and what still gets me is the room. Every person in it earned their seat, and they’re all generous with what they know,” says Burgess, who spent the week watching agents trade ideas between sessions as much as in them.

“It’s a privilege to say thank you to people who work this hard, and to send them home with a few new ideas and a few new friends,” he adds.

Reflecting the company’s commitment to delivering the complete real estate experience—a theme central to HomeServices of America’s strategy—this year’s event mirrored that philosophy by offering agents inspiration, practical insight and community in one setting. Newly introduced Super Sessions, which go beyond a traditional breakout or panel, and inspiring voices provided top-performing agents everything they need to keep winning in today’s market.

For clients, that complete experience is delivered through OnePoint, the integrated platform HomeServices of America launched earlier this year to provide a more streamlined, end-to-end experience to buyers and sellers alike.

“We changed a few things this year, including the Super Sessions and a main stage with three voices instead of one, because folks at this level already know their craft. What they want is room to keep growing,” explains Kelly.

“It’s the same thinking behind OnePoint: Give people what they need in one place, so they aren’t piecing it together alone,” he adds. “When our agents grow, their clients feel it.”

Providing agents the tools and motivation to keep succeeding, entrepreneur, investor and bestselling author Jesse Itzler kicked off the conference with a keynote presentation challenging attendees to redefine wealth through experiences, relationships, mindset, health and purpose.

In addition to Super Sessions designed to give agents the time and depth to explore a multitude of topics impacting the industry, attendees also heard shared perspectives on kindness, connection and determination. Another Stronger Together highlight was a live recording of the “Stay Paid” podcast with Kelly, Burgess and Prosperity Home Mortgage President and CEO Justin Messer.

“A gathering like this sets a standard for the industry,” says HomeServices of America Senior Vice President Lacey Conway. “With more than 400 agents in the top 1.5% nationally, the program had to match the people in the room, and it did.

“From the keynote to the ‘Super Sessions’ to the speakers on (the topic of) belonging, you could see the same things we see in these agents: Curiosity, discipline and a willingness to keep learning when they’re already at the top,” she says.

HomeServices of America is putting that same forward-thinking mindset into action across the board.

“What stands out about HomeServices is that it keeps building while it’s winning, and OnePoint is a good example. Bringing brokerage, mortgage, title and insurance together as one coordinated team is a real answer to what clients want,” says HomeServices of America Chief Marketing and Strategy Officer Brian Boero, who led one of the conference’s Super Sessions.

“The energy in the room at Stronger Together had that same appetite for what’s next…and that’s a sign of a company that plans to keep leading,” he adds.

The value of coming together: Ideas, connections and new perspectives

Attending HomeServices of America’s Stronger Together event along with 15 top-producing agents, Long Companies CEO Kevin Kaplan says that being able to bring together hundreds of the most successful real estate professionals from across the HomeServices network to learn, share ideas and build relationships is what ultimately makes the conference so valuable.

“What sets Stronger Together apart is that the focus goes beyond traditional sales skills and tactics. It’s about mindset, leadership, motivation and challenging yourself to think differently about your business and what’s possible,” says Kaplan, who believes that surrounding yourself with successful people who challenge and inspire you is one of the greatest opportunities for growth.

“Our agents came away energized, with new ideas, valuable connections and fresh perspectives on how to take their businesses to the next level,” he adds. “In my view, this was the best ‘Stronger Together’ event yet, and we’re already looking forward to 2027.”

For Elizabeth Nunan, president and CEO of Houlihan Lawrence, the conference was equally as powerful.

“Stronger Together was an outstanding conference from start to finish. The speakers were exceptional, the content was timely and relevant, and the opportunities to connect, network and share ideas made it even more valuable,” she says.

“Our agents left feeling inspired, energized and excited about what’s ahead. In fact, they’re already making plans for next year in Nashville. That’s always a pretty good indication of a great event,” adds Nunan.

For RISMedia Founder and CEO John Featherston, what stood out most was the energy and the positive, can-do attitude throughout the event.

“I had the privilege of attending the 4th Annual Stronger Together conference, and watching the attendees connect with so many other passionate real estate professionals reminded me just how valuable it is to meet face-to-face and collaborate with industry leaders to share knowledge and techniques—and why it’s so important to success in our industry,” says Featherston, who applauded Kelly and his team for delivering an informative, meaningful and well-received experience for attendees.

“When you couple the industry talents educating attendees—from Jimmy Burgess’ coaching insights and Brian Boero’s informative, practical information—with incredible guests such as serial entrepreneur and best-selling author Jesse Itzler and award-winning singer, songwriter and author Nicolle Galyon along with several other incredible speakers—Stronger Together was a smashing success,” he adds.

“Congratulations to Chris and his team for a job well done!”

The Mighty Nein Season 2 Officially Has Fans Worried About One Major Plot Change

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Prime Video teases a pirate-soaked second season, but will some major developments be left behind?

The Mighty Nein season 2 is officially set to return to our TV screens in March 2027. And while Critical Role fans may be a little put out by Amazon's new episode schedule, the season’s content has Critters excited for what’s to come from our band of lovable misfits.

However, there does seem to be a significant catch: Season 2 may be widening the gap between the show and the original Dungeons & Dragons campaign even further than season 1 did. While the first season already moved away from the source campaign in several ways, the second season could be taking those departures even further.

[Ed. note: This article contains spoilers for the tabletop campaign of The Mighty Nein.]

Prime Video’s announcement for season 2 of The Mighty Nein gave Critters a glimpse of what to expect. You can read the summary below.

“In Season Two, The Mighty Nein set off on a pirate adventure across the high seas as they face their complicated pasts and hurtle toward a treacherous future. While Fjord’s burgeoning powers tempt him down a dark path, the Beacon reveals its own enticing possibilities, and the group must choose between newfound magic and their friendship.”

On the surface, it looks like business as usual for the Mighty Nein (there are only seven of them, but we digress). However, some eagle-eyed Critters who have watched the original 141 episodes of the tabletop campaign have started to speculate what this new plot summary could mean for The Mighty Nein season 2’s story.

Last time, Critters left off with the Mighty Nein encountering the aasimar barbarian Yasha in the Zadash sewers after stealing the Luxon Beacon. Briefly free from the mind-control of Obann, a cambion, Yasha pleaded with the group to help her.

Though season 1 has already introduced several differences — for example, Yasha wasn’t introduced alongside Mollymauk as part of the Fletching and Moondrop Traveling Carnival of Curiosities troupe — the new season’s summary, combined with season 1’s ending, has fans speculating that the Iron Shepherds arc may be skipped entirely in favor of jumping straight to the Mighty Nein’s pirate arc.

The Iron Shepherds arc is a formative experience for the group, as it comes shortly after the Mighty Nein are given a task by the Gentleman (a crime boss in the city of Zadash) to head to Shadycreek Run and retrieve an ally of his. However, on the way there, the group is ambushed in the middle of the night, resulting in the capture of Fjord, Jester, and Yasha by a group of slavers known as The Iron Shepherds. When the rest of the group tries to deal with the slavers, they find themselves overwhelmed, with Mollymauk dying in the process.

The plot summary of Season 2, however, suggests nothing of the sort; its main focus is the pirate arc that comes afterward. Now, this could be Critical Role and Prime Video choosing to obscure the Iron Shepherds arc entirely to surprise new fans with what’s to come. That said, given how many changes Critical Role has already made to the campaign — and for what it’s worth, we think they are for the better — there’s also a chance the Iron Shepherds' encounter will be woven into the pirate arc. After all, the Mighty Nein make plenty of enemies during that arc, too, including Captain Avantika, who plays a major role in Fjord’s storyline. Could she be the one who ends up slaughtering Mollymauk?

It’s hard to predict what changes are coming to The Mighty Nein season 2, but given how much the first season changed from the original campaign, Critters should brace for even more in March 2027.

The Mighty Nein airs exclusively on Prime Video. Season 1 is available to watch now, with season 2 set to debut on March 24, 2027.

Huddersfield Town told to make January transfer move for ‘aggressive’ Barnsley star

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Optimism is beginning to build that Huddersfield Town can gain promotion and finally make their return to the Championship.

The Terriers currently sit at the summit of the third tier standings after taking 29 points from their opening 14 league games, while having a game in hand on some of their promotion rivals after not playing last weekend.

It had been a disappointing last couple of weeks in Yorkshire after Huddersfield were held at home in the league by Luton Town to end a run of four successive victories, while they also suffered defeat in the FA Cup at the hands of Exeter City, going down 2-1.

Martin Drury’s men were able to respond in their final group game of the EFL Trophy last week however, putting Liverpool's U21’s to the sword in a 4-0 victory that helped them top Northern Group H.

League action finally returns for Huddersfield this coming weekend, with the Terriers hosting Leicester City in a massive game for both teams towards the top end of the table.

Meanwhile, to solidify their place in the promotion picture after a hectic run of winter fixtures, the January transfer window could be the perfect time to make a statement signing and highlight their intent of returning to the second tier this season.

With that in mind, FLW’s Huddersfield Town fan pundit has backed the Terriers to complete a raid of a fellow Yorkshire club for an ‘aggressive’ star.

Much of Huddersfield Town’s excellent form leading into the festive period has been down to their recruitment in the forward areas, with the Terriers looking so much more potent compared to their previous couple of seasons in League One.

The addition of Derensilli Sanches Fernandes looks inspired at the moment, with the creative Cape Verde midfielder contributing seven goals and six assists from 14 matches, while the signing of frontman Ashley Fletcher has added much-needed experience and allowed Martin Drury to chop and change between him or Joe Taylor leading the line, who himself has 10 goals in 13 league games.

Martin Drury wasn't on the touchline for Huddersfield's recent EFL Trophy win, and the reasoning has sparked a reaction amongst the fanbase

Their quality moving forward and doggedness in their defensive duties has seen them lose just once all season so far in the league, with eight wins and five draws collected from their other 13 outings.

There’s such a feel-good factor at the Accu Stadium at the moment, with Huddersfield fans in awe of the job Drury is doing that could finally see them be back in the Championship setup next season.

Speaking to Football League World, Huddersfield Town fan pundit Graeme Rayner has urged the Terriers to raid fellow Yorkshire outfit Barnsley for winger Reyes Cleary in January, believing the addition of the 22-year-old can help the team be even more dangerous in attack, and that the player has ‘all the attributes’ to help the club achieve a top two spot come the end of the campaign.

When asked who Huddersfield should try and sign in January, Graeme told FLW: “I think it’s a player I’ve mentioned before in the summer, I would love us to take a really good hard look at Reyes Cleary, who plays for Barnsley.

“He’s a left-winger, 22-years-old, was the top assister in League One last year. He was playing in a pretty poor Barnsley side and still managed 15 assists and seven goals in 50 appearances.

“This season, he’s playing in a Barnsley side that are right at the foot of the table, they’re joint-bottom of the league, and he’s scored a couple and assisted one in a team that’s only scored 12 goals, he’s contributed in a way to a quarter of those goals.

“He’s quick, he’s capable, he’s clearly got a good finish on him, he’s aggressive, I think he’s six-foot, he’s a big unit, and got every kind of attribute that we’d want from someone, and what we lack at the moment is a really good option on the left that would give us what Derensili Sanches Fernandes gives us on the right.

As another transfer window rolls around, the future of Reyes Cleary hangs in the balance, and Huddersfield Town might be the club to finally take advantage.

And Huddersfield would seem the perfect place, with the Terriers currently so dangerous in the forward areas and having a line that rips apart opposition defences.

Not to mention that a left-winger is the one area that Huddersfield are missing at the moment despite having such an impressive attack, and you feel if that is addressed, then there is no way of stopping them blowing away most of their opponents and dominating the division for the rest of the campaign.

Huddersfield’s current setup would be a perfect one for Cleary to come into, with the 22-year-old able to use his pace, power, directness and clinical eye for goal to great effect, and it could be the missing piece that makes Huddersfield a shoo-in for promotion back to the Championship.

If the Terriers were able to pull this deal off in January, then their chances of automatic promotion are bound to considerably increase, and it’s a piece of transfer business that the club must get over the line if they are serious about achieving promotion.

DJI spinoff Amflow has spent the last couple of years shaking up the electric mountain bike (eMTB) market with ridiculously high-torque, lightweight models. Today, it’s bringing that same overengineered energy to pavement with the UE Series, its first urban commuter line.

The series consists of two models: the UE Carbon and UE Carbon Pro. Both pack 125Nm of torque, 1,100W of peak power, and an 8x max assist ratio capable of climbing 30 percent grades — all while operating quietly under 45dBA (quieter than a household refrigerator). A swappable 600Wh battery provides up to 132km (about 82 miles) of range and fast-charges from zero to 100 percent in 2 hours and 23 minutes.

While most city e-bikes deliver their power modestly, Amflow is strapping a mountain-bike-grade motor onto a 20.9 kg (46 pounds) upright step-through frame — and pricing it slightly below its flagship trail bikes. The UE Carbon starts at €3,099 (about $3,500 USD), while the Pro will sell for €3,499 ($3,900 USD).

But this isn’t just another commuter bike. With its proprietary Avinox drivetrain, DJI has been ahead of the curve in blending consumer gadget UX — features like wireless phone charging, Apple Watch proximity unlock, Find My integration, and camera controls — with serious hardware specs. That’s one of the reasons why The Verge’s own Thomas Ricker has been so bullish on the brand for the past few years.

With 125Nm of max torque and 1,100W of peak power, the Avinox M2 in the UE Series is slightly more dialed back from Amflow’s 150Nm flagship eMTBs. That said, 125Nm is still massive overkill for flat city streets — but it won’t hurt to have it when encountering any grades over 30 percent. A new Auto Boost mode automatically dumps extra power when starting at a traffic light or hitting a steep hill. And traction control will help prevent any spin-outs on wet asphalt.

The 600Wh removable battery is good for up 82 miles in Eco mode — and likely less than that while relying on higher levels of assist. It’s worth noting that the 1,100W peak power and 8x assist ratios will likely be software-capped depending on where you live and what regulations allow.

As a brand within the larger DJI ecosystem, Amflow loves to integrate a lot of tech into its bikes. A 1.4 inch touchscreen is integrated into the handlebars alongside a 15W wireless phone charger — plus a 20W USB-C charger, if you prefer. The cockpit also features plenty of Apple tie-ins, like the aforementioned Find My tracker and Apple Watch proximity unlocking. The UE can also pair with a heart rate monitor, allowing riders to set a target heart rate zone that will dictate how much pedal assist the bike provides. And, of course, there’s direct recording controls for DJI Osmo cameras, in case you like to film your rides.

Additional security is provided by a built-in 10mm hardened steel wheel lock keyed to match the removable battery. And if the bike is paired with “Lost Mode” in the Amflow app, the user can remotely brick the drive system if the bike ever gets stolen.

There’s plenty of commuter-friendly touches, like a 65mm suspension fork, suspension dropper seatpost, and heavy-duty racks rated for 20kg (44 pounds) on the front and 27kg (60 pounds) on the rear.

The bikes are on sale globally starting today via Amflow’s website and retail partners. The company says a smaller XS frame size is coming in 2027 for shorter riders.

📅 Nov 17, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

When Amazon CEO Andy Jassy became a parent for the first time 26 years ago, he didn’t want to choose between being a good worker and being a good father.

That left him with only one option.

“I realized that I had to change the way I worked,” Jassy recently said during an appearance at Amazon’s Bring Your Parents to Work Day. “[Otherwise,] I was either going to have to leave Amazon, or I was going to be a lousy dad.”

Jassy was used to working grueling hours—often staying at the office late into the night—and he wasn’t sure if he could do his job effectively on a tighter schedule.

“I didn’t know how I would do my job without [working all the time],” he said. “But like everything else, if you think about it and you rework it and you find ways to be more efficient and you make trade-offs, you can do it.”

One step was to ensure that, whenever he was at the office, he was using his time efficiently.

“I did spend a lot of time in the hallway talking to people, and I wasn’t always prioritizing the things that mattered most,” Jassy said. “It forced me to get more efficient in what I did.”

He also established rules to help him prioritize family: being home for dinner every day, having weekly breakfasts with each of his children, and maintaining a standing Tuesday date night with his wife.

The challenge Jassy faced will likely be familiar to any parent struggling to balance career ambitions with responsibilities at home. And it is increasingly common among men, a growing number of whom say they are interested in being parents or caregivers. 

In one recent survey, over two-thirds of men said they consider caregiving to be as important as paid work and that both parents should contribute equally—yet only 40% of men who are caregivers said they shared the responsibility equally with their partners.

In the same survey, nearly two-thirds of male caregivers said work obligations interfered with their family time. A third reported having “no control” over their schedules, and only 40% said they had a hybrid or remote work arrangement. (Amazon issued a five-day RTO policy in 2024.)

Meanwhile, the study found that male caregivers are increasingly missing work or cutting back on their work hours. The percentage of men who care for adults or children with extra needs who reported missing work rose from 64% in 2019 to 76% in 2026.  

And, as Fast Company reported recently, men in some states are filing at least as many claims for parental leave as mothers.

For fathers who want to keep their career ambitions and still be equal parents, Jassy said there may be no one-size-fits-all solution.

“There are times you have to be flexible with what’s happening in your life and at work,” he said. “But it’s doable if you want to do it.”
