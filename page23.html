2 AI Robotics Stocks That Could Help Set You Up for Life

📅 Nov 12, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

While AI models and chips take center stage, the AI robotics boom is silently building.

While chips and AI models have been receiving most of the attention, robotics is a long-term growth opportunity that can mint new millionaires. Robots can help with a wide range of tasks, including security, physical labor, and military.

Grandview Research projects a 32% CAGR for the AI robotics industry through 2033, and these two robotics stocks are well-positioned for that opportunity.

All robots need GPUs to operate, and Nvidia (NVDA +2.87%) remains the gold standard. It's leagues ahead of the competition, with revenue surging by 106% year-over-year in its fiscal 2027 second quarter.

The high revenue growth also comes with rising profit margins. Net income was up by 126% year-over-year, and net profit margins reached 62%. Nvidia was able to return $26 billion to shareholders that quarter and guided for 12.2% sequential growth in its fiscal 2027 third quarter.

Despite the strong quarter, Nvidia has stumbled significantly over the past month. The main headwind for the company is Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's two affairs. News about an affair with a spy was quickly followed by details about a separate affair with a Nvidia executive.

These details are affecting Nvidia's stock price because the company's board is reportedly preparing to oust the CEO from his position. Investors tend to get jittery when there is uncertainty around a company's leadership.

However, Nvidia's AI chips are already the preferred choice for many companies, and the company is far more than Jensen. Just as Apple (AAPL +2.04%) has continued to thrive after Steve Jobs' passing and Tim Cook stepping down as CEO, I anticipate a similar story for Nvidia, assuming Huang is actually removed from his position. If Huang manages to stay put, the entire dip over the past week would be hard to justify.

The robotics industry will need plenty of chips to support the long-term buildout. Nvidia has the strongest reputation and delivers better results than other GPUs. Its market share should be safe regardless of recent developments.

Just as humanoid robots need GPUs, they also need memory chips. Micron (MU +11.89%) specializes in that part of the trade and has delivered solid fundamental results that justify its shares almost tripling year-to-date.

The company's high-bandwidth memory chips have become a staple for many hyperscalers and data centers. This level of reach, combined with ongoing memory chip shortages, helped Micron boost its revenue by 30.8% sequentially in its fiscal 2026 fourth quarter.

The chipmaker crushed guidance by delivering $54.23 billion in revenue during that quarter instead of the expected $50 billion. Micron anticipates a stronger fiscal 2027 and guided for $61.5 billion at the midpoint for Q1 FY27.

Humanoid robots can eventually become a big part of Micron's fundamental growth. These robots require robust data centers, and all of those facilities need plenty of memory chips. However, each of these same humanoid robots needs its own memory chips inside the physical unit to function properly. Each humanoid robot increases the need for memory chips for physical units and the actual data centers.

While you can guess which humanoid robot will be the biggest winner, Micron is essentially selling shovels during the gold rush. All of the humanoid robot start-ups will need memory chips, including the ones that turn into tech giants in the future.

In the meantime, Micron is already producing substantial revenue growth while guiding for even better results in future quarters. All of that momentum also comes with high net profit margins. That figure came to 69.5% in the most recent quarter, showing that Micron keeps a large majority of its revenue.

A 4 forward P/E ratio is the icing on the cake that highlights Micron's low valuation compared to most tech stocks. It's trading like a bank stock despite delivering some of the highest growth rates in the entire stock market.

Mississippi State’s 41 points without Kamario Taylor explain his reported $7.5 million deal to return in 2027

📅 Nov 12, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Kamario Taylor is finalizing a deal to return to the Mississippi State Bulldogs in 2027 that will make him the highest-paid quarterback in college football, On3's Chris Low and Pete Nakos reported Wednesday. The deal is worth $7.5 million and could climb past $9 million with incentives. Taylor has played less than a quarter of football since Oct. 3, and Mississippi State is paying him like a player who never left the field.

With Taylor healthy, Mississippi State opened 4-0 and scored 172 points, an average of 43 per game, capped by a 31-24 win over the No. 19 Missouri Tigers on Sept. 26. He hurt his toe in that game, played through it in a 56-23 loss to the Alabama Crimson Tide, sat out at the LSU Tigers and left the Oklahoma Sooners game in the first quarter. The school announced on Oct. 27 that he would miss the rest of the season with a foot injury requiring surgery and said he is expected to return at 100 percent.

Oct 24, 2026; Starkville, Mississippi, USA; Mississippi State Bulldogs quarterback Kamario Taylor (1) passes the ball during warm ups prior to the game against the Oklahoma Sooners at Davis Wade Stadium at Scott Field. Mandatory Credit: Petre Thomas-Imagn Images

In the four games Taylor either missed or did not finish, Mississippi State scored 41 points total. The Bulldogs were shut out 31-0 at LSU, lost 23-15 to Oklahoma, managed three points in a 31-3 loss to the Texas Longhorns and dropped a 25-23 decision at home to the Vanderbilt Commodores last Saturday. A team that climbed to No. 16 in the AP poll at the end of September now sits at 4-5 on a five-game losing streak, and those 41 points are the clearest measure available of what Taylor is worth to it.

ESPN's Pete Thamel posted Wednesday that the deal was expected to be completed later in the day and called it significant for head coach Jeff Lebby's portal recruiting and roster building. He then passed along what a source told ESPN about the effect Taylor has on other players.

"One of the thing we realized is the level of quality players who are intrigued by joining Kamario at Mississippi State," the source said.

Lebby heads into the transfer window with a five-game skid on the books, a 5-8 finish in 2025 and a team that needs two wins in its last three games just to reach .500. A signed quarterback is the one thing a portal target can see on a depth chart before he commits, and Taylor's 4-0 start is the most recent picture of what this program looks like with a full-strength offense.Getting the number done in November puts that picture in front of recruits before anyone else can make Taylor an offer.

Oct 24, 2026; Starkville, Mississippi, USA; Mississippi State Bulldogs quarterback Kamario Taylor (1) walks off the field after the game against the Oklahoma Sooners at Davis Wade Stadium at Scott Field. Mandatory Credit: Petre Thomas-Imagn Images

Taylor has said repeatedly that he intends to finish his career at Mississippi State. When his season ended in late October, plenty of fans online predicted he would sit out and transfer, and the outside interest in a healthy Taylor would have been real once the portal opened. Wednesday's figure turns his stated loyalty into a contract before it ever had to survive that test.

Mississippi State hosts the Auburn Tigers on Saturday, then finishes with the Tennessee Tech Golden Eagles at home and the Ole Miss Rebels on the road on Nov. 27.

Former Michigan interim HC Biff Poggi sends message about Wolverines that leaves out coaches

📅 Nov 12, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

The Michigan Wolverines are 5-4 and 2-4 in the Big Ten after a 15-6 home loss to the Michigan State Spartans on Saturday, a game in which the Spartans kicked five field goals, forced four turnovers and recorded five sacks to beat Michigan for the first time since 2021. On Wednesday, the man who coached many of those players through last season's bowl game went on X to speak up for them.

"I know the kids on this Michigan team. It is not going as they hoped. However, they will not be broken, discouraged or quit. There is a lot of toughness and determination in that group. Selling them short is a mistake. So many complex issues go into a season. GoBlue. I believe," Biff Poggi wrote.

Michigan acting head coach Biff Poggi walks onto the field for second half against Central Michigan at Michigan Stadium in Ann Arbor on Saturday, Sept. 13, 2025.

Poggi was Michigan's associate head coach when Sherrone Moore was fired last December, ran the program as interim head coach through the bowl game and now works as a Big Ten Network studio analyst. He has said the search that followed came down to him and Kyle Whittingham, and that he believed he had the job.

This is the second time in three weeks Poggi has asked Michigan fans for patience, and the two posts are built differently. After the 13-6 overtime loss to the Indiana Hoosiers left Michigan at 4-3, he opened with a defense of everyone in the building.

"To all of my Michigan friends. First, GoBLUE. Second, no one is getting fired. Please relax. The team is 4-3. It is a new staff, a very good staff," Poggi wrote in late October, adding that the worst thing Michigan supporters could do was abandon the players and coaches when they struggled.

Wednesday's post names the players, their toughness and their determination, and stops there. Whittingham, offensive coordinator Jason Beck and the rest of the staff go unmentioned, and the line about complex issues leaves open where those issues come from.

Earlier this month, in the same week he discussed finishing second to Whittingham, Poggi said publicly that Michigan's offense was not playing Michigan football. He defended Bryce Underwood, the quarterback Michigan benched after the Indiana loss in favor of Tommy Carr, against the criticism Underwood had taken, and he pointed to scheme and the use of personnel as the problems. That is a view of the offense that places the responsibility with the people who design it. Michigan's offense then produced one touchdown against Michigan State, with 1:35 left in the game.

Poggi did not criticize the staff in the post, and nothing in it should be treated as a shot at Whittingham. A former interim coach who has said he expected to get the job, and who has already questioned the scheme, chose to vouch for the roster and left the coaches out. Three weeks earlier, at 4-3, he vouched for both.

The replies under Poggi's post went where the post itself did not, straight to the head coach. One fan pointed to Ohio State's handling of the 2011 season, when the Buckeyes gave Luke Fickell the job for a year after Jim Tressel resigned.

"Can't wrap my head around why they didn't pull a Fickell and sign you to 1-year for these kids while they got their s— together in the athletics department and spent the last half of the year on a national coaching search. It's just beyond me," @TrueCrimeKeith wrote.

Others skipped the history and asked for a change now. "Sometimes complexity can have simple solutions. It's never fun to demand for change in someone's job but it's time. The change needs to happen for those kids," @UMichTakes wrote.

Another account, @KYVAMIguy, wrote: "I believe if we rid this program of Whit and bring in someone who gives a s— about Michigan, it's tradition, and its players, we can save the roster next year and go back to competitive football again."

A fourth reply called for Whittingham to be fired immediately.

All four replies accepted Poggi's case for the players and turned the criticism on Whittingham, the coach his post left unnamed, which is the conversation Poggi stayed out of on Wednesday.

Sep 20, 2025; Lincoln, Nebraska, USA; Michigan Wolverines acting head coach Biff Poggi watches during the fourth quarter against the Nebraska Cornhuskers at Memorial Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Dylan Widger-Imagn Images

Michigan visits the Oregon Ducks at 3:30 p.m. ET on Saturday on FS1, hosts the unbeaten UCLA Bruins on Nov. 21 and closes at the Ohio State Buckeyes on Nov. 28. One more win makes the Wolverines bowl eligible. Three losses would leave them 5-7 in Whittingham's first season, with the players Poggi backed on Wednesday carrying the record and the coaches he no longer mentioned carrying the questions.

Why Alabama’s real Vanderbilt concern may be closer to home than Kalen DeBoer suggests

📅 Nov 12, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Kalen DeBoer used his Wednesday night Hey Coach radio show to push back on the questions he has fielded all week about the Vanderbilt Commodores, the 4-5 team the No. 3 Alabama Crimson Tide visit on Saturday in Nashville, and he aimed his answer at the people asking them.

"I can tell by the questions as I go through interviews throughout the course of the week that everyone outside of the building hasn't watched Vanderbilt. They haven't watched them. Our guys understand what it looks like. They're preparing for it, and having a good week of practice right now," DeBoer said, as relayed by Bama 24/7's Mike Rodak.

Oct 24, 2026; Tuscaloosa, AL, USA; Alabama head coach Kalen DeBoer leaves the field after Alabamas win at Saban Field at Bryant Denny Stadium. Alabama defeated Texas A&M 31-20. Mandatory Credit: Gary Cosby Jr.-Tuscaloosa News

The last time Alabama went to Nashville, it arrived ranked No. 1 after beating the Georgia Bulldogs and lost 40-35 on Oct. 5, 2024. DeBoer brought that game up with his players in the visitors' locker room at Tiger Stadium on Saturday night, minutes after a 31-13 win over the LSU Tigers.

"A lot of these guys don't have the memory from a couple years ago. Big win, then going up to Vanderbilt," DeBoer told Crimson Tide Sports Network radio postgame.

Vanderbilt opened 3-0, lost five straight and then won 25-23 at the Mississippi State Bulldogs last Saturday, and part of that losing streak supports DeBoer. Two of those five losses were decided by 10 points or fewer, 21-15 at the Auburn Tigers and 31-21 at home against the Arkansas Razorbacks. At the Kentucky Wildcats, then ranked No. 12, the Commodores trailed only 24-17 going into the fourth quarter before losing 34-17.

Georgia beat Vanderbilt 38-14 in Athens, and the Ole Miss Rebels put up 52 points in Nashville. Vanderbilt also ranks next-to-last in the SEC in rushing at 112.1 yards per game, and its 160 rushing yards at Mississippi State were its most against an FBS opponent all season.

A team that hangs around into the second half against SEC opponents is a fair warning for a coach to issue, and DeBoer has the 2024 result to justify issuing it loudly. The stronger case for concern, though, sits inside his own building.

LSU outgained Alabama 457-285 on Saturday and crossed midfield on each of its first seven drives, and Alabama won because its defense allowed 10 points on four red-zone trips. After topping 124 rushing yards in each of its first six games, Alabama has not reached 80 in any of the last three.

Vanderbilt kept Kentucky within one score for three quarters with a defense that will now face an Alabama offense that has stopped producing on the ground. It also has a kicker in Brock Taylor who went 6-for-6 on field goals at Mississippi State, including two from 54 yards or longer, which is how a 4-5 team keeps a close game close.

Kickoff is at 12:45 p.m. ET on SEC Network. Alabama finishes the regular season against the Chattanooga Mocs and Auburn.

Philip Rivers reportedly accepts NC State head coaching job ahead of Friday board vote

📅 Nov 12, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

The NC State Wolfpack board of trustees is expected to finalize a contract Friday that makes Philip Rivers the program's next head football coach, ESPN's Pete Thamel and Mark Schlabach reported Wednesday. The full board meets Friday morning in Raleigh, and the agenda ends with a closed session, according to WRAL News.

Rivers, 44, is in his sixth season as head coach at St. Michael Catholic High School in Fairhope, Alabama, a program he started from nothing and took to the state semifinals in 2024 and 2025. He has never coached in college or the NFL. Per ESPN's report, he told NC State he wanted to finish the high school season before anything moved forward, and that season ended last Friday with a 42-35 home playoff loss to The Glenwood School.

Health insurance reset drives Philip Rivers comeback (Fox Sports)

Asked afterward if he would follow his son Gunner to Raleigh, Rivers kept it short. "I don't know that," Rivers said to reporters in Alabama. Pressed by WRAL on a next step in the talks, he went a little further. "There could be. I think that the potential next step would involve all of those things you referenced," Rivers said.

NC State fired Dave Doeren on Oct. 24, a day after a 31-28 loss at the Stanford Cardinal dropped the Wolfpack to 3-4, and handed the interim job to running backs coach and special teams coordinator Todd Goebbel. Doeren leaves as the winningest coach in school history at 98-74, and the school owes him $9.75 million in base salary on a contract that ran through December 2029. Goebbel's team beat the California Golden Bears 31-10 and then lost 41-3 to the Duke Blue Devils, so Rivers takes over a 4-5 roster with the Syracuse Orange, Florida State Seminoles and North Carolina Tar Heels still to play.

Rivers emerged as the favorite early in the search and met with athletic director Boo Corrigan and other NC State officials in Alabama on Nov. 4, per WRAL. Russell Wilson, another former Wolfpack quarterback, was also in discussions for the job before the search settled on Rivers.

Gunner Rivers committed to NC State on Feb. 23 and is ranked 168th in the SC Next 300, though his senior season at St. Michael ended with a knee injury.

Dec 14, 2025; Seattle, Washington, USA; Indianapolis Colts head coach Shane Steichen, left, talks with quarterback Philip Rivers (17) during a second quarter timeout against the Seattle Seahawks at Lumen Field. Mandatory Credit: Kevin Ng-Imagn Images

Philip Rivers threw for 13,484 yards and 95 touchdowns at NC State from 2000 to 2003 under Chuck Amato, who died last month at 80, and took the Wolfpack to four straight bowl games. The 2002 team went 11-3, still the only double-digit win season in program history, and NC State has not won an ACC title since 1979.

Rivers interviewed for the Buffalo Bills head coaching job in January before withdrawing, and he came out of retirement last fall to start three games for the Indianapolis Colts after injuries to Daniel Jones and Riley Leonard. NC State hosts Syracuse at noon ET Saturday with Goebbel still running the sideline.

The HBO Max and Paramount+ bundle comes with one tech platform within a year, built with AI tools. David Ellison says AI could change film more than CGI did.

Skydance will sell HBO Max and Paramount+ as a bundle from early 2027. It also plans to merge the two services’ technology within a year, using AI tools to move faster. The company set out the plan on 9 November with its first results since Paramount bought Warner Bros. Discovery.

The details are in the shareholder letter from chief executive David Ellison and co-CEO Ynon Kreiz. Skydance did not give a price for the HBO Max and Paramount+ bundle. It said it would share more about the bundle and its streaming plans “in the coming months”.

Moving Pluto TV, Paramount’s free streaming service, onto the Paramount+ platform took about 18 months. Skydance says this one will be quicker.

“We expect to bring Paramount+ and HBO Max together much faster, aiming to align our backend systems within a year of closing,” Skydance wrote.

The figures cover Paramount only, because the Warner Bros. Discovery deal closed on 6 October. Revenue rose 5% to $7bn and adjusted EBITDA, a profit measure that leaves out some costs, was $1bn.

On the earnings call, Ellison said AI would “significantly” reshape the business, The Verge reported.

He compared AI with the arrival of computer-generated imagery in the early 1990s, which gave the industry Jurassic Park and Terminator 2.

“It’s hard to imagine that AI is not going to have a more significant impact than that,” Ellison said.

Terminator cost $4M to make, he said, and a shot-for-shot remake would cost $60M to $70M today. AI tools could make such budgets “more digestible”, he said. He also said AI “will never be a replacement” for artists.

AI also sits inside the cost plan. Skydance has promised at least $6bn a year in savings within three years. The letter lists AI workflows and AI coding tools among the ways to get there.

Pluto TV has about 75 million monthly active users, the letter says. That is fewer than the 89.6 million it had in 2025, according to an internal presentation that Business Insider reported on. Two Paramount streaming employees told Business Insider that a redesign in September may have played a part.

Fox-owned Tubi, a rival free service, has grown to 110 million monthly users, Business Insider reported. YouTube took 15.8% of US TV viewing in August, according to Nielsen. Skydance calls itself the second-largest media distributor by share of US TV time, after YouTube.

The deal left Skydance with $87bn of gross debt and about $10bn in cash after closing, the letter says. It aims to bring net debt down to three times its annual adjusted EBITDA by the end of 2029.

“The newly merged Skydance comes with massive debt that remains at the center of concern over the company’s ability to profitably grow,” Forrester analyst Mike Proulx said before the results, Business Insider reported.

The same day as the results, Skydance announced that James Gunn and Peter Safran would step down as heads of DC Studios, the Associated Press reported. Jeremy Latcham, a former Marvel Studios executive, takes over on 1 December. Gunn is finishing Man of Tomorrow, the next Superman film.

The bundle plan comes five weeks after Skydance closed the deal and offered staff buyouts in October. Elon Musk is among the backers, having invested about $1bn in Ellison’s company.

6 Upcoming Netflix Thriller Shows, Ranked by Hype

📅 Nov 12, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Netflix has never had trouble making thriller shows. The problem is that the streamer releases so many of them that it can be hard to keep up, since plenty of great ones vanish from the Top 10 within a week, replaced by the next viral hit. The real challenge is making one that people still talk about after the binge cycle is over, and that takes more than a twisty premise and a cliffhanger at the end of every episode.

Lately, Netflix has been aiming much higher. Some of the most acclaimed writers in TV are bringing their next big ideas to the streamer, and they’re pairing them with casts that would normally headline a prestige film. The result is an intentional, ambitious slate that could finally prove that the streamer’s thrillers are worth more than one lazy weekend on the couch. Here are six upcoming Netflix thriller shows, ranked by hype.

The Twin has a premise that sounds simple until it starts to spiral. Jamie Dornan plays identical twin brothers, Adam and Lee, in this six-episode crime noir set against the moody backdrop of the Scottish Highlands. Adam is stuck in a cold, loveless marriage with Nicola (Mackenzie Davis), and the return of his estranged brother throws their already fragile life into chaos. When tragedy strikes, a dark secret forces the twins’ lives to become dangerously tangled, and Lee and Nicola soon find themselves drawn to each other. However, they know their time together could run out at any moment. The show, based on the Norwegian series Twin, arrives on Netflix on January 1, 2027.

Twin stories are a thriller staple, but The Twin seems more interested in guilt and desire than in the typical identity-swap narrative. Dornan has already proven he’s great at playing men with something to hide thanks to his performance in The Fall, and playing two versions of the same face gives the actor plenty to work with. The love story at the center of it all only complicates things further and gives the audience one more reason to stay until the very end. Netflix is giving the series a New Year’s Day slot, which says a lot about how much faith the streamer has in it. If it holds, The Twin will be the perfect slow-burn mystery to kick off 2027.

The mere idea of the creator of Bodyguard reuniting with Richard Madden has viewers hooked on Trinity, and the show isn’t even out yet. The series, helmed by the brilliant Jed Mercurio, follows Gugu Mbatha-Raw as Commander Katherine Decker, a decorated U.S. Navy officer, who becomes romantically involved with the charismatic Secretary of Defense, Webb Preston (Madden), only to discover that he may be at the center of a dangerous conspiracy. As a nuclear crisis looms, Katherine has to figure out who she can trust before it’s too late. Marcia Gay Harden and Bruce Greenwood round out the cast, and the series premieres on December 1.

Now, Mercurio is a master of keeping the audience on the edge of their seats. His shows are known for tense, twisty conversations where a single line can change everything, and Bodyguard proved he knows exactly how to use Madden’s charm to keep viewers guessing. This time, the roles are flipped, since Madden is the powerful man under suspicion instead of the loyal protector. Putting a romance at the heart of a political conspiracy is also an interesting choice that gives the drama an emotional center the characters can keep coming back to. Trinity is all set to live up to the fans' expectations of Mercurio and become Netflix’s next big obsession with the holiday season.

The Boys from Brazil has one of the most chilling premises in thriller history, and Netflix is bringing it back with a cast that is almost too good to be true. The show is based on Ira Levin’s 1976 novel, which also inspired the 1978 film starring Gregory Peck and Laurence Olivier. The five-part limited series follows Nazi hunter Yakov Liebermann (Jeremy Strong). Liebermann stumbles onto evidence that a notorious Nazi doctor, long believed to be dead, is alive and orchestrating a mysterious plan to bring about a Fourth Reich. Gillian Anderson, Daniel Brühl, August Diehl, Shira Haas, and Lizzy Caplan co-star, and the series is being written by The Crown creator Peter Morgan.

All of that already sets The Boys from Brazil up to be a massive success. Anyone who knows the book knows how shocking the twist behind the plot is, and a limited series has far more room to explore it than the original film did. The story slowly builds as Liebermann realizes that dozens of ordinary, unrelated men in different countries are being murdered one by one. Figuring out what connects them is the heart of the mystery. Peter Morgan has spent years turning real history into gripping drama, and Strong is the perfect fit for Liebermann, a man whose obsession with justice consumes his entire life. Netflix hasn’t set a release date for the show yet, but The Boys from Brazil already looks like one of the streamer’s most ambitious and unsettling thrillers to date.

After years of terrifying audiences with Black Mirror, Charlie Brooker is using his sharp wit in a gritty detective drama. Blackmere follows the tormented Detective John Blackmere (Paddy Considine), who leaves the rain-soaked northern city of Bleakford for London to avenge his partner’s death and catch a ritualistic serial killer before they run out of victims. The cast of the show, which arrives on Netflix in early 2027, also features Georgina Campbell, Lena Headey and Rory Kinnear.

There is a catch, though. Netflix describes Blackmere as a deadly serious crime thriller, but early details make it clear that Brooker intends for the show to poke fun at every cliché in the genre. That makes the series one of the most unusual entries on this list, because it works as both a parody and a true mystery. Brooker is great at satirizing his subject matter, and gritty British detective shows give him plenty to work with. The showrunner has spent more than a decade proving that he understands what makes audiences squirm, and there is no doubt that Blackmere will be all over social media the moment it drops.

The Body sounds like a wild mix of coming-of-age drama and Catholic horror, with an extremely intriguing premise. The eight-episode series, created by Quinn Shephard, follows a group of students at a Catholic girls’ school whose strange visions soon plunge their small town into chaos. The whole thing starts as a ritual but quickly spirals into something much darker, as the line between faith and hysteria begins to blur. Kristina Bogic, Sara Boustany and Geena Meszaros lead the young cast, with Sofia Wylie also starring.

The series premieres on Netflix on November 11 and aims to put a spin on all the nightmares of being a teenager. Shepherd’s show takes obvious inspiration from similar films, including Carrie, Jennifer’s Body, and Heavenly Creatures, all of which talk about the dark side of growing up as a girl, where friendship and obsession can be hard to tell apart. The Body sets out to capture that same mix of camp and dread, and if it does that right, it can easily become a Netflix cult favorite.

Rabbit, Rabbit brings together some of TV's most exciting names for a compelling hostage thriller. The series follows Adam Driver as J-Will, an escaped convict who is cornered by police at a truck stop in Illinois and takes hostages to bargain for his freedom. Opposite him is Regina Hall as Poppy, a veteran FBI crisis negotiator trained in a technique known as “tactical empathy.” As the standoff between the two drags on, it turns into a twisted social experiment among the captives and an emotional battle of wits. Filming took place in New Jersey earlier this year, but the series does not have a release date yet.

The talent behind the camera is what makes Rabbit, Rabbit the most hyped show on this list. It’s written by Peter Craig, who co-wrote The Batman and Top Gun: Maverick, and every episode is directed by Philip Barantini, whose Adolescence became one of Netflix’s biggest phenomena. Barantini has a gift for trapping audiences in a single, suffocating moment, and a hostage standoff is the perfect playground for that style. It’s also Driver’s first major TV role since Girls, and watching him go head-to-head with Hall sounds like an acting showdown for the ages.
