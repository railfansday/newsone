Will we see a shock at the Europa-Park Stadion, or will we see the points gap between both sides continue to widen?

Let’s begin with the home side, SC Freiburg.

Freiburg have been on a burner to commence the 2026/27 season with 5 wins, 3 draws and a solitary loss only coming against champions Bayern München, along with a respectable +8GD (18 goals scored). In addition to this, Freiburg ensured their Pokal survival by staving off recently relegated Wolfsburg with a last-gasp winner by Keisuke Gotō. The team from Baden-Württemberg also had a healthy start to their Conference League campaign with 2 wins and 1 loss, their most recent result being a 1-0 away win against Trabzonspor.

To go back to the Bundesliga, Freiburg managed to overcome a 1-0 deficit to Augsburg to come away from Bavaria with a 2-1 win in their most recent match. A win against Union Berlin would see them not only move back up to 3rd, but also come to within 2 points of league leaders Borussia Dortmund and Bayern München.

Apart from their 3-1 loss to Bayern München, Freiburg have been otherwise flawless at home and look poised to strike while the iron’s hot as they push for a first-ever UCL qualification.

A duo to watch this match will certainly be the strike partnership of Igor Matanović and Yuito Suzuki, who account for 8 goals (4 goals each) - nearly half of Freiburg’s total goals this season.

SC Freiburg will be without Florent Muslija and Patrick Osterhage.

Ironically, the club known as “Eisern Union” (Iron Union) seem to have let their iron cool down as they sit near the bottom of the table as one of the league's most out of form teams.

Since shocking the footballing world by qualifying for the UCL in the 2022/23 season, Union Berlin has in fact looked quite malleable in recent seasons (I promise that’s the last metal-related joke I will make!), with this season shaping up to be a relegation survival battle for the Berliners.

With only 1 win in 8 games thus far (4D, 3L), Union squandered their previous Bundesliga matchup against 1. FC Köln at home, losing out on some much needed points with direct relegation rivals. This followed a 5-0 battering by that very same Köln team in the Pokal less than a week prior.

The bad news for Union continues if we look at their away form thus far - in just 3 matches, Union has drawn 1 and lost 2, with a shocking -11 goal differential (1 scored, 12 conceded), by far the worst in the league.

With local rivals Hertha Berlin having just beaten top-of-the-table rivals Wolfsburg and continuing their flawless run in the 2. Bundesliga (10W, 1D, and a +16 GD), the pressure is surely beginning to mount on Union securing their status as Berlin’s best team.

Union Berlin will miss the likes of Andrej Iic, Stanley Nsoki, Andrik Markgraf and Robert Skov. Felix Uduokhai remains out via suspension after his red card against Augsburg.

To give Union fans a bit of hope, Freiburg have had a shaky record in recent years against the team from Berlin.

Union have been flawless against Freiburg in their last 7 matches (4 wins, 3 draws, and outscoring Freiburg 9:4), with their last loss occurring nearly 4 years ago to the day. Union is also currently on a 2-game winning streak against Freiburg when playing in the Europa-Park Stadion.

This does come with a caveat however, which is that both sides were a lot closer in regards to form and table positioning in the same fixture last season - in 2025/26, Freiburg was 8th (34 points, -5 GD) while Union was 11th (28 points, -12 GD). This time, 16th place Union (7 points, -12 GD) will really have to punch up if they want to win against 4th place Freiburg (16 points, +8 GD).

If Union Berlin are to regain their form, perhaps they can make it happen against Freiburg and potentially eke out another win to pick up some much needed points and create some separation at the bottom of the table.

Fixtures and standings as Nations Championship returns

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

The Nations Championship resumed on Friday with Ireland securing a 42-12 win over Argentina, as the battle for continental supremacy heats up.

After three rounds of home games for southern hemisphere nations in July, the northern hemisphere teams are the hosts for the final three rounds.

Ireland's win moved them top of the northern standings until France thrashed Fiji 54-17 on Saturday to regain their lead with 17 points from four matches.

Scotland were outclassed by New Zealand in the second half and are in third place in the northern table following a 43-21 defeat at Murrayfield.

Fourth-placed England have 10 points and play Australia - who are third in the southern standings - at Allianz Stadium on Sunday.

Wales overcame Japan 28-21 to move level on points with England but have a vastly inferior points difference.

Bottom-side Italy, who lost all three of their away games in the summer, were outgunned 66-7 by South Africa on Saturday.

The Springboks continue to lead New Zealand on points difference, with both teams having won all four of their games with bonus points.

The world champions face Six Nations champions France in a huge game in Paris on Friday.

Player-coach rift report will only galvanise England - George

The Nations Championship pits the northern hemisphere teams who compete in the Six Nations - England, Ireland, Scotland, Wales, France and Italy - against southern hemisphere giants South Africa, New Zealand, Australia and Argentina, plus invited sides Fiji and Japan.

All teams will play each of the six in the opposing hemisphere once, with three rounds of fixtures staged in July and another three in November.

Their results will rank the teams within their own hemisphere, from one to six.

On the final weekend in November, there is a three-day play-off event staged at Allianz Stadium in Twickenham. The sixth-placed team in the northern hemisphere plays off against the equivalent in the southern hemisphere standings and so on, culminating in the two top-ranked sides taking each other on.

The winner of that final match is crowned the inaugural Nations Championship winner.

There is also a parallel, Ryder-Cup style hemisphere title. The winner of each of the matches on the play-offs weekend will earn one point for their hemisphere, except for the contest between the two top-ranked teams which delivers two.

The first hemisphere to earn four points over the weekend will be crowned winners.

Is a Market Crash Coming? Warren Buffett's Warning Is Ringing Loud and Clear.

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Buffett has successfully invested through all kinds of markets over decades and is worth listening to.

Is a market crash coming? That's the question on many investors' minds today as the strong bull market continues. The S&P 500 (^GSPC +0.31%) has more than doubled over the past four years, and it's up 12% year to date. Artificial intelligence (AI) euphoria is still driving the market, but there are signs that suggest a crash could be on the way.

Consider what Warren Buffett recently said about the state of the market today.

Warren Buffett is arguably the greatest investor of his generation. He recently stepped down as CEO of Berkshire Hathaway (BRKA -0.01%)(BRKB -0.25%) after leading it for 60 years, during which time its stock gained 5,502,284%, vs. 39,054% for the S&P 500.

Warren Buffett. Image source: Getty Images.

Buffett is a value investor, which means that he invests in undervalued stocks, or stocks trading beneath their intrinsic values. However, the most important thing he looks for in a stock is a great business. As he puts it, "It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price." Note the word fair, which suggests that the stock isn't overpriced.

He has invested in all kinds of circumstances, including long bull markets, major crashes, high inflation, and just about everything else. Admirers look to him for guidance, and he gives advice freely.

What he said this past July should make every investor pay attention. In an interview with CNBC, he said, "It's tough to find values when everybody is preferring gambling." That was a follow-up to a remark he made in an interview in May, when he said, "I've compared the markets to a church with a casino attached."

Warren Buffett would be the first person to admit he doesn't know what's coming next. But decades of experience give him an edge in understanding how the market works.

Since this bull market started four years ago, Buffett built up Berkshire Hathaway's cash pile to its highest ever, nearly $400 billion. He was a net seller of stocks for many quarters while the market was rising and investors were pushing up some AI stocks to outrageous levels.

The entire time, Berkshire Hathaway found something to buy every quarter. However, when the market becomes very expensive and it seems people are gambling, it's even more important to pay attention to valuation and look for market bargains.

A market crash might be around the corner, and investors should consider emulating Buffett by having cash ready and investing in fairly priced stocks.

What activating Mack Hollins means for the Patriots

The writing was on the wall, and on Saturday the New England Patriots made it official. Mack Hollins has returned to the team's 53-man roster after spending the previous four weeks on its injured reserve list; the veteran wide receiver was sent there after sustaining a calf injury in Week 4 versus the Buffalo Bills.

Let's assess what Hollins' activation means for the Patriots ahead of their Week 9 contest versus the Green Bay Packers.

Despite returning to practice earlier this week to open his 21-day window to return from injured reserve, Hollins initially remained on the list. While this is standard procedure — teams want to take a look at players at practice first before making any decisions regarding activation — it did leave the Patriots potentially three top-level wide receivers short heading into the weekend.

Romeo Doubs remains on IR with a shoulder injury, while A.J. Brown was ruled out for a second straight game due to a knee issue. Had Hollins missed Sunday's game as well, Drake Maye's supporting cast would again have looked suspect, particularly considering running back TreVeyon Henderson's medical status and tight end Hunter Henry being questionable for the contest as well.

Hollins cannot patch all of those holes by himself, but having him available does upgrade New England's overall defensive depth.

With Hollins back on the 53-man team, the Patriots now have five wide receivers rostered. In addition, their practice squad features three wideouts — one of whom, Ryan Miller, was elevated to the game day roster against the Miami Dolphins last week.

53-man roster (5): A.J. Brown (1), Mack Hollins (13), DeMario Douglas (3), Kyle Williams (18 | KR), Efton Chism III (86)

Practice squad: Kyle Dixon (83), Chandler Brayboy (81), Ryan Miller (11)

On paper, the Patriots are nearing full strength at the position again. That is particularly true given that Doubs will be eligible to come off IR himself after New England's trip to Germany next week. The expectation is that he too will rejoin the active roster sooner rather than later, giving the club a full complement of wideouts again.

That said, A.J. Brown's status remains a question mark. While the team is optimistic that he will return before the end of the month, his participation in the upcoming international game against the Detroit Lions is uncertain.

After Hollins exited the game against the Bills, one player in particular took on a bigger role: second-year man Efton Chism. A fringe game-day contributor since joining the team as a rookie free agent in 2025, Chism was on the field for 72.4% of offensive snaps during Hollins' four-game absence. He caught 11 passes for 110 yards along the way (after already hauling in two passes, including the game-winning touchdown, down the stretch in Buffalo).

While Chism's production was solid, it would not be a surprise to see Hollins' return seriously eat into his playing time share. More than any other player, he might feel the trickle-down effect of the activation.

Hollins has been a solid contributor on the field so far this season, playing 63% of offensive snaps and catching 15 passes for 209 yards before going to injured reserve. However, his impact goes beyond his statistics: he is a well-respected voice in the locker room and tone-setter both on and off the field, in the pass and the run game. Getting him back especially under the current circumstances, is a positive development for New England beyond just his projected role and abilities as a pass catcher.

The Patriots entered the weekend with 52 players on their active roster, meaning that activating Hollins now put the team at a full 53 again. This also means that a follow-up will be necessary in case any other additions are planned, either ahead of the upcoming NFL trade deadline or if fellow IR-return candidate Greg Van Roten — whose 21-day window also opened this week — will be ready to rejoin the active team.

At least 21 killed after migrant boat sinks off Senegal

📅 Nov 08, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

The incident occurred near Kebemer off the coast of northwestern Senegal. Authorities on El Hierro in the Canary Islands have declared an emergency after a marked increase in migrant boat arrivals

At least 21 people were killed after a small boat carrying migrants sank off the coast of northwestern Senegal, Senegalese authorities said.

Thousands have died in recent years after embarking on boats from the Senegalese coast in order to reach Spain's Canary Islands through the perilous Atlantic route.

According to the Senegalese Interior Ministry, only one person was rescued from the capsizing, and 21 bodies were recovered.

The ministry added that the toll was "provisional" and that search operations were still ongoing.

The incident occurred near Kebemer, some 180 kilometers (around 112 miles) north of the capital, Dakar.

On Saturday, the ministry stressed the government's "determination to combat people smuggling networks, notably through strengthened cooperation with neighboring countries."

Senegalese authorities said they intercepted 142 migrants on a similar boat aimed at reaching Spanish territory on Friday. The boat included nationals of multiple West African countries.

Authorities on El Hierro Island in the Canary Islands declared an emergency on Saturday after a marked increase in migrant boat arrivals.

El Hierro officials say that over 3,300 people have arrived on around 30 boats since the start of the month.

"We are very concerned... by this sharp increase in arrivals," Alpidio Armas, head of the El Hierro regional council, told Spanish television.

"This is precisely what has led us to declare an emergency in order to ensure a framework of cooperation with higher-level institutions," he added.

Armas said that conditions at the island's hospital have become "undesirable."

El Hierro is home to about 11,400 residents.

Migrant arrivals reached a record high of 46,000 in 2024. Afterwards, arrivals have fallen sharply due to increased surveillance and cooperation with African countries.
