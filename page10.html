Week 10 underrated games: 3 games you should watch this week, from divisional matchups to first QB starts

📅 Nov 13, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

It's Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season, and the trade deadline is officially behind us. We saw the Jaguars go all in, the Vikings add a familiar face in QB Kirk Cousins and the Bears land Jeffery Simmons. Some players will make their debuts on their new teams in an exciting week for the league.

As we go down the stretch of the regular season, these games each mean more and more, and we have some big ones coming up. While some are obvious matchups to circle on the schedule, there are a few that are flying under the radar but could turn out to be a great watch.

Here is a look at three games you should keep an eye on this weekend:

Sunday, Nov. 15 at 1 p.m. ET on FOX (Stream on fubo, try for free)

Browns quarterback Deshaun Watson is set to return after missing last week due to a concussion, and the return holds more significance than normal. Watson is facing his former team, the Houston Texans, who drafted him at No. 12 overall in 2017 and where he stayed until 2021.

The Cleveland Browns (5-4) are the first team outside the playoff bubble, sitting at No. 9, and the Houston Texans (3-5) are No. 11 in the AFC. According to CBS Sports projections, if the Browns lose, their playoff chances drop to 19.0%, and a win puts their playoff chances up to 52.6%. If the Texans lose, their postseason odds drop to 16.75%, while a win would put them at 50.1%.

The AFC North is in a tight race, and this game could be big for the Browns' standings.

This matchup intrigues me because these two teams have potential, aren't quite out of the race but are long shots, and are finding their identities. The offense vs. defense matchups also make this game a fun one to watch.

The Texans are third in passing yards per game at 296.4 and face a Browns defense that has allowed the sixth-fewest passing yards per game at 201.0. The Browns offense is among the bottom in rushing yards, and the Texans defense is in the top half in terms of rushing yards allowed, so the run game could be a major factor in who wins here. Another mismatch is the Browns' struggling offensive line and the Texans' ability to generate sacks. Houston had eight sacks last week, tying a franchise record.

Houston quarterback C.J. Stroud is coming off a huge win, tying a career-high with five touchdowns, while the Browns are looking to snap a two-game losing streak.

Sunday, Nov. 15 at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS, streaming on Paramount+

In the last few hours of the trade deadline, the Raiders traded Cousins to the Vikings after nine starts and a 4-5 record. He finished his time with Vegas this season with 2,340 yards, 20 touchdowns and eight interceptions. With Cousins in Minnesota, first-overall pick Fernando Mendoza will get his first NFL start.

This game marks the beginning of what should be a lengthy Mendoza Era in Las Vegas, and it will be interesting to see how Mendoza comes out. The Seahawks defense won't make it easy, though; they are at the top of the league, allowing the fewest passing yards (155.1) and the fewest total yards allowed (244.2).

The Raiders offense has been moving the ball, at least in the air, landing fifth in passing yards per game (259.7). Their run game is what they need to improve on, as it's second worst in the league in yards per game (71.3).

The run game will be crucial this week, especially with what they're dealing with at QB. Sam Darnold is sidelined, leaving Drew Lock to run things for a second straight week, but he is dealing with an oblique/elbow injury himself. His injury could mean Seattle leans more toward the run, something that hasn't been their strongest offensive element. They have a defensive mismatch on their side, since the Raiders' defense has allowed the most rushing yards per game (151.2).

Sunday, Nov. 15 at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC (Stream on fubo, try for free)

This game is big in terms of divisional standings and the playoff picture, but these are two teams who haven't received as much attention as some of the other AFC heavy hitters.

The Steelers and Bengals are both 6-2, but Pittsburgh has the edge in the division and the top spot thanks to a 2-1 record among AFC North teams, compared to Cincinnati's 1-1 record.

This is their second meeting of the year, and with such a close race, their head-to-head record could be the difference maker when it comes to crowning a divisional champ. The Steelers won their last meeting, a close one, 30-27. I expect this game to be an exciting, close matchup as well.

Both are playoff teams right now, with the Steelers currently occupying the No. 4 seed and the Bengals at No. 6, and both teams are currently in four-game win streaks. One of those streaks will have to come to an end this week, though.

These teams are coming off very different weeks. The Steelers return from a bye, while the Bengals are coming off an international game in Madrid.

This game will be one that's looked back on as crucial for both teams this season, and with so much on the line, this game should be one you watch this week.

GUANGZHOU, China, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CNFinance Holdings Limited (NYSE: CNF) ("CNFinance" or the "Company"), a leading home equity loan service provider in China, today announced the change of auditor, effective November 13, 2026. HTL International, LLC ("HTL") has served as the Company's independent registered public accounting firm since December 2024. HTL declined to stand for re-election to serve as the independent registered public accounting firm for the audits of the Company's financial statements for the financial year ending December 31, 2026. The decision was not a result of any disagreement between the Company and HTL on any matter of accounting principles or practices, financial statement disclosure, or auditing scope or procedure. The audit committee of the Company's Board of Directors has approved the change of auditor and appointed Guangdong Prouden CPAs GP ("Prouden") as the Company's independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2026.

The appointment of Prouden was made after a careful and thorough evaluation process and has been approved by the audit committee of the Company's board of directors.

Prouden succeeds HTL, the Company's previous independent registered public accounting firm. The reports of HTL on the Company's consolidated financial statements for each of the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2025 did not contain an adverse opinion or a disclaimer of opinion and were not qualified or modified as to uncertainty, audit scope or accounting principle.

During each of the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2025 and the subsequent period through November 13, 2026, there have been no disagreements between the Company and HTL on any matter of accounting principles or practices, financial statement disclosure, or auditing scope or procedure, which disagreements, if not resolved to the satisfaction of HTL, would have caused HTL to make reference to the subject matter of the disagreements in its report on the consolidated financial statements, and there have been no "reportable events" as the term is described in Item 16F(a)(1)(v) of Form 20-F.

The Company is working closely with HTL and Prouden to ensure a seamless transition.

The audit committee would like to express its sincere gratitude to HTL for its quality of services rendered to the Company over the past years.

CNFinance, through its operating subsidiaries in China, conducts business by connecting demands and supplies through collaborating with sales partners and trust companies under the trust lending model, and sales partners, local channel partners and commercial banks under the commercial bank partnership model. Sales partners and local channel partners are responsible for recommending micro- and small-enterprise ("MSE") owners with financing needs to the Company and the Company introduces eligible borrowers to licensed financial institutions with sufficient funding sources including trust companies and commercial banks who will then conduct their own risk assessments and make credit decisions. The Company's primary target borrower segment is MSE owners who own real properties in Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities and other major cities in China. BARCELONA, Spain, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Freightos Limited (NASDAQ: CRGO) ("Freightos" or the "Company"), the leading vendor-neutral global freight pricing, booking and procurement platform, today announced that on November 10, 2026, it received a written notification letter (the "Notification Letter") from the Listing Qualifications Department of The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC ("Nasdaq") notifying the Company that it is not in compliance with the minimum bid price requirement set forth in Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2) for continued listing on the Nasdaq Capital Market.

Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2) requires listed securities to maintain a minimum bid price of $1.00 per share, and Nasdaq Listing Rule 5810(c)(3)(A) provides that a failure to meet this requirement exists if the deficiency continues for a period of 30 consecutive business days. The Notification Letter indicated that the closing bid price of the Company's ordinary shares had been below $1.00 per share for 30 consecutive business days, from September 29, 2026 through November 9, 2026.

In accordance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5810(c)(3)(A), the Company has been provided an initial period of 180 calendar days from the date of the Notification Letter, or until May 10, 2027, to regain compliance with the minimum bid price requirement. The Company will regain compliance if, at any time during this compliance period, the closing bid price of its ordinary shares is at least $1.00 per share for a minimum of 10 consecutive business days, unless Nasdaq exercises its discretion to extend this period under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5810(c)(3)(H). If the Company regains compliance, Nasdaq will provide the Company with written confirmation and the matter will be closed.

If the Company does not regain compliance by May 10, 2027, the Company may be eligible for an additional 180-calendar-day compliance period.

The Notification Letter has no immediate effect on the listing of the Company's ordinary shares. The Company's ordinary shares will continue to be listed and to trade on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the symbol "CRGO" during the compliance period. The Notification Letter does not affect the Company's business operations.

The Company intends to monitor the closing bid price of its ordinary shares and is evaluating available options to regain compliance with the minimum bid price requirement within the applicable compliance period, which may include, if necessary, effecting a reverse share split, subject to any required shareholder approval. There can be no assurance that the Company will regain compliance with the minimum bid price requirement or maintain compliance with any other Nasdaq continued listing requirement.

Nvidia is done with Jensen Huang. The fallout in Washington is just beginning

📅 2026-11-13  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Jensen Huang is out at Nvidia. Washington is just getting started with him and the company he co-founded.

Huang, the leather-jacket-clad CEO of the world's most important chipmaker, resigned from Nvidia on Thursday after revelations that he had an affair with a suspected Chinese spy. But the spy saga is gripping Washington, and lawmakers are preparing for investigations into how the now-ex-CEO's affair may have damaged U.S. artificial intelligence dominance, the national security implications and the executive's close relationship with President Donald Trump and his Cabinet.

Republicans will have their chance first during their last six weeks controlling both chambers of Congress. GOP leaders immediately summoned Huang to Capitol Hill and said they would subpoena him if he doesn't agree to show up. In January, Democrats, who are already eager to make their mark with oversight when they take over the U.S. House and Senate, will be in the driver's seat.

"In this environment where there's some really high stakes when it comes to our national security, I would expect there would be some follow-up, yeah," Senate Majority Leader John Thune, R-S.D., told reporters at the Capitol on Thursday.

Huang and the woman with whom he reportedly had a relationship, Alice Huang, an executive at the Singaporean technology company Megaspeed and an alleged Chinese spy have drawn congressional attention. And Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has drawn fire after reports this week that he stalled President Donald Trump's crackdowns on firms routing Nvidia chips to China.

Some Congress members have also called for an investigation into the role of the Bureau of Industry and Security, a subagency of the Commerce Department that manages and enforces export controls, in the Huang scandal.

The New York Times reported on Monday that Nvidia's general counsel, Tim Teter, claimed in emails to Commerce investigators probing Alice Huang's company that he'd been in touch with Jeffrey Kessler, who leads BIS. The Commerce investigation into Megaspeed, which allegedly smuggled restricted chips to China, was then transferred to the Justice Department.

By Thursday night, Senate banking committee Chair Tim Scott, R-S.C., summoned both Huang and Lutnick to testify before his committee in December. Later that same night, Lutnick had "proactively agreed to appear voluntarily before the Banking Committee to address their questions," Kristen Eichamer, a Commerce Department spokesperson, said in a statement.

Scott threatened to subpoena Huang if he does not appear. A banking panel spokesperson did not immediately respond Friday morning when asked if they'd heard from Huang.

"Recent reports raise serious concerns about our national security and the protection of sensitive U.S. technology," Scott wrote to Huang. "The American people deserve answers."

And with Democrats poised to take control of both chambers come January, the tech mogul's close ties to the White House will add fuel to an already burning fire as the new majority spools up myriad investigations into the Trump administration.

Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer, D-N.Y., in a post to X on Thursday, said "Congress must demand answers from Mr. Huang and others at Nvidia about how they may have put U.S. national security at risk." Schumer is in a contested race to become Senate majority leader in the next Congress.

The senators who will lead those investigations, and hold subpoena power in the new Congress, are gearing up for Nvidia to take center stage.

Sen. Elizabeth Warren, D-Mass., the likely incoming chair of the Senate banking panel, said in a statement Thursday that "Mr. Huang, NVIDIA executives, and NVIDIA's board of directors still owe Congress answers about the company's compliance with U.S. export controls

Warren and Sen. Chris Coons, D-Del., the top Democrat on the Senate Foreign Relations subpanel with jurisdiction over East Asia and the Pacific, sent a letter Wednesday to Lutnick, Secretary of State Marco Rubio and White House Chief of Staff Stephen Miller instructing them to preserve all documents and communications related to Nvidia's exports of "advanced AI chips to China or to entities that may divert such chips to China."

Even before Democrats take control in January, Huang is expected to appear before Congress.

House Foreign Affairs Committee Chair Brian Mast, R-Fla., is actively negotiating for Huang to testify before House committees and told reporters Thursday the Nvidia CEO has agreed to appear in the coming weeks.

Details of that testimony are still being worked out, Mast said. It would likely include his committee, as well as the House intelligence panel and the House Permanent Select Committee on China, and it would be under oath and transcribed. It's not clear at this point whether it would be public.

"We want to see him basically in a week … Sooner rather than later," Mast told reporters Thursday, adding that he believed it would happen before Thanksgiving.

If Huang doesn't willingly appear, Mast said he will subpoena him.

Mast, along with House Select China Committee Chair Rep. John Moolenaar, R-Mich., and House Intelligence Committee Chair Rick Crawford, R-Ark., send a second request Thursday to Huang and Teter requesting they appear and giving them an 8 p.m. ET Friday deadline to respond. Mast sent a second request to Kessler.

Rep. Josh Gottheimer, D-N.J., a member of the House Intelligence Committee, said Friday during an appearance on CNBC's "The Exchange" that he wants answers from Huang on the national security and export implications of the affair.

"From an intelligence committee perspective, you can imagine from an AI perspective, how important this is for our national security, for our critical infrastructure and understand when it comes to chip policy, what exactly got to China and ... did Megaspeed divert chips?" Gottheimer said.

The calls for immediate investigation into Huang and Nvidia on the day of his resignation represent a stunning fall from grace in the halls of power for the former CEO. Huang was one of the most effective voices for the AI industry in Washington — able to toggle between leveraging his relationship with Trump and defending his industry from fears about AI's risks to lawmakers.

"He is always the TED Talk guy," one senior Democratic staff member who was granted anonymity to describe private meetings they had attended with Huang, told CNBC. "He's really good and articulate, and really good at explaining why AI is important and why it's so important for the United States to be leading here."

The staffer said Huang was a "happy warrior" for AI, and stayed longer than scheduled or anticipated for questions from lawmakers. But in the meetings with lawmakers the aide attended, they said Huang did not aggressively push against export controls or get too "deep in the weeds" to really "lobby one way or the other" with the lawmakers.

Huang has repeatedly resisted export controls publicly, a move that has rubbed some lawmakers the wrong way.

Some lawmakers even lamented the loss of Huang, despite the inevitable buzzsaw he now faces on Capitol Hill.

"It's just sad all around," Sen. John Fetterman, D-Pa., said of Huang's resignation.

Grand Theft Auto 6 ditched traditional discs for its "physical" launch, with Rockstar's game being sold as a digital code-in-box at retailers beginning this week. However, it appears a packaging choice may have accidentally left the game vulnerable to thieves--assuming they have a flashlight.

As shared on the forum ResetEra, users who received a physical version of Grand Theft Auto 6, at least in PEGI regions, report that the game's download code is printed directly on the inside of the cover rather than on a card or separate insert. When a flashlight is focused on the cover from the outside of the closed game case, the download code is visible in reverse.

In other words, there's a serious risk people could go into a store, do this trick, redeem the code, and leave the poor soul who actually buys the game off the shelf without GTA 6.

At this time, the trick does not seem to work on North American copies of GTA 6, as the code is intentionally obscured. Why Rockstar would not use this strategy on all regions is unclear. We've reached out to Rockstar for an explanation and will update this post if and when we receive a response.

If you happen to get your GTA 6 code stolen after paying $80, you can at least sleep soundly knowing that the thief can't play the game yet. Code-in-box copies, as well as digital preorders, all unlock for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19.

GTA 6 Owner Take-Two Is Not Interested In Selling Itself After EA Goes Private For $55 Billion

📅 Nov 13, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Recently, the gaming giant Electronic Arts was taken private in a $55 billion sale to an investor consortium led by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund. Could GTA 6 owner Take-Two be the next big company that gets acquired? Don't bet on it, CEO Strauss Zelnick says.

Speaking to David Rubenstein, Zelnick said Take-Two's business results have proven that it "can do very well as an independent company."

He said Take-Two's business is already "at scale" and healthy enough financially with a "terrific balance sheet," as he put it.

"We are not in a place where we need to be part of a bigger organization to be competitive. And in the entertainment business, scale matters," he said.

Zelnick said companies that do less than a billion in annual revenue might have issues with developing, marketing, and distributing games at scale. Not only that, but companies that don't generate a lot of money might not be able to survive the realities of the creative business, in which sometimes your product flops.

"But once you're over a billion dollars in annual revenue, and we're way beyond that point. Our guidance is $8 billion to $8.2 billion this fiscal year, additional scale doesn't seem to help you succeed."

In early 2008, Electronic Arts privately pursued a buyout of Take-Two, but after the GTA and BioShock company rejected it, EA went public in an attempt to get a deal done. Later that year, EA abandoned its plans to try to buy Take-Two after the GTA company said it was worth more and EA refused to increase its bid.

In 2019, it was rumored that Sony was attempting to buy Take-Two, but that was ultimately deemed to be unconfirmed market chatter and not a formal bid.

Take-Two has a market cap of more than $42 billion, and that is expected to grow in the year ahead, so it would seemingly take a lot of money to convince Take-Two to sell. Going forward, Zelnick has teased that new GTA games could release even faster, and that could be a positive for the company's financials.

‘Who’s In The Band?’ Music Competition Series From Richard Bacon, Simon Cowell & Savan Kotecha Lands Greenlight At ABC

📅 Nov 13, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

EXCLUSIVE: Music competition series Who’s In The Band? has finally scored a series order at ABC.

The Disney-owned network has officially greenlit the show, which comes from Richard Bacon, Simon Cowell and Savan Kotecha.

It marks the first new major music format for a U.S. broadcast network in some time and comes at the same time that Fox is developing a new version of Cowell’s The X Factor.

Deadline revealed that the project was in the works last year with a proof-of-concept pilot shot in London. For that taping, KPop Demon Hunters star Rei Ami as host with a panel of judges that included Joe Jonas, Mel B, and Kotecha.

Deadline understands that the deals for hosts and judges isn’t finalized but Ami is understood to remain as host. The judging panel, however, will be shaken up. Cowell was never going to be in the series, as he has a deal at NBCUniversal that precludes him from appearing on an ABC show, and Jonas is now a judge on the upcoming celebrity version of The Voice, but Mel B could still be part of the show going forward. Watch on Deadline

Who’s In The Band? is described as a “fast-paced competition built for the era of snap decisions.”

Five contestants sing the same song at once, each inside a brightly colored soundproof tube, while judges at their own mixing desks isolate vocals and eliminate hopefuls in real time. The series gives audiences an inside look at how top music industry professionals put together pop groups today, culminating in a live finale where one girl band and one boy band face a live vote.

The winners will record an album executive produced by Kotecha, who has written songs for the likes of Ariana Grande, Katy Perry, Lizzo, Britney Spears, Ed Sheeran and One Direction.

Filming will begin in January and the show will launch on ABC and Hulu in the summer of 2027.

It comes from Bacon’s Yes Yes Media, which is backed by Sister Group. Bacon devised the project and will produce alongside Syco Entertainment, Kotecha Publicis Media Content Innovation in association with Walt Disney Television Alternative.

Bacon is a well-known broadcaster in Britain, hosting shows such as Blue Peter and The Big Breakfast before moving into production. He was behind ABC’s Craig Ferguson-hosted game show The Hustler, which was produced by The Traitors producer Studio Lambert, as well as British series including This Is My House and I Literally Just Told You.

He will exec produce alongside Cowell, Kotecha and Elisabeth Murdoch. Matt Banks and Ashley Edens will serve as showrunners and will also exec produce.
